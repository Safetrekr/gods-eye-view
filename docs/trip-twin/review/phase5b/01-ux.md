# Phase 5b review 01: UX (review only, no code edits)

Scope: Jesse's five 5b asks. (1) Units change every distance. (2) Framing lands in the area the panels leave free. (3) 3D labels declutter. (4) Go to floor. (5) An incident asks before the site switches.
The test: does each one work for an operator under time pressure, and did anything regress?
Screens: `shots/1600-NN-*.png`, `shots/1280-NN-*.png`, `shots/1600-text.json`.
Code read: `page.js` (safe area 1003–1025, go-to-floor 325–359, switchSite 968–980, confirmToast 2076–2098, units 3138–3227, Layers KEY 2596+, keyboard 3345+), `twinDeclutter.js`, `twinPeople.js` (ruler and initials 180–300, floor tag 398–414), `tripTwin.js` (flySafe 635–690, chips 870–880), `twinRoutes.js` (defaultMode 187–195), `twinData.js` (formatDistance, formatRadius, formatShort), `twinUnits.js`, `twin.html`.

Verdict: items 4 and 5 work as written, with gaps around them. Items 1 and 3 are mostly in place, but with leaks that matter. Item 2 is broken. Because of one DOM check it frames worse than before 5b. Fix P0-1 first: it is a one-line change, and it also cures the clipped labels in P2-9.

---

## P0

### P0-1 · Framing ignores the left rail, so targets land about 180 px left, at or under the rail
**Shots.** 1600-02 (path midpoint at x≈610; free-area centre is 788), 1600-06 (hotel's left half under the rail), 1280-02 (Start label cut to "tart · Emi Sato"), 1280-06 ("Floor 9" and "point (secondary)" at the rail edge).
**Problem.** `visibleBox()` (page.js:1004) rejects any element whose `offsetParent` is null. `#tt-rail` and `#tt-advance` are `position: fixed`, and for fixed elements `offsetParent` is always null. So `left` is always 0. The card sits inside the fixed `#tt-rcol`, so it still counts, and `right` is about 355 px. flySafe therefore moves the target left by half the right panel: about 177 px at 1600 and about 158 px at 1280. That matches every framed shot.
Before 5b, framing used the canvas centre, which at 1600 was only about 12 px off the free centre. So this is a regression: Frame path, select-and-frame and floor focus now put the thing the operator asked for next to, or under, the rail. Emi's end of the hospital path is the end that matters, and it lands bottom-left. The `fit` widening also uses the wrong free width, so at 1280 the path is framed too tight.
**Fix.**
- page.js:1006: replace `!e.offsetParent` with `!e.getClientRects().length` (or `!e.checkVisibility()`).
- Pad the insets so end labels fit too: `left + 24`, `right + 24`, `bottom + 16`.
- Add a test: with the rail and card open, `safeArea().left === rail.getBoundingClientRect().right`. After `moveEnd`, the projected sphere centre is within ±24 px of the free-area centre at 1280 and 1600.

### P0-2 · Declutter moves floor-indexed labels up and down, so initials sit beside the wrong floor number during a floor incident
**Shots.** 1600-08 and 1600-09: "A.K. ↓" (likely floor 9) sits beside ruler 13–14, and N.P., O.H., P.N. and S.D. cascade down past 1. 1280-09: "M.R." (likely 10) sits beside **15**. 1600-06 and 1280-06: the "Floor 9" slab tag sits level with ruler 14–15.
**Problem.**
- The ruler is fixed (good). But the person initials (`twin-bracket-label:*`) and the slab tag ("Floor N") take part in the declutter like any other label. They slide in 16 px steps, up to ±64 px.
- At 1280 one floor is about 10 px, so one slide can move a label 4–6 floors.
- There is no leader line. When the stacking fails, the label is still placed at −64 px, overlapping.
- In a "smoke, floor 9" incident, the operator reads the ruler number next to a label as that person's floor. The bracket line is correct, but it is a thin dashed stroke, and the label is what the eye lands on.
**Fix.**
- Floor-indexed labels never move vertically. Mark them `declutterAxis: 'x'`: they slide right only (away from the ruler), in steps of one lane width (3.2 m projected, at least 14 px).
- When two or more initials share a floor band and would collide, merge them into one plate at that floor: "M.R. · A.K. ↓". The plate uses the brighter of the two lane colours. The full list is in the rail.
- Make the "Floor N" slab tag `declutterFixed: true` (it marks a floor, like the ruler).
- If any label is shifted at all, draw a 1 px leader in `--tt-text-dim` from the label to its anchor (its bracket top).
- Test: with the 1280 incident fixture, every initials plate's baseline is within half a floor (in px) of its bracket top.

## P1

### P1-3 · Feet · miles still shows metric in the path card, the Layers key and the heat chip
**Shots.** 1600-01 and 1600-02 in feet: the card says "Showing the drive: **1.7 km** in a straight line" right above "1.2 mi · 3–5 min". 1600-03 in feet: Layers › Paths says "Within **1 km** of a heat detection".
**Problem.** Three strings are formatted once and never again:
- **The path card reason.** `routeModel.reason` is built in `runRoute` (page.js:1376), and `rebuildRouteText` does not rebuild it.
- **The Layers key.** It is a `const KEY` evaluated at page load (page.js:2596), so `formatRadius(1000)` stays in the startup unit.
- **The heat-detection edge chip.** It is hard-coded as `` `${(d/1000).toFixed(1)} km` `` (tripTwin.js:877). In feet mode a hazard chip would read "3.2 km SW" next to "Hospital 1.0 mi N".

Jesse's ask was "every distance on screen", and the Display helper says so. A mixed-unit card is worse than a metric-only one, because the operator does unit conversion in their head mid-decision.
**Fix.**
- Store `straightM` on the route model and format it at render time (`reason: {kind:'drive', straightM}`).
- Make `KEY` a function that `renderLayers()` calls.
- Use `formatDistance()` in the heat chip.
- Keep instrument specs as they are, but label them as specs: "Heat detection (375 m sensor pixel)" and "8 km grid".
- Add a DOM test: with the unit set to ft and every surface open (card, path, incident, Layers, chips, Advance brief), visible text matches no `/\b\d[\d.,]*\s?(km|m)\b/` outside a short allow-list of sensor specs.

### P1-4 · Feet · miles rounds away the comparison the operator is making
**Shots.** 1600-01: "Rally point (primary) 0.2 mi" and "Rally point (secondary) 0.2 mi". 1600-08 (metres): the incident panel says "rally point (primary) · 640 m · walk", while the traveler card's 0.2 mi is a straight line from Emi.
**Problem.**
- `formatDistance` in ft switches to tenths of a mile at 1,000 ft. So 1,000–1,300 ft all read "0.2 mi", and two rally points can't be told apart.
- Metres give "320 m" and "350 m", so the same screen is more useful in metric.
- Distances on different bases sit side by side with no basis word: a straight line from a person in the card, a walk from the hotel in the incident panel.
**Fix.**
- **ft.** Below 0.25 mi, use feet rounded to 50 with a thousands separator ("1,050 ft"). At 0.25 mi and above, use tenths ("0.4 mi").
- **yd.** Below 0.25 mi, use yards rounded to 10 ("350 yd").
- **Basis word.** Every distance in a destination option or the incident line carries its basis: "0.2 mi straight line" or "640 m walk". Use `--tt-text-dim` for the basis.
- **Default unit.** Default to Feet · miles when the trip's country is US (it is a school-group product for US chaperones). Keep the stored preference over the default.
- **Ground relief readout.** "Ground in this view: −41 ft to +34 ft" now sits in Display, apart from the contours it explains. Move it under Layers › Site › Elevation, as the row's note.

### P1-5 · The site-switch question: Esc does the wrong thing, and focus is lost afterwards
**Shots.** 1600-09 and 1280-09.
**Problem.** The copy and the default (Stay, primary, focused) are right. But:
- **Esc does not mean Stay.** The toast has no key handler. Esc falls through to the global chain (page.js:3373): it removes the path, closes the place card, clears floor focus or deselects the hotel. Meanwhile the question stays up. Under pressure, Esc is the reflex for "no".
- **Focus is lost.** Stay, Switch and the 15 s timeout all hide the toast without returning focus. Keyboard focus drops to `<body>` on a hidden button.
- **It is not announced as a question.** The container is `role="status"` and focus jumps to "Stay". A screen reader may hear "Stay, button" without the question, depending on the live-region timing.
**Fix.**
- Make the toast `role="alertdialog"` (non-modal), with `aria-labelledby` pointing to the question span and `aria-describedby` on both buttons.
- Add a keydown handler on the toast: Esc means Stay, followed by `stopPropagation()`. Tab and Shift+Tab cycle Stay ↔ Switch.
- On any close (Stay, Switch or timeout), return focus to the control that asked: the Sites button, or the beacon's list entry.
- The timeout stays equal to Stay. Show it in the button ("Stay · closes in 15 s") so the question doesn't vanish without explanation.

### P1-6 · Go to floor is missing from the panel the operator is in during an incident
**Shot.** 1600-08: the incident panel's FLOORS list shows 11 to 4 in a scroll box, with no Go field. The hotel card says "Floors and people are in the incident panel above".
**Problem.** The Go field is attached only to the hotel card's cutaway (page.js:359). The docked incident cutaway (`docked`) has none. The time-pressured case is exactly when it's needed: a caller says "my kid is on 23", and the operator has to scroll a compact 37-row list.
**Fix.**
- Build the Go form as part of `createCutaway()`, so both copies get it (one component, one behaviour).
- In the incident panel, place it on the FLOORS header row, to the right of "Select a floor to see who may be on it".
- Add a key: `G` focuses the Go field of whichever cutaway is visible. List it in the shortcuts sheet.

## P2

### P2-7 · The Go-to-floor error reads like help text, and the stack looks as if it jumped to 37
**Shots.** 1600-05 and 1280-05.
**Problem.**
- The out-of-range message is 11 px `--tt-text-dim` (`.tt-note`), the same as passive hints.
- The input keeps "52" but doesn't select it, so a retype appends.
- There is no `aria-invalid`.
- In the same shot, floor 37 has a dashed outline and the plate under the stack says "Floor 37 · 0 most likely". Next to "52", that reads as if the request was clamped to the top floor.

**Fix.**

On error:
- Set the message colour to `--tt-attention` and the size to 12px/16px.
- Set `aria-invalid="true"` on the input and `aria-describedby` pointing to the message.
- Call `goIn.select()`.
- Don't move or outline any floor, and clear the plate.

Copy:
- Keep the current sentence, then add the numbering facts: "Floor 52 is not in this building (1–37, lobby = 1) — check the numbering".

Input:
- Prevent wheel changes on the number input: blur it on `wheel`.

### P2-8 · The site-switch bar sits far from what triggered it and covers the ruler
**Shots.** 1600-09 and 1280-09.
**Problem.**
- The operator clicked the Sites list (top bar) or a beacon, and the question appears about 750 px away at the bottom centre.
- At 1280 it covers the bottom of the floor ruler and the initials (P.N., S.D.) during the incident it is warning about, and at 1600 it covers the compass.
- "Switch to Long Center" wraps onto two lines at both widths.
**Fix.**
- Anchor the bar under the top bar, below the chips row (`top: calc(var(--bar) + 44px)`), centred in the free area.
- Shorten the action to "Switch site". The question already names the site.
- Keep `white-space: nowrap` and let the question text wrap instead.

### P2-9 · Declutter only avoids other labels: it ignores panels and markers, and moved labels lose their anchor
**Shots.**
- 1600-07: "oup shelter · chaperones only" is cut by the rail. "Fire station" floats with no visible marker or leader.
- 1600-06 and 1600-08: the "Rally point (secondary) · Draft" label covers its own R2 marker.
- 1600-02: the path's Start label overprints the HL and JO person markers.
**Problem.** Only `label` entities are boxes. Billboards, meaning people and places, aren't obstacles, and neither are the side panels. When all 8 tries fail, the label is forced to −64 px and overlaps anyway.
**Fix.**
- Add the safe-area panel rects (the same function as P0-1) and billboard boxes as fixed obstacles.
- Clamp label x into the free area.
- If no slot is free, hide the lower-priority label rather than overprint. Priority order: incident/hazard 1, people 2, approved place 3, path ends 4, draft place 5, itinerary 6.
- Draw a 1 px `--tt-text-dim` leader for any |dy| ≥ 16 px.
- Under Reduce motion, apply the shifts only on `moveEnd`, so labels don't jump in 16 px steps during an orbit.

### P2-10 · Live → Advance during an incident skips the question and silently drops the path and floor focus
**Code.** `setAppMode` (page.js:2023) calls `clearRoute()` and `clearFloorFocus()` with no guard. The question only runs when `appMode === 'live'`, so a site picked inside Advance (page.js:1830) never asks.
**Problem.** Jesse listed Advance as a trigger. A stray click on the Advance tab mid-incident discards the hospital path and floor 9 focus with no word.
**Fix.** During a live incident, the Advance tab asks the same one-line question: "Incident at Fairmont Austin — open Advance anyway? The incident stays in the top bar." [Stay] [Open Advance]. Also keep the drawn path and floor focus in memory, so Live restores them when the operator returns.

## Keep (do not change)

- **Units.**
  - The Display › Units control is a single select with the plain helper "Every distance on screen: cards, paths, chips, grid and contours."
  - Grid spacing is remembered per unit, with round values per unit (50/100/250/500 ft).
  - The footer grid line follows the unit.
- **Layers › Site.** Scale grid → Spacing, and Elevation → Interval, sit under their own toggles. The pointer "Units in Display (D)" stays. This is the right IA move.
- **flySafe.** The camera right/up vectors are correct, the fit cap is 1.8, framing only follows operator actions, and Reduce motion is respected. Once P0-1 fixes the insets, the design holds.
- **The fixed floor ruler** as the declutter obstacle, and the Fire station / Rally point (secondary) separation (1600-07, 1600-08). The overprint Jesse called out is gone.
- **Go to floor.**
  - The out-of-range sentence names the range and says what to check.
  - A valid entry opens the floor card with a "‹ Fairmont Austin" back crumb and clears the field (1600-06).
- **Site-switch question.**
  - It names both sites and says the incident will persist. It is one line, not a modal, and Stay is primary and focused with a visible ring.
  - After switching, the incident truly stays: the "INCIDENT · OTHER SITE" pill, plus a hazard chip ending "— show" that returns in one click without asking again.
- **Copy and brand.** No banned words. Green appears only on approved places. Blue is only on paths (Start/End labels in `--tt-path-text`). Every path is labelled "Suggested · unverified".
