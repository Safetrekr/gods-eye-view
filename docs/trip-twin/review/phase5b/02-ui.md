Scope: units, framing, label declutter, go-to-floor, incident site-switch bar.
Evidence: `shots/1600-NN`, `shots/1280-NN`, `1600-text.json` / `1280-text.json`, `tripTwin.css` (phase 5 block + 5b block at the end), `twinDeclutter.js`, `page.js`.
Coordinates are approximate screen pixels from the screenshots.

Free area used below means the scene region between the panels:
x from `var(--rail) + 2*var(--g)` to `100vw - var(--rcol) - 2*var(--g)` (1600: about 344–1232, 1280: about 304–952), y from the chip row bottom (about 80) to the dock top (1600: about 825, 1280: about 645).

## P0

### 1. Units = feet still prints km and m, and one card mixes km with mi
- **Shots:** 1600-01, 1600-02, 1280-01, 1280-02 (path card); 1600-03 / 1280-03 (Layers legend, from text.json).
- **Problem:** With Distances set to "Feet · miles", these strings stay metric:
  - Path card: `Showing the drive: 1.7 km in a straight line.` This sits directly above `1.2 mi · 3–5 min`. Read quickly, the drive looks shorter than the straight line (1.2 < 1.7). That misleads on the medical-escort card.
  - Layers › Hazards: `Heat detection (375 m pixel)`.
  - Layers › Paths: `Within 1 km of a heat detection`.

  The Display popover says "Every distance on screen", so these strings break that promise.
- **Spec:**
  - Send every distance literal through the units formatter in `twinUnits.js`, including legend copy and the "Showing the drive" sentence. Feet mode should read `1.1 mi in a straight line`, `Heat detection (1,230 ft pixel)` and `Within 0.6 mi of a heat detection`.
  - Add a test to `twinPhase5b.test.mjs`. With units=ft, render the path card, the Layers popover and the chips. Fail if the text matches `/\d\s?(km|m)\b/`. With units=m, check for `/\d\s?(mi|ft|yd)\b/` the same way.

## P1

### 2. The site-switch bar covers the compass, and its secondary button wraps
- **Shots:** 1600-09, 1280-09.
- **Problem:**
  - `.tt-toast` takes the full free width (`max-width: calc(100vw - rail - rcol - 6g)`) and sits at `z-index: 30` over `.tt-compass` (`z-index: 9`, 84 px, bottom-right of the free area).
  - At 1280 the bar spans about x 330–952 and y 577–628. It hides about 80% of the compass and cuts off the "Viewing from S · looking N" caption. At 1600 it covers the lower half of the compass.
  - "Switch to Long Center" wraps onto two lines at both widths because the secondary `.tt-btn` does not get `nowrap`. Stay is 36 px tall and the wrapped secondary is taller, so the two buttons do not align.
  - The message runs to two lines even at 1600, although the spec calls for one line.
  - The bar is plain glass, so nothing marks it as an attention question.
- **Spec (CSS, append to the 5b block):**
  ```css
  .tt-toast {
    left: calc(var(--rail) + 2 * var(--g));
    right: calc(var(--rcol) + 3 * var(--g) + 84px); /* clear the compass */
    transform: none;
    margin-inline: auto;
    width: fit-content;
    max-width: none;
    border-left: 3px solid var(--tt-attention);   /* amber = attention, not red */
  }
  .tt-toast > span { flex: 1 1 auto; min-width: 0; }
  .tt-toast .tt-btn { flex: none; height: 36px; white-space: nowrap; }
  ```
  - Copy (`page.js` line 963): change to `Incident open at Fairmont Austin. Switch to Long Center?`, which is 56 characters. Move "The incident stays in the top bar." to the secondary button as `aria-description`, or drop it. The result is one line at 1600 and at most two at 1280. The buttons must never wrap.
  - Keep: Stay is solid primary and gets focus on open; the secondary names the destination.

### 3. Labels slide under the side panels and over person markers
- **Shots:**
  - 1280-01: `Start · Emi Sato (position at 21:31 · 59s o…` is cut off by the right panel at x 964.
  - 1280-02: `tart · Emi Sato (po[MR]ition…` is cut off by the left panel at x 291, and the marker prints over the text.
  - 1600-02: the JO marker prints over the start label (`(p[JO]tion`).
  - 1280-06: `point (secondary) · Draft · not approved` is cut off by the left panel, and the label covers its own R2 marker.
  - 1600-07 and 1280-07: `oup shelter · chaperones only` / `lter · chaperones only` are cut off by the left panel.
- **Problem:**
  - `declutter()` only tests labels against labels, and only moves them vertically. It does not know the panel edges or the billboard markers.
  - When all 8 tries fail, `dy` falls back to −64 px and the label still overlaps whatever it is on top of.
- **Spec (`twinDeclutter.js`):**
  1. Pass `bounds` = the free area rect, read each run from `--rail`, `--rcol`, `--g`, `--bar` and the dock's `getBoundingClientRect()`.
     - If a label box crosses the left or right bound, shift it horizontally (`pixelOffset.x`) back inside by up to its own width.
     - If the anchor point itself is under a panel, set `show = false`.
  2. Add person and safety billboards as fixed obstacles. Use about 36×40 px per marker, which includes the "T" badge.
     - Non-fixed labels slide off them.
     - Fixed labels (ruler) that intersect a marker hide (`show = false`) instead of moving.
  3. If no slot is found, hide the lower-priority label instead of parking it at −64 px. Path Start/End labels and the incident label get the highest priority and are never hidden.

### 4. "Frame path" leaves the path left of center in the free area
- **Shots:** 1600-02, 1280-02.
- **Problem:**
  - 1600: the path plus its end label spans about x 500–890, so its center is about x 615 against a free-area center of about 788. That is about 170 px left.
  - 1280: the path spans about x 370–566 (center about 468) against a free-area center of about 628. That is about 160 px left, and the start label runs under the left panel (see #3).
  - The right half of the free area is empty in both shots. Vertical placement is fine.
- **Spec:**
  - After `flyTo` completes, project the path polyline vertices and both endpoint label boxes to screen space and take their bounding box.
  - Pan the camera (`camera.moveLeft`/`moveRight` in metres, from the pixel-to-metre ratio at the path midpoint) so the bounding-box center lands on the free-area center, with ±8 px tolerance.
  - Keep a 48 px inset on every side of the free area. If the box is still too large, zoom out one step and repeat, at most twice.
  - Acceptance: in 1600-02 and 1280-02 the bounding-box center is within 24 px of the free-area center, and no label is cut off.

### 5. Floor ruler: the reported floor's tag runs into 10, looks like every other tag, and "25" sits under markers
- **Shots:** 1600-08, 1600-09, 1280-08, 1280-09 (and the same ruler in 06).
- **Problem:**
  - The `10` and `9` tags are about 11 px apart vertically. With 13–14 px tall boxes they touch or overlap (1600: y 607 and 618; 1280: y 484 and 495).
  - Floor 9 is the reported incident floor, but its tag uses the same neutral dark box as 5, 10 and 15. The red slab is the only cue on the building.
  - `25` (1600: x 950, y 437; 1280: x 757, y 350) is half covered by the TC/OH marker cluster.
- **Spec:**
  - When a reported or focused floor tag lies within ±1 floor of a 5-step tag, drop the 5-step tag. Here that means drop `10` and keep `9`.
  - Style the reported floor's tag as an incident tag: `background: rgba(238,119,137,0.18); border: 1px solid var(--tt-hazard); color: var(--tt-hazard-text); font-weight: 600`. Red is valid here because this is the incident floor.
  - In non-incident floor focus (06), the focused tag uses `border: 1px solid var(--tt-text)` with white text.
  - Ruler tags that intersect a person marker hide (see #3.2). The ruler has redundant 5-step tags, so losing one costs little.

## P2

### 6. Go to floor: an out-of-range entry looks like a neutral note, not an error
- **Shots:** 1600-05, 1280-05.
- **Problem:**
  - "Floor 52 is not in this building (1–37) — check the numbering" uses `.tt-note` in dim grey. The input does not change and has no `aria-invalid`, so nothing on the input itself marks it wrong.
  - In the same frame the stack shows a dashed frame on 37 and the plate reads "Floor 37 · 0 most likely". That reads as if the entry was clamped to 37. It may only be a hover artifact; confirm before fixing.
- **Spec:**
  - On an invalid entry:
    - Set `goIn.setAttribute('aria-invalid','true')`.
    - Set `goMsg.id = 'tt-gofloor-msg'`, then call `goIn.setAttribute('aria-describedby', goMsg.id)`.
    - Add `goMsg.classList.add('warn')`.
  - On the next `input` event, clear all three.
  - CSS:
    ```css
    .tt-gofloor input[aria-invalid='true'] { border-color: var(--tt-attention); }
    .tt-gofloor .tt-note.warn { color: var(--tt-attention); }
    ```
  - Shorter copy: `No floor 52 here — floors 1–37`.
  - An invalid entry must not change `twin.floorFocus` or the stack's focus/plate. If submitting is what triggers the 37 highlight, suppress it.

### 7. Hotel card floor stack: axis numbers overprint at the compressed top and bottom
- **Shots:** 1600-05, 1280-05.
- **Problem:** Quiet floors shrink to 2 px rows. That leaves `37` and `35` about 11 px apart, so the glyphs touch. `15` and `13` are 13 px apart, and 13 is a flagged floor directly below 15.
- **Spec (`twinCutaway.js` render):**
  - Always label the top floor, flagged floors and the lobby.
  - Label a quiet 5-step floor only if its row top is at least 12 px from the previous labelled row top, in both directions.
  - With that rule, `35` and `15` drop in 05.

### 10. Right column: content cut off mid-line at the top and bottom
- **Shots:** 1600-01, 1280-01, 1280-02 (path card); 1600-08, 1600-09 (incident card).

## Keep

- **Display › Units:** the select with the plain "Every distance on screen…" sentence, and the live "Ground in this view: −41 ft to +34 ft vs hotel street level" readout. The footer "Grid: 250 ft · bold lines 1250 ft" follows the units correctly, and chips, rally distances and the ±39 ft accuracy all switch.
- **Layers › Site option rows:** indented under their parent toggles, with the interval row hidden while Elevation is off. Moving these out of Display was the right call.
- **Declutter wins:** the Fire station / Rally point (secondary) labels no longer overprint (06, 07, 08). The 1–37 ruler no longer collides with the initials column, apart from #5. The initials leader dots form a readable column.
- **Go to floor:** placed between "FLOORS ▾" and the stack, with a numeric field and a secondary sm "Go". There is one `role=status` message line, the value stays so it can be corrected, and floor 9 opens the floor card with the "Floor 9" scene tag.
- **Site-switch bar:**
  - Non-modal, and Stay is the solid primary with focus on open, so Enter is the safe choice.
  - The secondary names the destination.
  - The incident stays in the top bar, and the 15 s auto-dismiss is the same as choosing Stay.
- **Colour roles:**
  - Green appears only on approved places (chips, Fire station, Group shelter, Rally point (primary)).
  - Blue appears only on paths and Start/End labels.
  - Red appears only on the incident (headline, reported floor slab, "Reported floor 9" dock button, INCIDENT pill).
  - Amber marks attention bars and the not-current/not-located counts.
  - One solid primary per region: Copy path summary, Copy for responders, Stay.
- **1280 layout:** the chips, dock, both rails and the Layers/Display popovers fit without overflow. The only clipping found at 1280 is in #2, #3 and #10.
