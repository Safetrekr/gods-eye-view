# 04 IA review, phase 2: cutaway, floor card, weather, Site layers

Owner: information architect. Review only, no code changed. Scope: taxonomy and labels, wayfinding, where things live, consistency with phase-1 vocabulary, phase-3 readiness.
Inputs: `review/phase2/shots/` (1600-01…14, 1280-09) and `1600-text.json`; `twin.html`; `tripTwin/page.js` (GROUPS, weatherGroup, keydown, crumbs, focusFloor/clearFloorFocus); `twinHud.js` (renderRail, incident, createCard building/floor/person); `twinCutaway.js`; `twinData.js` (floorOccupancy, floorRoster); `twinWeather.js`; `tripTwin.js` (select/selectPerson/floorFocus); 06, 07, ROUND-2, the approved phase-2 plan, phase-1 `review/04-ia-review.md` and `00-FIX-LIST.md`.
I have not reopened recorded decisions: the cutaway colours, weather as a sub-group of Hazards, grid and elevation as Site layers, the hotel auto-selected on arrival, and free/open weather sources all stand.

## Verdict

Phase 2 is structurally sound. Each new thing has one sensible home. The cutaway sits in the hotel card and docks into the incident panel, leaving a pointer behind. Weather is a collapsed Hazards › Weather sub-group with a source note on each row. Grid and Elevation are Site layers, and their settings stay in Display. `#tt-site` is reserved.

Weather needs no new top-level group. It has four homes and each has one job: the chip shows conditions now, Official shows the authority's warnings, the exception lines show our computed rules, and Layers shows the spatial layers. One of those homes is broken (P0-2).

There are two P0s:
1. **The floor card contradicts itself.** "Likely", "detected" and "assigned here" mean different things in the bar, the hover plate, the floor card and the copied responder text.
2. **Official weather warnings are not in the Official slot.** They sit as a separate line in the rail.

The rest is wayfinding: opening a person from a floor card is a dead end, `[ ]` is overloaded, and the breadcrumb skips the site. There are also phase-3 seams that are cheap to cut now.

## Findings

### P0-1: "Likely / detected / assigned here" contradict each other on the floor card, the cutaway and the responder copy

- **Evidence.**
  - Shot 1600-10, Floor 11 card: `LIKELY HERE NOW (±1) · 0`, then "No current estimate puts anyone on this floor", then `ASSIGNED HERE · 1 · Maya Ruiz · estimate range includes this floor`.
  - Incident cutaway bar for floor 11 (1600-08): `8 assigned · none detected`.
  - Hover plate for the same floor (`floorPlate`): "0 likely here · 1 more within estimate range · 8 assigned".
  - `twinData.js floorRoster`: "Likely here now" lists only people whose *likeliest* floor is this one (`f.likely`). People whose range touches the floor but who are not assigned to it (`touches − likely − assignedHere`) appear in **no section**. `floorRosterText` (Copy for hotel / responders) inherits all of this.
- **Problem.**
  - Floor 11 is two floors above the reported smoke. On screen it reads "no one, none detected" while an estimate range includes it.
  - "(±1)" promises a band, but the bands vary in width ("Rough: floors 5–8").
  - The section title "Assigned here" is a subset of the other section, "Assigned here, not detected here".
  - The row tag "assigned here" in the Likely section repeats the section title.
- **Fix.** Use one set of definitions in the bar, plate, floor card, copy text and 06. "Detected here" means *a current estimate range includes this floor*. Floor card sections, in this order:
  1. `Most likely here · {n}`: the likeliest floor is N. Empty state: `No one's most likely floor is {N}`. Row note: `assigned to this floor`, or `assigned floor {k}`, or nothing.
  2. `Possibly here (estimate range includes floor {N}) · {n}`: every current range that touches N and is not in section 1, whether or not the person is assigned here. Row note: the estimate plus the assignment, e.g. `Likely floor 10 (9–11) · assigned to this floor`.
  3. `Assigned here, not detected here · {n}`: label and reasons unchanged.

  Delete the bare `Assigned here` section; its people move to section 2. Drop "(±1)" from every title.

  **Cutaway bar:** use the amber text `{n} assigned · none detected` only when no current range touches the floor. Otherwise show `{k} possibly here · {n} assigned`, using the existing `range` styling. Jesse's colour meanings do not change.

  Update `twinFloors.test.mjs` and the copy text to match.

### P0-2: Official weather warnings live outside the Official slot

- **Evidence.**
  - Rail head (every shot): "No official instruction on file · Headcount: not reported", then a separate line "¦ Official weather warning: Wind Advisory (NWS) until Fri 19:00" (`twinHud.js renderRail` l.235).
  - The incident panel's official box (l.462) shows only "No official instruction on file · Headcount: not reported". The warning is absent there.
  - Recorded in phase 1 (§2.6): official weather alerts go in the Official slot using HAZ-12.
- **Problem.** There are two "official" items in two homes. The adjacent lines read as a contradiction ("No official…" / "Official…"). Where the operator actually works during an incident, the warning is missing. Wind Advisory is harmless, but the same code path will carry "Tornado Warning" or "Flash Flood Warning". Those must outrank our computed top line (06 F4, 01).
- **Fix.** One `OFFICIAL` block: first in the incident panel during an incident, and directly under the top line in the rail when calm. Lines:
  - `No official instruction about this incident on file. Check local authorities.` (HAZ-11 plus scope words)
  - `Official · NWS: Wind Advisory · until Fri 19:00 (CDT) · area weather warning` (HAZ-12 order; every official line starts `Official ·`)
  - When the feed is down: `Official weather warnings unavailable · NWS` (keep this).

  `Headcount: not reported` is ours, not official, so it moves to its own line outside the block. During an incident, drop the rail copy (one home). Warnings with NWS severity Severe/Extreme, or MeteoAlarm orange/red, also add a clause to the top line: `… · Official: Tornado Warning (NWS)`.

### P1-3: Weather row names promise data the sources do not provide

- **Evidence.**
  - `weatherGroup` labels.
  - Source labels in `twinWeather.js`: "Lightning density · NOAA nowCOAST (8 km grid)", "Lightning flash area · EUMETSAT MTG-LI (2 km)", "Rain rate · satellite estimate · EUMETSAT H SAF".
  - Cloud cover is a MET Norway point forecast drawn as an area (shot 11 note "MET Norway · 20:00 · low 85%…").
  - The lightning exception says "of the assembly point".
- **Fix.**

| Now | Row label | Note line under the row (source · time) | Key line when on (missing today; every other row has one) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lightning strikes | `Lightning` | `Strike density · NOAA nowCOAST · 8 km grid · fetched 20:18` / `Flash area · EUMETSAT MTG-LI · 2 km grid` / `SIMULATED strikes (prototype)` | `Red tint = lightning in that grid cell · bolt = strike (simulated only)` |
| Precipitation (radar) | `Precipitation now` | `Radar · IEM NEXRAD · ~5 min` / `Satellite estimate · EUMETSAT H SAF` | `Grey wash = rain rate, darker = heavier` |
| Rain & snow totals (24 h) | keep | Europe: `Not available for this area` (move the licence reason to Sources) | `Grey ground tint = 24 h total` |
| Cloud cover | `Cloud cover (forecast)` | `MET Norway forecast for 20:00 · low 85% · mid 70% · high 40%` | `Faint layers = forecast cover at ~1 / 4 / 8 km` |

Exception copy: change "the assembly point" to `the hotel assembly point` (06 l.96 term), so that it is never confused with rally points.

### P1-4: Opening a person from a floor card is a dead end

- **Evidence.**
  - `tripTwin.js selectPerson` calls `setFloorFocus(null)` (l.482).
  - `onSelect` replaces the crumb `› Floor 8` with `› Kai Vance` (page.js l.354).
  - The person card has no back link.
  - Esc then clears everything, because floor focus is already gone. The brief's chain is Hotel → Floor N → Person, but only two of those steps are walkable.
- **Fix.** When the person was picked from a floor card or the docked cutaway, keep floor focus as context:
  - Crumb: `… › Floor 8 › Kai Vance`.
  - Person card first line: `‹ Floor 8`.
  - The 3D slab stays.
  - Esc order: popover → person (back to Floor 8) → floor (back to the hotel card) → hotel selection → nothing.
  - Help Esc row: `Close panel, then go back one step (person › floor › hotel), then clear · never exits`.

  Picking a person from the rail, Needs attention or Find still clears floor focus, because that starts a new context.

### P1-5: `[ ]` means two different things, and `]` moves *down* the building

- **Evidence.**
  - page.js l.1026–1033: with a floor focused, `[ ]` step through flagged floors. The array is top-first, so `]` goes to the next lower floor. Otherwise `[ ]` step through Needs attention (l.1058).
  - ↑/↓ only work while DOM focus is inside the cutaway. In floor focus the cutaway is not on screen: when calm the card shows the floor card instead (1600-02); during an incident the docked cutaway is squeezed out (1600-10, 1280-09).
- **Fix.**
  - `↑` / `↓` while a floor is focused (and not typing): go to the floor above or below that has people or assignments. The same keys work in the cutaway.
  - Visible control in the floor card header: `▲ Floor 9` `▼ Floor 7`, with the title "Floor above with people or assignments (↑)".
  - `[` `]` always mean previous / next **person** in the list the current card came from: Needs attention by default, or the floor card's rows in floor focus (phase-1 §5). The person card gets `‹ ›` buttons.
  - Help rows: `↑ ↓ · Floor above / below` and `[ ] · Previous / next person in the current list`.

### P2-10: The weather chip mixes forecast, live, simulated and sample time

- **Evidence.**
  - `82 °F · clear · MET Norway 20:00` sits next to `21:32 local · sample`. The 20:00 is a forecast valid time, but it is not labelled as one.
  - `82 °F · SIMULATED moderate rain · MET Norway 20:00` attributes simulated rain to MET Norway.
  - The unit follows the region (`useF = region === 'us'`, l.610), not Display › Units, which still offers Meters / Feet / Yards.
- **Fix.**
  - Chip: `82 °F · clear · forecast 20:00 · MET Norway`.
  - Simulated: `82 °F · MET Norway 20:00 · SIMULATED rain (prototype)`. SIMULATED must never sit inside an attributed source.
  - Chip title: `Weather now at Fairmont Austin (real time, not sample time)`.
  - Units: `Metric (m, km, °C)` · `US (ft, mi, °F)`. Remove Yards (phase-1 L-18). Temperature follows the setting.

## What works (keep)

- The cutaway is part of the hotel card, not a layer. During an incident it docks in the incident panel and leaves a pointer behind, so the one-readout-one-home rule holds.
- Hazards › Weather is collapsed, off by default, and every row carries a source and time note. "Rain & snow totals" is disabled with a reason rather than hidden. Lightning is evaluated even when the layer is off.
- Scale grid and Elevation are independent Site layers, with settings in Display, a back-pointer in Display, and `G` / `E` keys. The Display popover now holds only settings.
- "Assigned here, not detected here" gives a reason on every row ("Floor unknown — phone has no pressure sensor", "Outside the hotel, nearby", "Last known · 2h ago"). The words "missing" and "empty" never appear.
- The EX-FLR-03 clause in the top line and the hero sub-line `+ 1 assigned above with floor not detected` are exact and honest.
- `REPORTED FLOOR · NOT VERIFIED` as the Floor 9 kicker. Also London's "Floor count not mapped for this building · floor numbering not verified", an honest failure.
- `‹ Hotel` back link, `› Floor N` crumb, Esc never exits, and "No key sends a message or dismisses a report."
- `Copy for hotel / responders` gives a plain-text floor list, with floor-only assignments recorded in 07.
- The footer credit `Weather: MET Norway (CC BY 4.0)`, Sources lines with "forecast for … · fetched …", and "Official weather warnings unavailable right now".
- `#tt-site` is reserved (move it per P1-6).
