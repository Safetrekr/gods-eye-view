# Trip Twin phase 2: UX review 01 (operator task flow under time pressure)

Date: 2026-10-15. Reviewer: factory UX designer. Review only: no code or existing files were changed.
Evidence: `shots/1280-01`…`14` and `shots/1600-01`…`14`, plus `1280-text.json` and `1600-text.json`. Code was read, not edited: `twinCutaway.js`, `twinData.js` (`floorOccupancy`, `floorRoster`, `deriveMode`, `assessSnapshot`), `twinHud.js` (`renderIncident`, `pathCards`, `responderText`), `page.js` (cutaway wiring, keyboard, weather), `twinWeather.js`, `tripTwin.css`, `twin.html`. I also ran the Demo A fixture through `assessSnapshot`, `floorOccupancy` and `floorRosterText` (Austin and London) to read the exact copy text.
Decisions kept, not reopened: white/amber/red-hatch cutaway colours; no green/red on people; hotel auto-selected on arrival; people on their estimated floors; at-or-above rows in both the rail and the incident panel; weather off by default; `[` `]` step floors while a floor list is open; the fictional sample clock beside live weather.

## Verdict

Phase 2 puts the right answers in the right places. In Austin Demo A the operator sees `2 likely at or above floor 9 + 1 assigned above with floor not detected` and the three names with no clicks. One click on a bar opens a floor list. Grid and Elevation work as independent layers with clear feedback. Weather stays out of the way until it matters. The keyboard map is complete.

Three things stop it from being trusted under pressure:
1. **London shows a false zero.** The hero reads `0 likely at or above floor 9` in a building whose floor numbering is not verified.
2. **The floor list leaves out the person who matters most.** Maya Ruiz (likely 10, range 9–11) is not on the floor 9 list, sits last on floor 11, and is missing from the floor 9 text the operator copies for the hotel desk.
3. **Amber means two things.** "Assigned, none detected" also counts people who are seen outside the hotel, so mid-evacuation it flags the floors people have already left.

At 1280 × 720 the docked cutaway also pushes `Copy for responders` below the fold. That is the hotel-desk task. All three P0/P1 logic fixes are in `twinData.js` and are about 40 lines in total. Their copy is given below.

### The three operator questions, as built (Austin Demo A, 1280 × 720)

| Question | Path today | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Who might be on or above floor 9? | 0 clicks: incident panel hero + 3 names (`1280-08`) | **Pass** in Austin. **Fails in London** (finding 1). The floor 9 list contradicts the hero (finding 2) |
| Who is assigned there but not detected? | 1 click per floor (bar → floor list); 3 clicks for floors 9, 10, 11 | **Misleads.** Amber and "not detected" mix people with an unknown floor and people seen outside the hotel (finding 3). The most relevant person is in the last section (finding 2) |
| What do I tell the hotel desk? | Scroll the incident panel, then `Copy for responders`. Or open a floor, scroll the card, then `Copy for hotel / responders` | **Slow.** Both buttons are below the fold at 1280 (finding 4). The floor text leaves out range-touching people (finding 2) |

## Findings (ranked)

### 1. P0 · London: the hero shows a confident `0` when the floors cannot be compared
**Evidence:** `1280-14`, `1600-14`. Top line: `… — 0 likely at or above, 8 more may still be inside …`. Hero: big `0` `likely at or above floor 9`. Fixture run: London estimates read `Rough: levels 2–4 … above lobby · Floor numbering not verified`. The cutaway correctly says `Floor count not mapped for this building · floor numbering not verified`.
**What is wrong:** `floorPartition` compares the hotel desk's "floor 9" with phone estimates measured in levels above the lobby. Neither the floor count nor the numbering is known, so the comparison means nothing, but it still produces `0`. The likeliest wrong reading is "nobody is up there". A zero in the largest type on screen is a guess presented as fact (00-CONTEXT, honest failure). EX-FLR-03 also goes quiet, because London has no rooming list.
**Fix:**
- In `deriveMode` / `renderIncident`: when the lodging place has `levels_above_ground == null` (numbering not verified), do not render the hero number.
- Hero block text: `Floor estimates can't be matched to the hotel's floor 9 here — this building's floor numbering is not verified`. Second line: `8 at the hotel: 5 with a level estimate · 2 floor unknown · 1 last est. only — all listed below`.
- List all 8 in the at-or-above slot under the heading `At the hotel · floor not comparable`, highest estimate first (`Maya Ruiz · Rough: levels 2–4 above lobby · 40s ago`).
- Top line: `Smoke reported, floor 9 (hotel desk, 2 min ago, not verified) — floors not comparable here (numbering not verified), 8 may still be inside, 3 not current or not located`.
- Add a test that London Demo A never renders `0 likely`.
- Optional: in the report dialog, for unverified buildings only, add `How does this hotel number floors? Lobby = 0 / Lobby = 1 / Don't know`. With an answer, the comparison can run, labelled `numbering set by operator`.

### 2. P0 · The floor list leaves out or buries people whose estimate range includes that floor, and so does the copy for the hotel
**Evidence:** `1280-09` / `1600-09`: the floor 9 list (the reported floor) shows `Likely here now (±1) · 1 — Aron Kim`. Maya Ruiz, `Likely floor 10 (9–11)`, is not listed, but the hero lists her as at or above. `1280-10`: on floor 11 she is the **last** row, under a third heading `Assigned here · estimate range includes this floor`, below six `Outside the hotel, nearby` rows and a `Show all 7`. The floor 11 bar says `8 assigned · none detected` while her range covers 11. The floor 8 bar says `none detected` while Aron Kim's range (8–10) covers 8. Copied text for floor 9 (from `floorRosterText`) has no Maya at all. Code: `floorRoster` builds sections only from `likely` (likeliest floor = this one) and `assigned`; `touches` is used only in the hover plate.
**What is wrong:** "±1" in the heading suggests the list includes everyone within one floor. It does not. On the reported floor, the people whose band touches it are exactly the ones responders need. The section named `Assigned here` after `Assigned here, not detected here` reads as a duplicate heading.
**Fix (`floorRoster`, `floorBarText`, `floorRosterText`):** use these sections, in this order:

| # | Heading | Who | Row note (example) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `Likely here now · estimate ±1 floor` | likeliest floor = this one | `Aron Kim · Likely floor 9 (8–10) · 55s ago · assigned here` |
| 2 | `Possibly here · estimate range includes floor 9` | band touches, likeliest elsewhere (assigned or not) | `Maya Ruiz · Likely floor 10 (9–11) · 55s ago · assigned floor 11` |
| 3 | `Assigned here · floor not known` | assigned, at the hotel or not current/not located, with floor unknown / not shared / last est. only / no estimate | `Leo Brandt · Floor unknown — phone has no pressure sensor · at the hotel, position 40s ago` |
| 4 | `Assigned here · estimate on another floor` | assigned, band entirely elsewhere | `Nia Park · Rough: floors 5–8 · 55s ago` |
| 5 | `Assigned here · now outside the hotel ({n})` | assigned, verdict Outside, nearby / Away | Collapsed to one line of names: `Hana Lee (chaperone), Ada Brooks, Ben Ito, Cara Moss, Dev Shah, Emi Sato` |

- Bars never say `none detected` when `touches > 0`. Use `0 likely · 1 possibly` instead.
- The copied text uses the same five sections in the same order.

### 3. P1 · Amber "assigned · none detected" counts people seen outside, and white hides assigned people who are not detected
**Evidence:** `1280-08`, `1600-08`: during the evacuation the docked cutaway paints floor 11 (`8 assigned · none detected`) and floor 8 (`11 assigned · none detected`) amber. The fixture shows 6 of 7 and 6 of 11 of those people are `Outside the hotel, nearby`. `1280-01`: in calm, floor 8 shows plain white `2 likely`, yet its list (`1280-02`) has `Assigned here, not detected here · 9`. Code: `state = likely.length ? 'occupied' : assigned.length ? 'assigned-none-detected' : …`.
**What is wrong:** Amber is the attention colour. Mid-incident it marks the floors people have already left, and the bar text invites "19 of ours not detected on 8 and 11" over the phone to the desk. In calm, a floor where 9 of 11 are not detected looks like a normal occupied floor.
**Fix:**
- Amber outline when `floorNotKnown(assigned) > 0` (section 3 above), whatever the `likely` count. People in section 5 (outside the hotel) never drive amber.
- White fill keeps meaning `likely` (unchanged).
- Bar text, full width: `{likely} likely · {assigned} assigned · {k} floor not known · {o} outside`. Drop zero terms and keep `likely` first.
- Compact (docked), Demo A: floor 11 `1 floor not known · 6 outside · 1 possibly`. Floor 8 `5 floor not known · 6 outside · 1 possibly`.
- Hover plate unchanged in shape.
- No new colour. A floor that is reported and also has floor-not-known people keeps the red hatch and appends `· 1 floor not known` in amber text.

### 4. P1 · At 1280 × 720 both Copy buttons need a scroll, and the hotel card takes the space
**Evidence:**
- `1280-08`: the incident panel ends at `5 more may still be inside` (cut off). The official slot, `Copy for responders` and the paths are all below the fold, because the docked cutaway (about 230 px) now sits between the names and the Copy button. The bottom 110 px of the column is the hotel card saying only `Floors: in the incident panel above · H`.
- `1280-02` and `1600-02` (calm): the floor 8 list stops at `Rory Owen` / `Tess Cole`, with 220–330 px of unused column below. `.tt-rcol > .tt-card.floor-view { max-height: 58% }` applies even when no incident panel is open, so `Show all 9` and `Copy for hotel / responders` are hidden.

**What is wrong:** "What do I tell the hotel desk" is the task, and phase 1 set the goal at one click (G3). It is now scroll plus click in both modes.
**Fix:**
- (a) Incident panel order: kicker → title → hero + `Copy for responders` **on the hero row, right-aligned** → names → weather lines → docked cutaway → `n more may still be inside` → official slot → paths.
- (b) Apply the 58% cap only when the incident panel is visible. Otherwise the floor card fills the column. Make the Copy button sticky at the card foot (`position: sticky; bottom: 0`) in both cases.
- (c) While the cutaway is docked, show the auto-selected hotel card as a single 36 px line: `Fairmont Austin · 37 floors · ~122 m ›` (`›` expands). Do not show the `Floors: in the incident panel above` pointer card.

### 5. P1 · The cutaway reads as a complete picture: blank upper floors suggest "nobody up there"
**Evidence:** `1280-01`, `1280-08`: floors 12–37 are blank bars (26 of 37). The only clue that some people have no floor is the footer `Floor unknown 9 · Not shared 1 · Last est. only 0`. That footer does not reconcile with the roster. In calm, the bars total 6 likely, while 15 are `Inside (likely)` and 3 `At hotel, undetermined`. Calm hint: `Point at a floor · Enter opens who is on it`.
**What is wrong:** A person whose floor is unknown could be on floor 20. Blank bars plus an unreconciled footer let a tired operator read "upper floors clear". "Who is on it" overclaims for an estimate list.
**Fix:**
- Footer, reconciled and in plain words: `6 placed on a floor · 12 at the hotel with no floor: unknown 9 · not shared 1 · no estimate 2 — could be on any floor`. In the incident: `5 placed on a floor · 3 at the hotel with no floor: unknown 2 · last est. only 1 — could be on any floor`.
- Above the highest flagged floor, label the quiet run once in the gutter: `12–37 · no estimates or assignments`. This replaces repeated numbers.
- Calm hint: `Click a floor (or ↑ ↓ then Enter) to see who is likely there and who is assigned`.

### 6. P1 · In floor focus the 3D scene contradicts the list
**Evidence:** `1280-02`, `1600-02`: floor 8 focus. The camera looks down onto the floor 8 slab, and 8–9 pins (PN, SD, IS, LB, …) appear to stand on it. Those people are at lobby level or have an unknown floor (Leo Brandt, Ivy Stone). The card says `Likely here now · 2`. All 27 pins keep full strength. `1280-09` / `1280-10`: the red reported-floor slab and the white focus slab are hard to tell apart at the -14° pitch. Code: `frameFloor` uses `HeadingPitchRange(heading, -14°, …)` and nothing dims.
**What is wrong:** The 3D view and the list must agree (the plan's own rule for the slab). From above, height is the weakest depth cue, so the scene seems to show more people on the floor than the estimate supports.
**Fix:** while a floor is in focus:
- Pins in sections 1–2 of that floor's list get the selection ring. All other pins drop to 25% opacity, with labels hidden.
- The slab carries its own edge label at the ruler: `Floor 8 · 2 likely · 11 assigned`.
- Pitch -6° (near-horizontal) so the slab reads as a level.
- Esc / back restores pin opacity.

### 7. P1 · Floor focus is not fully reversible, and in the incident it leads to a dead end
**Evidence:**
- `page.js` `clearFloorFocus()` clears the slab and card but leaves the camera framed on the floor. The operator who was at Neighborhood must work out `2` for themselves.
- `1600-09`: when the floor list opens during the incident, the incident panel shrinks and the docked cutaway scrolls out of view, so no other floor can be picked by pointer.
- `‹ Hotel` returns to a card whose only floor content is `Floors: in the incident panel above · H`.
- While a floor is open, `[` `]` silently stop stepping through Needs attention (documented in `?`, invisible elsewhere).

**Fix:**
- Store the camera view on `focusFloor`. On Esc, `‹ back` or `×`, fly back to it if the operator has not moved the camera since. Otherwise leave the camera where it is.
- During an incident, the back link reads `‹ Incident` and closes the floor card, so the panel gets its full height back.
- Put a floor stepper in the floor-card header: `Floor 9 · ▲ 10 · ▼ 8` (buttons, same as `[` `]`).
- Under the header: `[ ] step floors while this list is open · Esc returns`.

### 8. P1 · Floor focus is hard to find from where the operator is looking during an incident
**Evidence:**
- `1280-08`: the docked cutaway has no heading and no hint (compact mode sets the plate to `''`). It looks like a chart, not a control.
- The incident title `Smoke · floor 9 · hotel desk · 02:12` and the rail group header `AT OR ABOVE REPORTED FLOOR 9` are plain text.
- Nothing in 3D opens a floor. ROUND-2 §1b asked for clicks "in the 3D view or the cutaway"; `twinSelect.js` has no floor pick.

**Fix:**
- Docked heading, 1 line: `FLOORS · click one for its list`.
- Make `floor 9` in the incident title a button that runs `focusFloor(9)` (`aria-label="Open floor 9 list"`). Do the same for the rail group header.
- Clicking the reported-floor slab or a ruler floor number opens that floor.
- Keep `H`.

### 9. P1 · Simulated rain enters the path cards and the responder text without a label, and arrive-by ignores it
**Evidence:**
- `1280-11` and `1280-12` text: the path card shows `Moderate rain: al[low +25% walking time → 13–17 min]`. There is no `SIMULATED`, while the chip says `SIMULATED moderate rain`.
- `pathCards` adds the line from `pace` with no simulated flag. `responderText` copies `p.lines`, so the line reaches responders.
- `Suggested arrive-by: 32 min` stays as before (computed from unadjusted distance).
- The title still reads `walk 10–13 min`, so the card shows two walk times.
- Live rain comes from the MET Norway next-hour **forecast**, not radar, and is not labelled as a forecast.

**What is wrong:** It breaks the rule "SIMULATED everywhere", and the numbers on the card disagree with each other.
**Fix:**
- Line copy: `SIMULATED moderate rain: allow +25% walking time → 13–17 min` when simulated. When live: `Moderate rain (MET Norway forecast 20:00): allow +25% walking time → 13–17 min`.
- Recompute arrive-by with the factor and show the arithmetic in "Why these numbers?". Otherwise append `· arrive-by not adjusted for rain`.
- Keep the title's walk time unchanged so the allowance reads as a delta.

### 10. P1 · Live weather layers are grey texture with no scale, so they add noise rather than answers
**Evidence:** `1600-13` / `1280-13` (live Austin, City scale, precipitation + 24 h totals + cloud on): a coarse grey checkerboard covers the whole ground to the horizon, on a clear night with no rain. `1280-12` / `1600-12`: the simulated precipitation is a large grey blob with no label in the scene. The Layers rows give source and fetch time but no value or scale.
**What is wrong:** The operator cannot tell what the grey means, how much rain it is, or whether there is any at the hotel. On a clear night the overlay is pure noise. The plan said accumulation would be "a ground tint inside the site". It is not clipped.
**Fix:**
- Clip every weather tint to the site disc.
- Under each Layers row, show a value at the hotel and a 3-step scale. For example `24 h total at hotel: 3 mm · light < 5 · moderate 5–25 · heavy > 25 mm · IEM MRMS 20:19`, and `Precipitation now at hotel: none · radar IEM 20:19`.
- When a layer has no signal within the box, draw nothing and say `Precipitation (radar) · none within 20 km · 20:19`.
- In the scene, label the precipitation overlay once at its edge: `Rain · radar 20:19` / `SIMULATED rain`.

### 11. P2 · The weather chip presents a forecast as current conditions, and credits MET Norway for simulated rain
**Evidence:** `1280-01`: `82 °F · clear · MET Norway 20:00`, where 20:00 is the forecast valid time (`hhmm(sum.validTime)`). `1280-11`: `82 °F · SIMULATED moderate rain · MET Norw…`, which credits MET Norway for simulated rain and is truncated at 1280. Everything else in the top bar says `sample`, while the chip is live and unmarked.
**Fix:**
- Live: `Forecast 20:00 · 82 °F · clear`, with the source in the tooltip and the Sources popover (credit stays in the source line).
- Simulated: two parts, `Forecast 20:00 · 82 °F` + a dashed `SIMULATED rain` tag.
- When an official warning is active, add the relevant field: `· wind 25 mph`.
- At width < 1440, drop words, never the `SIMULATED` tag.

### 12. P2 · The lightning line sits above the hero, uses a different distance from the chip, and names an ambiguous place
**Evidence:** `1280-11`, `1600-12`: `SIMULATED lightning 6.3 km SW of the assembly point, 4 min ago — 16 people outdoors there` sits **above** `2 likely at or above floor 9` and pushes it down 54 px. The edge chip says `SIMULATED Lightning 6.2 km SW` (measured from the site centre). "The assembly point" can be confused with `Rally point (primary)`.
**Fix:**
- Place it after the names and before the docked cutaway. It concerns a different group (the people outdoors).
- Copy: `SIMULATED lightning 6.3 km SW of the hotel assembly point (across the street) · 4 min ago · 16 people outdoors there`.
- Edge chip, same reference: `SIMULATED lightning · 6.3 km from assembly point`.
- Keep the rule running when the layer is off (it does today; keep it).

### 13. P2 · EX-FLR-03: the panel hard-codes "above", Leo's row lacks where and when, and the rule misses people with no current position
**Evidence:**
- `renderIncident` always writes `+ {n} assigned above with floor not detected`, while `deriveMode` correctly switches to `at or above` when the assigned floor equals the reported floor.
- Leo Brandt's row (`1280-08`): `Assigned floor 11 · floor not detected — phone has no pressure sensor`, with no age and no "at the hotel".
- `assessSnapshot` admits only `unknown` / `not_shared`. Someone assigned to 11 who is not current at the hotel, not located, or last-estimate-only stays in "Not current" / "Not located" and is not counted against floor 9.

**Fix:**
- Use the same wording as the top line.
- Leo's row: `Assigned floor 11 · floor not detected — phone has no pressure sensor · at the hotel · position 40s ago`.
- Extend the rule to any assigned ≥ reported whose last verdict was at the hotel and whose floor is not current, with the reason `Assigned floor 11 · position not current (last at the hotel 12m ago)`. Count it in the same clause.

### 14. P2 · At 1280 the new Site rows are below the Layers fold, and the warning line costs a row of attention
**Evidence:**
- `1280-06` (named "layers-site-rows"): the popover ends at `Within 1 km of a heat detection`. `Scale grid` and `Elevation (terrain contours)` are hidden below, with no scroll cue (`.tt-pop` scrolls silently).
- `1600-06` shows them.
- `1280-08`: `Official weather warning: Wind Advisory (NWS) until Fri 19:00` wraps to 2 lines at the rail head in the incident, leaving room for only 2.5 at-or-above rows.

**Fix:**
- Add a bottom fade/shadow on `.tt-pop` when it is scrollable.
- Order the groups People · Hazards · Site · Safety · Paths, so Site is above the fold.
- In incident mode, shorten the warning to one line: `NWS Wind Advisory until Fri 19:00 ›` (`›` opens the headline).
- Keep the full form in calm.

### 15. P2 · Two Copy buttons with different scopes and similar names
**Evidence:** `Copy for responders` (incident panel, whole incident: groups + first path) and `Copy for hotel / responders` (floor card, one floor), in `1280-08` and `1280-09`.
**What is wrong:** Under time pressure the operator has to stop and work out which one the desk needs.
**Fix:** Name the buttons by their content:
- `Copy incident summary` (incident panel).
- `Copy floor 9 list` (floor card).
- After copying, show both buttons as `Copied · 14 lines` for 1.6 s, so the operator knows the size before pasting.

## What works: keep

- **The incident hero with names, bands and ages, at zero clicks** (`1280-08`). Phase-1 problem 1 is solved. EX-FLR-03's `+ 1 assigned above with floor not detected` is the right shape, in amber, with the reason on Leo's row.
- **The top line is one sentence carrying the new clause**, and `No official instruction on file · Headcount: not reported` stays.
- **Cutaway placement and the docked copy.** It sits at the top of the hotel card in calm and moves into the incident panel when a reported floor is in the building. There is one copy, and `H` reaches whichever is showing. The red hatch matches the 3D slab.
- **Honest copy almost everywhere:** `FLOOR · ESTIMATES` kicker; `No current estimate puts anyone on this floor` (no "empty"); not-detected reasons (`Floor unknown — no ground reference since this phone entered the hotel`, `Estimate elsewhere · Rough: floors 5–8`); `Phone-barometer estimates, not verified · assignments are floor only`; the footer counts are never omitted.
- **London's cutaway message** (`Floor count not mapped for this building · floor numbering not verified`) and the fixture choice of no rooming list where numbering is unverified. Finding 1 only asks the hero to follow the same rule.
- **Keyboard:** one tab stop in the cutaway, ↑/↓ moves, Enter opens. Esc closes in order (panel → floor list → selection, never exits). `G` and `E` are independent. The source line confirms the state in words (`Grid and elevation off`, `Elevation contours every 2 m, relative to hotel …`). The help overlay lists every key and says `No key sends a message or dismisses a report`.
- **Weather is quiet by default.** The sub-group is collapsed and off. `Weather · 4 on` shows the state in the closed row. Lightning rule EX-WX-01 runs even when the layer is off. `SIMULATED` appears in the chip, the edge chip and the incident line. Rows show their source and fetch time. Licensing kept the prototype on sources that are free for commercial use.
- **Breadcrumb `› Floor 9`** and the `‹ Hotel` / `×` pair on the floor card give the operator a visible way back (finding 7 only asks that the camera follows).
- **The `#tt-site` slot** is reserved and hidden, so phase 3 adds a site switcher without moving the bar.
