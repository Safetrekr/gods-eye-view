# Phase 2 · UI / visual review

Reviewer: factory-ui-designer · 2026-10-15 · Review only, no code touched.
Inputs: `00-CONTEXT.md` (COLOR AND BRAND), `ROUND-2.md`, the approved phase-2 plan, `review/02-ui-review.md`, `review/00-FIX-LIST.md`, `tripTwin.css` (phase-2 sections), `twinCutaway.js`, `twinWeather.js`, `twinPeople.js` (`setFloorFocus`), `twin.html` (footer), and the 28 screenshots plus `1600-text.json` / `1280-text.json`.
Contrast numbers are WCAG 2.x ratios. They are computed from the CSS tokens blended over `--tt-card #07212c`, or measured from screenshot pixels where marked "measured".

## Verdict

The full cutaway in the hotel card works. It is calm and readable, it uses the agreed colours, and it puts text inside every flagged bar, so colour never carries meaning alone. Weather is correctly placed in Layers › Hazards › Weather. Four problems need fixing before phase 2 is done:

1. The source line pushes the Cesium/Esri credits off screen.
2. When a floor is opened during an incident, the docked cutaway disappears, at exactly the moment it matters most.
3. The 3D floor-focus slab is almost invisible (about 1.2:1), and its white fill disagrees with the amber cutaway on floor 11.
4. The weather layers look like fog and a checkerboard rather than data. Lightning and clouds do not appear in any captured frame.

Fix the occupied-bar contrast curve and the compact-bar sizing in the same pass. No motion or flashing problems were found (see the end).

## Findings (ranked)

### P0-1 · Required credits pushed off screen; active weather sources not credited
**Evidence:** 1600-03, 1280-03 and 1280-04. With grid and elevation on, the ground text ("… Elevation contours every 2 m, relative to hotel street level") overflows the 22 px source line. The Cesium ion logo, "Powered by Esri" and "Data attribution" are cut off ("Pov…" in 1600-03; gone entirely in 1280-04). In 1600-13 (live MRMS on) the line credits only "Weather: MET Norway". IEM/NOAA MRMS, nowCOAST and (in London) EUMETSAT, which is CC BY 4.0, are not credited.
**Problem:** Required notices are hidden, the same S1 class as phase 1. `.tt-sources` is `overflow:hidden; white-space:nowrap`, and nothing in it can shrink.
**Fix:**
```css
.tt-sources > span:not(.tt-credits-slot) { flex: 0 1 auto; min-width: 0; overflow: hidden; text-overflow: ellipsis; }
#tt-ground-line { flex-shrink: 4; }             /* truncates first */
.tt-credits-slot { flex: 0 0 auto; margin-left: auto; padding-left: 12px; background: var(--tt-field); }
```
Ground copy: `Grid 50 m (bold 250 m) · Contours 2 m, from hotel street level`. The weather span lists only the layers that are on, for example `Weather: MET Norway · Radar: NOAA MRMS via IEM 20:19` (London: `· EUMETSAT (CC BY 4.0)`). Put the full text in a `title` attribute.

### P1-2 · Docked cutaway disappears when a floor is opened during an incident
**Evidence:** 1600-09 and 1600-10. With the floor list open, the incident panel shrinks to about 330 px and shows only the hero and names. The compact cutaway sits behind the scroll, and only its top hairlines show at y≈350. At 1280 (1280-09, 1280-10) the panel stops at "Aron Kim", and the cutaway is fully hidden. The floor card has no cutaway either.
**Problem:** The perspective-free floor read, which is the reason the cutaway is docked in the incident panel (review/03 T1), disappears exactly when the operator drills into the reported floor or the floors above it. At that point no 2D view shows where floor 9 sits relative to 11 and the roof.
**Fix:** While `.floor-view` is open, render the same compact cutaway (160 px) at the top of the floor card, with the open floor marked (see P2-12). Collapse the incident panel to its header:
```css
.tt-rcol:has(> .tt-card.floor-view:not([hidden])) > .tt-incident { flex: 0 0 auto; max-height: 132px; }
```
The panel then shows kicker, title and hero line, plus a `Show incident ▾` link. The incident names stay in the rail, so nothing is lost. Give the docked cutaway a kicker, `FLOORS · ESTIMATES`. Today it floats unlabelled between the names and the footer (1600-08).

### P1-3 · The busiest floor gets the least legible text
**Evidence:** `twinCutaway.js` sets `--fill = min(0.55, 0.16 + 0.1·n)`. White `#eff7f3` text on that fill measures:
- n = 1: **6.79:1**
- n = 2: **4.91:1**
- n = 3: **3.62:1** (fails AA at 11 px)
- n ≥ 4: **2.79:1** (fails)

The demo peaks at "2 likely" (1600-01), so the screenshots hide the problem. A real trip with 3 or more on a floor will hit it.
**Problem:** Contrast falls exactly where attention should rise.
**Fix:** Cap the alpha and let the text carry the count: `--fill = Math.min(0.28, 0.14 + 0.05 * n)`. That gives 0.19 / 0.24 / 0.28, or ≈8.6 / 7.3 / 6.4:1. Do not switch to dark text. It only reaches 4.5:1 at a fill of 0.46 or more, which washes out the neighbouring bars.

### P1-4 · Compact bars too small: text touches the outline; hatch cuts through glyphs
**Evidence:**
- 1600-08 and 1280-08 (floors 11 and 8, "8 assigned · none detected"): the bars are 11 px tall with 10 px Inter at line-height 1 inside a 1 px inset amber outline. The descenders touch the outline, so the text reads as underlined.
- The reported bar ("Reported · not verified · 1 likely") puts the 135° hatch stripes under the glyphs. Pink `#f299a6` on the dark stripe measures **3.85:1** (fails 4.5:1 at 10–11 px).
- 10 px is below the phase-1 type floor of 11 px.

**Fix:**
- In `twinCutaway.js`, set `tall = compact ? 14 : 16`.
- CSS: `.tt-cut.compact .tt-cut-floor .t { font-size: 11px; line-height: 12px; }`.
- Reported bar: put a solid tint under the text and confine the hatch to a cap at the right end:
```css
.tt-cut-floor[data-state='reported']::after {
  background:
    repeating-linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(238,119,137,.55) 0 2px, transparent 2px 5px) right / 28px 100% no-repeat,
    rgba(238,119,137,.16);
  box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px var(--tt-hazard);
}
```
Pink on the 0.16 tint measures **6.27:1**. It is still a hatch, so it is still not colour alone. Keep the amber outline (Jesse's decision). The extra 3 px of height is what clears it.

### P1-5 · The 3D floor-focus slab is near-invisible, z-fights with the reported partition, and contradicts the cutaway
**Evidence:**
- An amplified diff of 1600-02 against 1600-03 shows a slab at floor 8, but the measured luminance step against the glass is **1.15–1.34:1** (pixels such as (149,162,165) against (136,151,155)). That is below the 3:1 non-text minimum (1.4.11). In practice the operator sees only the "Floor 8" plate.
- On floor 9 (1600-09, 1280-09) the white edge rings sit at the same z as the pink partition, so the two cannot be told apart.
- On floor 11 (1600-10) the slab is filled white, which means "likely occupied" in the cutaway, while the cutaway shows that floor amber, "no one detected". The plan's goal ("so the 2D and 3D agree") is not met.
- At 1280-09 the "Floor 9" plate covers the ruler's 9 tick and touches the 10.
- The plate uses mono for a word, and its background `#0b1416` is not a token.

**Fix (in `setFloorFocus`):**
- Fill by floor state: occupied `#eff7f3` α 0.16; assigned-none-detected `#f59e0b` α 0.14; reported, no fill (the pink volume already shows it); quiet, no fill.
- Edge rings: width 3, `#eff7f3` α 1.0, scaled 0.6 m outward from the footprint centroid, and placed at z0 + 0.2 / z1 − 0.2 so they sit outside the pink lines. Add four vertical corner posts.
- While focus is on, drop the rest of the hotel glass to 50 % of its alpha. This is the reference film's "isolate the component" move, and it gives the slab its contrast.
- Plate: text `Floor 9 · reported` when the focused floor is the reported one; `font: 600 12px Inter`; background `#061a23` α 0.9; anchor at `rulerAt(-14)` with a `pixelOffset` of (-8, 0) so it clears the ruler ticks.

### P1-6 · Precipitation and 24 h totals look like fog and a floor texture, not data
**Evidence:**
- 1600-12 and 1280-12 (simulated rain): a soft grey radial blob with no edge covers the west half of the scene and dims the site. There is no key and no boundary.
- 1600-13 and 1280-13 (live MRMS 24 h): about 1 km source pixels are drawn as hard squares, giving a perspective checkerboard that reads as a rendering glitch.
- In both, the 12 km data box has no visible edge, so "no grey" looks like "no rain" rather than "outside the data".

**Fix (in `recolour` / scene):**
- Upsample the WMS image 4× on a canvas with `imageSmoothingQuality = 'high'`.
- Then quantise to 3 classes at α 0.18 / 0.36 / 0.54, still in the neutral grey `#b8c3c7` (never blue). Rain now: light / moderate / heavy. 24 h: < 6 / 6–25 / > 25 mm.
- Stroke class boundaries 1 px `#b8c3c7` α 0.6 so the bands have edges.
- Draw the data box as a dashed `#80959b` hairline labelled `Radar area · 12 km · IEM 20:15` (Inter 11 px on `#061a23` α 0.85).
- Add one legend chip in the scene safe area, bottom-left: `Rain now  ░ light  ▒ moderate  ▓ heavy · SIMULATED`.

### P1-7 · Lightning and cloud cover are on but appear nowhere
**Evidence:**
- 1600-11 and 1600-12 show "Weather · 4 on" and a chip reading "SIMULATED Lightning 6.2 km SW". No bolt glyph or age label is in frame at City scale (1600-12, 1280-12): the nearest simulated strike is 6.2 km out, beyond the framed area.
- Cloud rings have a radius of 6 km at 1, 4 and 8 km altitude, so they fall outside the frame. Their fill α of 0.02–0.07 measures 1.12:1.
- The only legible cloud data is the Layers row text.

**Fix:**
- When a strike is off-screen, pin a 16 px bolt to the safe-area edge along its bearing, labelled `6.2 km SW · 4 min` (Inter 11 px `#f299a6` on `#061a23` α 0.9). Keep it static.
- Add a dashed hazard-pink ground ring, 1.5 px, at the EX-WX-01 threshold, labelled `10 km lightning watch`. It states the rule, not decoration.
- Clouds: draw the rings at 1.5 × the site-disc radius so they frame the site at Neighborhood and City scale, labelled at the near edge `Low cloud 85 % · ~1 km`. Drop the fill, and make the ring α `0.35 + 0.5·cover` (≥ 3:1).

### P1-8 · The weather chip truncates its source and time, the freshness part
**Evidence:** 1600-11, 1600-12, 1280-11 and 1280-12 show `82 °F · SIMULATED moderate rain · MET Norw…`. `max-width: 280px` plus ellipsis cuts the end of the string, which is the source and valid time. The real temperature and the simulated condition also share one string.
**Fix:** Split the chip into three spans:
- `.wx-val`: mono numerals, `flex: 0 0 auto`
- `.wx-cond`: `flex: 0 1 auto; min-width: 0`; the only part that truncates
- `.wx-src`: `flex: 0 0 auto`, for example `· 20:00`

Set `max-width: 320px`. Mark simulation with a leading `SIM` badge (mono 10 px, 1 px dashed `#80959b`, 0 4px padding) instead of the word inline. When the valid time is more than 60 min old, append `· 1 h old` in `--tt-text-dim`. Full source goes in `title` and the source line (P0-1).

### P2-9 · Amber weather line outranks the hero count; the rail top line is a 5-line pink block
**Evidence:** In 1600-11/12 and 1280-11/12, the 2–3-line amber lightning sentence sits between the incident title and the hero "2", pushing the hero down by about 40 px. A second amber line ("+ 1 assigned above…") sits directly under it with a different meaning. The rail top line (1600-08, 1280-08) is 5 lines of `--tt-hazard-text`.
**Fix:** Move the weather line below the cutaway as a flagged row:
```css
.tt-wx-line { border-left: 2px solid var(--tt-attention); background: rgba(245,158,11,.06);
  padding: 4px 8px; color: var(--tt-text-2); font-size: 12px; }
```
Precede it with the kicker `WEATHER`. In the chip, name the reference point so it no longer clashes with the panel's 6.3 km: `Lightning 6.2 km SW of hotel`. In the rail top line, colour only the first clause pink ("Smoke reported, floor 9 (hotel desk, 2 min ago, not verified)"); set the rest in `--tt-text`.

### P2-10 · The official warning uses the "draft" line style
**Evidence:** 1600-01 and 1280-01, "Official weather warning: Wind Advisory (NWS)". `.tt-wx-official` uses a 2 px dashed left rule. In the Layers legend, dashed means draft / not approved / rough range / alternate.
**Fix:** `.tt-wx-official { border-left: 2px solid var(--tt-attention); color: var(--tt-text); padding-left: 8px; }`, with the kicker `OFFICIAL · NWS` (mono 10 px, `--tt-text-dim`). Keep dashed only for the empty "No official instruction on file" slot.

### P2-11 · Compact cutaway: quiet floors form a venetian blind, and the top floor has no number
**Evidence:** In 1600-08 and 1280-08, 26 quiet floors draw as about 2.5 px bars with 1 px gaps, an unlabelled striped block of roughly 90 px. The compact cutaway shows no gutter numbers for quiet floors, so building height and the distance to the roof can't be read. The full cutaway labels 35 but not the top floor, 37 (1600-01).
**Fix:**
- `.tt-cut.compact .tt-cut-list { gap: 0; }`, with a 1 px `#1e404f` separator every 5 floors (`border-top` on `label % 5 === 0`). Quiet runs then read as one column with ticks.
- Always label the top floor and floor 1 in both modes, plus every 10th floor in compact mode (mono 10 px, `--tt-text-dim`, 6.79:1).
- Make the faint "range" tint (α 0.08, ≈1.1:1) a 1 px bracket in the gutter instead, as the plan specified.

### P2-12 · Selected floor and keyboard focus look the same; quiet bars are tiny targets
**Evidence:** `.focus::after` is a 2 px `#eff7f3` outline and `:focus-visible::after` a 2 px `#e3e7e9` outline, which look the same. On a white-filled bar, either outline nearly vanishes. Quiet bars are 2.5–5.5 px tall and flagged bars 11–15 px, both under the WCAG 2.2 2.5.8 target size of 24 px, with no equivalent control.
**Fix:**
- Mark the selected floor with `box-shadow: inset 3px 0 0 var(--tt-text)` on the bar and a `▸` before the gutter number in `--tt-text` 600.
- Keep the outline for `:focus-visible` only, with `outline-offset: 2px`.
- Add a `Go to floor [  ]` number field (28 px tall) in the `FLOORS ▾` header row as the equivalent control. Enter opens the floor list.

### P2-13 · Floor card clipped while space sits empty; kicker counts wrap; no scroll cues
**Evidence:**
- 1600-02: the floor card stops at 470 px (`max-height: 58%`) while about 340 px of the column below it is empty. "Show all 9" and "Copy for hotel / responders" are hidden below the scroll.
- 1280-02, -09 and -10: the kicker `ASSIGNED HERE, NOT DETECTED HERE · 9` wraps, leaving `· 9` alone on a second line.
- 1600-08: the path card is cut mid-word at the panel bottom.
- 1280-06: the new Site rows (Scale grid, Elevation) are below the fold of Layers.

None of these show a scroll cue.
**Fix:**
- `.tt-rcol:has(> .tt-incident:not([hidden])) > .tt-card.floor-view { max-height: 58%; }`; otherwise `max-height: none`.
- Section kicker: `display:flex; justify-content:space-between`, with the count right-aligned as in the rail's `NEEDS ATTENTION 06`, copy `NOT DETECTED HERE`, and `letter-spacing: .08em` below 1440 px.
- For any scrolling panel or popover, add a `.scrolls` class when `scrollHeight > clientHeight` and apply `mask-image: linear-gradient(#000 calc(100% - 20px), transparent)`.

### P2-14 · Scene labels collide on floor focus and along the ruler
**Evidence:**
- 1600-02, -09 and -10, and 1280-02, -03 and -09: "Fire station / Austin Fire Station Number 1" overprints "Rally point (secondary) · Draft · not approved", so neither can be read.
- 1280-01 and 1280-08: the people initials at the ruler (M.R., K.V., J.O., A.K., N.P.…) sit on top of the ruler numbers ("10 M.R.").

**Fix:** Declutter per frame: when two label rectangles intersect, keep the safety place's name and reduce the other to its glyph with the label on hover or focus. Move ruler initials into their own column at a +18 px x offset, at least 14 px apart vertically, with a 1 px `#80959b` leader to their band.

### P2-15 · Grid, Elevation and None look identical at the default framing
**Evidence:** Apart from the source line, 1600-03, -04 and -05 (and the 1280 equivalents) cannot be told apart. The contours measure **1.9:1** and run mostly behind the hotel's floor plate. The operator cannot see what G or E did.
**Fix:** Draw contours as 1 px `#80959b` at α 0.55. Every 10 m, draw an index contour at α 0.85 with a mono 10 px label (`+4 m`). Keep the grid solid `#365462` so the two read as different marks. Briefly show the toggle state in the source line, for example `Contours on`.

## Motion and flashing

No problems found. The CSS has no `@keyframes` and no animations. The only transitions are the 120 ms veil and 200 ms control transitions, and `prefers-reduced-motion` removes the veil. Lightning glyphs are static canvases (`twinWeather.js` notes WCAG 2.3.1). None of the cutaway or focus changes animate. Keep it that way: P1-7's off-screen pointer must be static too.

## Brand and colour check

- Green is used only on approved places (chips and safety pins) and blue only on paths.
- Precipitation and cloud are neutral grey.
- People never get red or green: the cutaway uses white, amber and pink hatch only for floors.
- There is one meaning conflict: the white 3D slab on an amber floor (P1-5).

## What works — keep

- **Full cutaway in the hotel card (1600-01, 1280-01).** One bar per floor with the top floor first. Text in each flagged bar ("2 likely", "8 assigned · none detected", "Reported · not verified") makes it readable without colour. The gutter numbers (mono 10 px, 6.79:1) and the footer counts `Floor unknown 9 · Not shared 1 · Last est. only 0` follow the honesty rules.
- **Jesse's colour decision, as implemented.** White means likely occupied, amber means assigned with no one detected, pink hatch means reported. Amber text on its bar measures 6.88:1. There is no green or red on people anywhere.
- **Per-floor roster.** The section order is Likely here now (±1) → Assigned here, not detected here → Assigned here. Every row gives a reason ("Floor unknown — phone has no pressure sensor", "Estimate elsewhere · Rough: floors 5–8"), and the words "missing" and "empty" never appear. Its left rules (white for likely, amber for not detected) match the cutaway.
- **London "Floor count not mapped" state (1600-14).** It degrades honestly, without drawing guessed floors.
- **Layers › Hazards › Weather sub-group.** It is collapsed with a `Weather · 4 on` count, each row carries its source and fetch time, and the SIMULATED notes sit under the rows.
- **Weather chip placement in the top bar's status cluster** (once P1-8 is fixed), and the `SIMULATED` labels on chips and in the incident panel.
- **Breadcrumb `› Floor 9`, the `‹ Hotel` back link and `Copy for hotel / responders`.** These are clear drill-down and return paths.
- **The incident hero.** "2 likely at or above floor 9 / + 1 assigned above with floor not detected" is the right number in the right place. Keep it above everything weather-related.
