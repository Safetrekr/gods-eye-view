# Phase 10 UX review: SOC multi-view (walls + remote)

**Verdict:** the foundation holds. Entry, QR pairing, the rail, the confirm and undo bar, the stale and offline states, and seeing hands-on from the remote all work. Three things would hurt a duty officer during an incident:
- one Day-2 push hides traveler positions on every wall;
- one tap on an event row moves every wall;
- S6 contradicts the other walls.

On top of that, the target picker promises "one screen or All", but half the controls ignore it.

**Works well:** the bar names the screens it changed (21, 24), "Remote offline" turns amber (33), and the remote sees hands-on (29).

## P0

### 1. "Advance · Day 2" to All during an open incident hides traveler positions on every wall
- **Shot:** 22→23, 30-S1. The confirm says only "Every screen → Advance · Day 2". S1 then shows ADVANCE with no pins under the INCIDENT banner.
- **Problem:** All-in-one asks first during an incident (`page.js:2205`). Screen mode bypasses it with `confirmed:true` (`page.js:5050`). Session pushes also ignore pins.
- **Fix:**
  - While an incident is open, the confirm names it: "Incident open at Fairmont Austin. Walls stop showing traveler positions." Make the primary button "Show Day 2 on S5 only".
  - The incident role and pinned screens stay Live.

### 2. Day-plan rows move every wall without asking
- **Shot:** 27 (`stations.js:375–412`).
- **Problem:**
  - "GO TO SITE" and the place rows send `session.siteKey`/`spotlight` with no confirm. They ignore the target and move S1's camera ("the camera never moves by itself").
  - A trip switch reloads every wall into another city (`twinScreen.js:148`). The confirm just says "Focus: Youth choir tour".
- **Fix:**
  - Respect the target, and confirm when the push goes to All or an incident is open.
  - Trip confirm: "All walls reload into London (~10 s blank)". Block trip switches during an incident.

### 3. S6 contradicts S1–S5
- **Shot:** 10-S6 against 10-S1/S2.
  - The Spring choir tile shows 13:59 local; the headers show 21:39.
  - The tile shows 22 not current; S2 shows 4.
  - The Austin tile lacks the smoke report; the London tile shows one. The incident reads as London's.
  - S4 shows "forecast 13:00" at 21:40.
- **Problem:** The tiles and the weather use the real clock and baked scenarios (`page.js:5207`).
- **Fix:**
  - One clock for every wall.
  - The focus tile takes `ss.report`.
  - Baked incidents name their site and carry a "sample scenario" tag.

## P1

### 4. The target picker's scope is unclear
- **Shot:** 22, 24, 25.
- **Problem:**
  - Mode, Day, Site, Incident and Visitor apply to the whole session, yet the picker still shows S1 or All.
  - "Show Day 2 on S1" turns the Site wall into The day (`remote.js:163`). The day write it sends first has no undo.
- **Fix:**
  - A scope line per station: "This station changes every screen", with the picker dimmed.
  - "Show Day" defaults to the screen that holds The day, and says so when it would replace a role.
  - One undo for both writes.

### 5. The day plan is buried
- **Shot:** 22, 27.
- **Problem:** Days are listed twice. The plan and its Show button sit below the trips, the mode and the days, off-screen on the iPad. Every day shows "!".
- **Fix:**
  - The rail holds the days.
  - The content opens on the plan with "Show Day N on …" at the top.
  - "!" becomes "3 to check".

### 6. The incident banner covers content
- **Shot:** 10-S1 (Group hotel), 10-S2/S3 (Needs attention), 10-S4 (timeline), 30-S5.
- **Fix:** Reserve a bottom band for the banner.

### 7. Four walls look alike
- **Shot:** 10-S1/S2/S4/S5 all share the same oblique camera.
- **Problem:**
  - S2 has no top-down multi-site map and no big headcounts, and it repeats S3's panel.
  - S4 shows no radar.
  - S5 shows checkboxes, which are interactive on a display-only wall, and no timeline.
- **Fix:**
  - S2: top-down at City scale, with headcounts at 96 px or more.
  - S4: radar on.
  - S5: a timeline and no checkboxes.

### 8. Text too small to read from 3 m
- **Shot:** all walls.
- **Problem:** At 3 m from a 55-inch wall, text needs about 28 px. The chip, "Remote offline" and "pinned" are 16 px; SAMPLE and map labels about 12 px; floor numbers about 10 px.
- **Fix:**
  - The chip at 28 px or more.
  - REMOTE OFFLINE, PINNED, HANDS-ON and VISITOR as badge-size pills.
  - Wall scale also applies to Cesium labels.

### 9. Visitor mode is invisible on the wall
- **Shot:** 10-* (initials) against 32 (names). `tt-visitor` has no CSS.
- **Fix:** A "VISITOR MODE" pill on every wall and in the remote's top bar.

### 10. Red on people
- **Shot:** 10-S1, 10-S3, 33. Incident-board person rows have red left bars.
- **Fix:** A neutral or amber bar.

### 11. Hands-on and the remote collide
- **Shot:** 29, 34.
- **Problem:**
  - `pointermove` alone starts hands-on, so a cursor crossing the Mac Studio's monitors flags walls for 20 s.
  - The wall barely shows it, and shows nothing when the remote is offline (34).
  - Camera pushes wait only while the button is held.
  - The hand-made selection stays, and the remote can't clear it.
- **Fix:**
  - Start hands-on on pointerdown or wheel only.
  - A "HANDS-ON · 18 s" pill on the wall.
  - Hold camera pushes for the whole window, with "apply now / when free" on the remote.
  - "Return S1 to role view" on the remote.

### 12. Gaps in the Incident station
- **Shot:** 24.
- **Problem:**
  - "Clear the reported floor" has no confirm.
  - Reporting again silently overwrites the open report.
  - "Frame … on S1" is hard-coded to slot 1.
  - The remote never lists who may be at or above the floor.
  - A row reads "Aging · Aging".
- **Fix:**
  - Confirm: "Clear smoke · floor 9 (16 may be at or above)?"
  - Rename the report button "Change reported floor (now 9)" while a report is open.
  - Frame on the screen that holds the Site role.
  - List the 16 by name, highest floor first.

### 13. Setting up across two computers is blind
- **Shot:** 03, 04.
- **Problem:**
  - The "Open S1–S6" buttons never show who has joined.
  - Opening a slot already open elsewhere silently takes it over (`twinSessionHost.js`).
  - Placement is `(slot−1) mod monitors`, so S5 and S6 land on the Mac Studio's monitors 1–2.
  - A relay restart means pairing everything again.
- **Fix:**
  - "S3 · joined · Mac Studio" on each button.
  - Confirm before a takeover.
  - A monitor picker per button.
  - Save tokens to disk.

### 14. The iPad rail can't fit six screens
- **Shot:** 20 and 25 show S1–S4 only; 26 clips S6.
- **Fix:** 44 px rows with a scroll fade.

## P2
- **15.** An empty status bar renders as a blank box (20, 29).
- **16.** With no controller, say "Take control", not "Request control" (29).
- **17. Colour:** the status dots are green, the entry URLs are blue, and the remote's OPEN pill is amber while the walls use red. Use steel for status and red for the incident pill.
- **18.** Entry:
  - 02 never asks where the screens will run.
  - Setup links last 10 min but pair codes 5 min. Label each.
- **19.** An orphan "or" under "PATH FROM FAIRMONT AUSTIN" (30-S5).
