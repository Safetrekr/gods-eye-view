Reviewer: Information Architecture · 16 Oct 2026 · review only, no code edits.

The rail, the numbered tiles and the persistent target picker are the right frame. The problems are these:

## P10-A · P0 · Scope is invisible, and "Pinned" does not hold
**Shots:** 22–23 (S3 pinned; `Confirm: Every screen → Advance · Day 2`; the undo bar names no skipped screen). `twinScreen.js` 166.

**Problem.** These are session writes: Live/Advance, Day, trip, site, spotlight, reported floor, Visitor mode and Return all. They reach every wall, pinned S3 included, so the pinned incident screen turns `ADVANCE · DAY 2` during an open incident. `Frame reported floor on S1` and `Make S3 the incident screen` are hard-wired to one screen. Only Screens and Layers follow the picker.

**Spec.**
- Each remote section kicker ends with its scope: `· APPLIES TO S2` / `· APPLIES TO ALL SCREENS` / `· EVERY SCREEN, PINS INCLUDED`. While a section that ignores the target is in view, the picker dims with the hint `Not used here`.
- Live/Advance/Day obey pins.
  - Confirm: `Advance · Day 2 → S1, S2, S4, S5, S6 · S3 pinned, stays Live`.
  - Undo bar: `Advance · Day 2 · S1, S2, S4, S5, S6 · S3 pinned, skipped`.
- Role-addressed actions: `Frame floor 9 on Site (S1)` (disabled with `No screen shows Site` if none does) and `Make {target} the incident screen`.

## P10-C · P1 · Station taxonomy and names: fix the order now
**Problem.**

- People (06 Q1, P0) is deferred while its roster sits inside Incident.
- An "Alerts" tile breaks 06 F4 (Alert is reserved for official sources), because that station would also hold sensed data and feed health.

**Spec.** Tiles 01–04 are the operator's questions and mirror walls S3, S2, S4 and S5. Tiles 05–06 run the wall. A tile label is the first word of its title.

| Tile | Title | Level-2 list |
|---|---|---|
| 01 Incident | Incident | open: `Reported floor 9 · not verified` · `May still be inside · 16` · `Actions` |
| 02 People | People | the six 06 buckets with counts → person → `Spotlight on walls` |

| 04 Trip | {trip title} | days |
| 05 Screens | Screens | `Roles & pins` · `Pair a screen` · `Whole wall` (no second screen list; the picker is the only selector) |
| 06 Layers | Layers & view | `People · Hazards · Safety · Paths · Site` (06 §2.1; the build's `Safety & paths` merges what 06 keeps apart). Text size moves here from Screens |

- Move the roster out of Incident into 02 now.
- Pin `Return all to Live + defaults` in the top bar (SOC §3). Today it is three levels down.
- Roles:
  - `Travelers` → **`People`** (`S2 · PEOPLE`): one word across the role, the Layers group, the roster heading and station 02.
  - `The day` → **`Day plan`** (`S5 · DAY PLAN`).
- The Trip station heading `Trips on watch` → **`Trip in focus`**. Trips on watch is S6's name.

## §18 amendments (Phase 10)

| Term (as shown) | Meaning | Never say |
|---|---|---|
| `S1`…`S6` · `All` | The target. Scope is printed on every section | "every control applies to the target" |
| `Pinned` / `PINNED` | All pushes and Live/Advance/Day skip it. Visitor mode and the reported floor still reach it | "locked", `PIN` |
| `Site` · `People` · `Incident & attention` · `Weather & alerts` · `Day plan` · `Trips on watch` | The six roles | "Travelers" (role), "The day" |
| `01 Incident · 02 People · 03 Weather · 04 Trip · 05 Screens · 06 Layers` | Stations; tile = first word of the title | "Alerts" as a station (06 F4) |
| `Trip in focus` | The trip S1–S5 show | "Trips on watch" outside S6 |
| `LIVE` · `ADVANCE · DAY 2` · `TODAY'S PLAN · DAY 1` | Wall badge | `PLAN · TODAY` |
| `LIVE · INCIDENT · …` | Banner on a wall that is not Live | `0 min ago` |
| `Remote: Laptop · changed 13 s ago` | Wall chip | a bare age |
| `Hands-on here · 0:18` | Local mouse use; the camera may differ from the remote | `hands-on` without a time |
| `VISITOR MODE · INITIALS ONLY` | Wall mark | — |
| `Not paired` · `Online` · `Offline · 0:42` | Screen link states | "not connected" |
| `Make S1 the day plan · Day 2` · `Plan Day 2 on all screens (Advance)` | Day verbs | `Show Day 2 on All` |
