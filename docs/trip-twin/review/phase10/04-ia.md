# Phase 10 IA review: SOC multi-view (entry, walls, remote)

Reviewer: Information Architecture · 16 Oct 2026 · review only, no code edits.

Inputs: SOC-MULTIVIEW.md, 06 §1–§2.1, §10 (modes), §17–§18. Shots 01–34. Code: `twinScreenRoles.js`, `twinEntry.js`, `twinScreen.js` (paint 218–263), `remote/stations.js`, `remote/remote.js` (send 116–140, showDay 163–179), `demoTrips.js` (tripTile 54–118).

Result: **2 P0 · 6 P1 · 4 P2**, plus §18 amendments. No banned words appear in the visible strings (`corridors` is a key only).

The two-level rail, the numbered tiles and one persistent target picker are the right frame. The main problems are scope and naming:
- §18 says "every control applies to the target". About half the controls ignore it.
- Pins do not hold against session-wide pushes.
- Walls describe themselves in three mode vocabularies.
- The station order follows build order, not what the duty officer asks.

## P10-A · P0 · Control scope is invisible, and "Pinned" does not hold
**Shots:** 22–23 (S3 pinned, `Confirm: Every screen → Advance · Day 2`, undo bar names no skipped screen). Code: `twinScreen.js` 166 (`mode = ss.mode` for every non-day role).

**Problem.** These are session writes: Live/Advance, Day, trip focus, site jump, place spotlight, reported floor, Visitor mode and Return all. They reach every wall, pinned S3 included. So the pinned incident screen turns `ADVANCE · DAY 2` during an open incident. Some controls are hard-wired to one screen (`Frame reported floor on S1`, `Make S3 the incident screen`). Only Screens and Layers follow the picker.

**Spec.**
- Every section kicker on the remote ends with its scope: `· APPLIES TO S2`, `· APPLIES TO ALL SCREENS` or `· EVERY SCREEN, PINS INCLUDED`. While a section that ignores the target is in view, the picker dims and shows the hint `Not used here`.
- Live/Advance and Day obey pins: a pinned screen keeps its own mode. Confirm text: `Advance · Day 2 → S1, S2, S4, S5, S6 · S3 pinned, stays Live`. Undo bar: `Advance · Day 2 · S1, S2, S4, S5, S6 · S3 pinned, skipped`.
- Only Visitor mode and the reported floor still reach pinned screens. Their buttons say so: `Visitor mode · every screen, pins included`.
- Role-addressed actions: `Frame floor 9 on Site (S1)` (disabled with `No screen shows Site` if none does) and `Make {target} the incident screen`.

## P10-C · P1 · Station taxonomy: set the final order now
**Problem.** `01 Screens` comes first because it was built first. Serras numbers become muscle memory, so renumbering after phase 11 has a cost. The People question (06 Q1, P0) is deferred, yet its roster already sits inside Incident (`Travelers · 27 … SPOTLIGHT`). "Alerts" as a tile breaks 06 F4: Alert is reserved for official sources, and that station would also hold sensed data and feed health.

**Spec.** Final top level (01–04 are the operator's questions and mirror walls S3, S2, S4, S5; 05–06 run the wall):

| Tile | Title (sub) | Level-2 list |
|---|---|---|
| 01 Incident | Incident (Report, board, responders) | open: `Reported floor 9 · not verified` · `May still be inside · 16` · `Actions`; idle: none |
| 02 People | People (Roster, spotlight) | the six 06 buckets with counts → person → `Spotlight on walls` |
| 03 Weather | Weather & alerts (Official · sensed · feeds) | `Official alerts · {n}` · `Sensed · lightning, radar, heat` · `Feed health` |
| 04 Trip | {trip title} (Days, plan, places) | days |
| 05 Screens | Screens (Roles, pins, pairing) | `Roles & pins` · `Pair a screen` · `Whole wall`. No second screen list: the target picker is the only selector |
| 06 Layers | Layers & view (Scale, layers, text size) | the 06 §2.1 groups |

- Rule: a tile label is the first word of its title.
- Move the roster out of Incident into 02 now. A `Next phase` tile may show it.
- Pin `Return all to Live + defaults` in the top bar, as SOC §3 requires. It is currently three levels down in Screens.

## P10-D · P1 · Role and label names
- `Travelers` role → **`People`** (kicker `S2 · PEOPLE`). This matches the Layers group, the roster heading `PEOPLE` and station 02. One word for one concept.
- `The day` → **`Day plan`** (kicker `S5 · DAY PLAN`). It is the noun the Trip hierarchy uses, and it never reads as "today".
- In the Trip station, the `Trips on watch` heading → **`Trip in focus`**. Trips on watch is S6's name.
- In Layers, `Safety & paths` breaks 06 §2.1, which keeps Safety and Paths apart so that "path never inherits safe". Use the single-screen groups: `People · Hazards (Weather overlays sub-kicker) · Safety · Paths · Site`. `Ways out view` goes under Site.
- Move Text size from Screens to Layers & view, so all display settings sit in one place.

## P10-E · P1 · Trip: days → day plan, and "Show Day 2"
**Problem.**
- `Show Day 2 on S1` silently turns the Site wall into The day (a role change hidden in a verb), and `on all screens` switches the mode instead.
- The day list appears twice, in the rail and in the content.
- The rail flag is a bare `!`.

**Spec.**
- Rail level 2: `D2 · Day 2 · 3 to check` (or `· 1 event`). Content shows only `Trip in focus` + `Mode` + the selected day's plan.
- The verb depends on the target:
  - Target is the Day plan screen: `Show Day 2 on S5`.
  - Target has another role: `Make S1 the day plan · Day 2`. Confirm: `S1 Site → Day plan · Day 2`.
  - Target is All: `Plan Day 2 on all screens (Advance)`, with the P10-A confirm.
- Event tag `GO TO SITE` → `SITE → EVERY SCREEN`. Place tag → `SPOTLIGHT`.
- Paths note: `Day 2 paths are on the Day plan screen (S5) · saved and suggested, with their checks`.

## P10-F · P1 · How a wall says what it is
**Shots:** 10-S5, 30-S5, 31, 33, 34, 32.

**Spec.**
- **Badge:** `LIVE` · `ADVANCE · DAY 2`. S5 in Live shows **`TODAY'S PLAN · DAY 1`**, not `PLAN · TODAY · DAY 1`, because 06 §10's mode words are Live/Advance. The S5 panel in Live reads `Today's plan · Day 1 · no traveler positions shown`, not `Advance planning…`.
- **Banner on a non-Live wall:** `LIVE · INCIDENT · Smoke reported, floor 9 · operator entry · under 1 min ago · not verified`. Never `0 min ago`.
- **S5 `where` in Advance:** `Spring choir tour · Day 2 · Sat 17 Oct`.
- **Chip:**
  - Drop the slot, because the kicker already shows it. The age names what it counts: `Remote: Laptop · changed 13 s ago`, then `changed 1:33 ago` (shot 31's `93 s ago` reads like link age).
  - Add `· PINNED` in caps.
  - Remote gone: `Remote offline · 0:45`, or `Remote offline · since 21:41` (shot 33 has no age).
- **Hands-on** shows in every chip state, including offline (shot 34 shows none): `Hands-on here · 0:18 · Remote: Laptop`. On the remote: `S1 · Site · hands-on at the wall · selected Waterline`.
- **Visitor mode** gets a header mark next to SAMPLE: `VISITOR MODE · INITIALS ONLY`. Shots 31 and 32 cannot be told apart by mode: S3 shows `IB` while S1 and S2 show `Iris B.`. Use one name form per mode on every wall.
- **Link states:** one set everywhere: `Not paired` · `Online` · `Offline · 0:42`. Drop `not connected`, `offline`, `Screen not connected` and the rail's `PIN`.

## §18 amendments (Phase 10)

| Term (as shown) | Meaning | Never say |
|---|---|---|
| `S1`…`S6` · `All` | The target. **Scope is printed on every section** (`APPLIES TO S2` / `EVERY SCREEN, PINS INCLUDED`) | "every control applies to the target" (false) |
| `Pinned` / `PINNED` | All pushes **and Live/Advance/Day** skip it; Visitor mode and the reported floor still reach it | "locked", `PIN` |
| `Site` · `People` · `Incident & attention` · `Weather & alerts` · `Day plan` · `Trips on watch` | The six roles | "Travelers" (role), "The day" |
| `01 Incident · 02 People · 03 Weather · 04 Trip · 05 Screens · 06 Layers` | Stations; tile = first word of the title | "Alerts" as a station (06 F4), "Alerts & feeds" |
| `Trip in focus` | The trip S1–S5 show | "Trips on watch" outside S6 |
| `LIVE` · `ADVANCE · DAY 2` · `TODAY'S PLAN · DAY 1` | Wall badge | `PLAN · TODAY` |
| `LIVE · INCIDENT · …` | Banner on a wall not in Live | `0 min ago` |
| `Remote: Laptop · changed 13 s ago` | Wall chip (`changed 1:33 ago` past 60 s) | a bare age |
| `Hands-on here · 0:18` | Someone is using the wall's mouse; the camera may differ from the remote | `hands-on` without a time |
| `VISITOR MODE · INITIALS ONLY` | Wall mark while Visitor mode is on | — |
| `Not paired` · `Online` · `Offline · 0:42` | Screen link states | "not connected", "disconnected" |
| `Make S1 the day plan · Day 2` / `Plan Day 2 on all screens (Advance)` | The day verb when the target is not Day plan / is All | `Show Day 2 on All` |
| `D2 · Day 2 · 3 to check` | Day row | `!` |
| `Add reported floor` · `Clear my reported floor` | Same as single-screen | "Report on every screen" |

**Synonym ring:** monitor, display, TV, wall → `S1`…`S6` · lock, hold, freeze → `Pinned` · travelers, roster, headcount → `People` · today, schedule, itinerary → `Day plan` · alerts, warnings, NWS → `Weather` (Official alerts).
