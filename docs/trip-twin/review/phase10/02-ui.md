# Phase 10: UI / visual review (SOC walls + remote)

Review only. Readability floor at 3 m: a 75″ 1080p panel is 0.86 mm/px, so body text **≥24 px**, labels **≥20 px**, and anything ≤16 px is decoration.

**Verdict:** The remote is a faithful Serras rail. These match: the 96 px wells, the steel 01–06 index, the active card, press-down, the 18 px fog pane and the fog swap. The swap to selection white is correct. The walls are the weak half:
- the incident banner covers content on five roles;
- S6 outlines the wrong trip;
- at 3 m, S1, S2 and S4 are the same picture.

Fix 1–2 before the demo.

## P0

### 1. The incident banner covers what it summarises
**Shots:** 10-S1–S5, 31-*.
**Problem:** `.tt-wall-banner` floats over the panels (z 22, `max-width:52vw`) and wraps to two lines. It hides S1's GROUP HOTEL card, the first Needs-attention row on S2/S3, the S4 timeline and the last S5 check.
**Spec:**
- Make it a reserved band: `left:0; right:0; bottom:26px; transform:none; max-width:none; height:72px; border-radius:0; border-width:2px 0 0; font:600 32px/72px var(--tt-font); white-space:nowrap; text-overflow:ellipsis`.
- `body.tt-wall-inc{--wall-band:72px}`. Panels, `.tt-rail`, `.tt-rcol`, `.tt-wxtl` and the compass take `bottom:calc((26px + var(--wall-band,0px) + 12px)/var(--wall-scale))`.
- Text: `INCIDENT · Smoke · floor 9 · not verified · reported 21:39`. "0 min ago" froze in 30/31.
- Acked: 44 px tall, 22 px text.

### 2. S6 outlines the wrong trip
**Shot:** 10-S6.
**Problem:** Every wall shows the Austin report. S6 outlines the London tile instead, with an identical "smoke · floor 9" line from its own mock. Spring choir tour shows no incident: `tripsPanel` ignores `session.report`.
**Spec:**
- The focus tile takes `session.report` and sorts first.
- Reword or drop London's mock incident.
- Label the focus tile `IN FOCUS · S1–S5` (20 px mono, `--tt-text-2`).

### 5. Confirm/undo bar never hides; the stage jumps
**Shots:** 20, 29 (empty capsule) vs 21–28.
**Problem:** `.rm-bar{display:flex}` overrides `[hidden]`, so the bar never hides. Each push moves the content 44 px.
**Spec:**
- Fixed slot: `min-height:64px`.
- Idle: transparent border with a dim `Target S1 · nothing pending`.
- Undo: a 2 px steel line shrinks over the 10 s.
- Confirm lists the skips: `… · S3 pinned, skipped` (22 omits it).

### 6. Rail rows clip; the last row hides under the tiles
**Shots:** 20, 25, 26.
**Problem:** The subtitle sits on the row border, and in 26 "Trips on watch" is cut off. `min-height:44px` pins each auto track at 44 px in a constrained grid, while two-line rows need about 55 px.
**Spec:**
- `.r-context{grid-auto-rows:max-content; mask-image:linear-gradient(#000 calc(100% - 24px),transparent)}`.
- `.r-grid{flex:none}`.

### 7. The Incident OPEN pill is amber
**Shots:** 20–28.
**Problem:** Red is for incidents. Serras uses a crit-red pill, and the wall banner is already red.
**Spec:** `.rm-tile .pill{background:var(--rm-hazard); color:#1a0508}`.

- **Tile rows:**
  1. Title 36 px; local time right-aligned in 28 px mono.
  2. Org · site, 24 px, dim.
  3. The incident line in red, or `No incident · 2 official alerts`. The alerts are amber when >0, and are missing today.
  4. Three 48 px mono stats: people · not current · not located. Amber when >0.
  5. `Next: Sat 19:30 · Concert · Festival Hall`, not the ISO date.
  6. Phase · data age.
- Subtitle: `3 trips · sorted by need`.
