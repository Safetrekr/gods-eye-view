# Phase 6 UX review: weather in 3D and the weather timeline

Reviewer: UX (review only, no code edits) · 16 Oct 2026
Inputs: PHASE-6-PLAN.md, WEATHER-TIMELINE-SOURCES.md, shots 1600/1280 01–11 and 1600-text.json, plus twinWeatherFx.js, twinWeatherTimeline.js, twinWeatherTime.js, the weather section of page.js and the end of tripTwin.css.

The direction is right. Forecast and observed data are labelled honestly in most places, amber means "not live", lightning is withheld ahead of now, and an incident snaps the view back to now. The problems below are about three things: which clock a time belongs to, whether an empty map means "dry" or "broken", and a few gaps in the honest labelling.

---

## P0

### 1. The sample trip clock and the real weather clock disagree on screen, and nothing visible explains it
**Shots:** 05, 06, 07, 08, 09, 11 (1600 and 1280)
**Problem:** The top bar reads `21:32 local · sample`. Right next to it, the weather chip reads `forecast 10:00`. The timeline's NOW sits just before a `13:00` tick, and the banner reads `VIEWING FORECAST 13:00 (+3 h)`. An operator sees two different "nows" about 11 hours apart. Without the "+3 h" they could not tell whether 13:00 is later today, tomorrow, or already past.
- In London (08, 11) the top bar still reads `21:32 local`, the same as Austin, while the London weather runs around 16:00. "local" is therefore not local to the site.
- Advance (09) adds a third frame of reference: `Day 2 19:00` in "Chicago time", with "sample dates start today". The weather for that event is the real MET Norway forecast for tomorrow 19:00. That is correct, but nothing says so.
- The only disclosure is the chip tooltip (`… live weather; the sample clock is fictional`). That needs a hover, and most operators will never see it.

**Fix:**
- Relabel the top-bar clock `Sample trip clock 21:32 · fictional`. Drop the word "local".
- While any weather layer is on, add a second top-bar item in `--tt-text-2`: `Weather · real time 10:15 CDT` (BST for London).
- Give every weather time the zone and a day: chip `forecast Fri 10:00 CDT · MET Norway`; banner `VIEWING FORECAST Fri 13:00 CDT (+3 h from real now) — not live …`.
- In Advance, the chip source reads `Day 2 19:00 = Sat 17 Oct 19:00 CDT · forecast +33 h · MET Norway`.
- Acceptance test: from a cold look at shot 06, a new operator can say how far ahead 13:00 is without reading "+3 h".

### 2. Simulated snow credits an invented temperature to MET Norway, and simulation hides the timeline without saying so
**Shots:** 01, 03, 04, 10 (chip `SIM 30 °F SIMULATED snow · prototype · temperature MET Norway`); compare 02 (`65 °F`) and 05 (`65 °F` real)
**Problem:**
- `wxSummary()` (page.js) hard-codes `tempC: -1` for simulated snow. `weatherChipParts()` still prints `temperature MET Norway`. The real MET value at capture was 65 °F, so the chip attributes a made-up number to a named real source. That breaks the honest-labelling rule.
- With either simulation on, `timelineModel()` returns null and the bar disappears (01–04, 10: two weather layers are on but there is no bar). The UI gives no explanation.
- A time scrubbed before simulation was switched on survives under it (`wx.at` is not cleared at page.js:3540/3553). The mode chip then reads `… · SIMULATED DATA · VIEWING FORECAST` with no bar and no Back to now.

**Fix:**
- When any value is simulated, the source text says `prototype · all values simulated`. Use this for snow; for rain, keep `temperature MET Norway` only when the temperature really comes from MET.
- Switching on any simulation sets `wx.at = null`.
- Show the bar in a disabled state with the caption `Timeline off while simulated weather is on · Prototype ▸ turn simulation off`. Don't silently hide it.

## P1

### 3. US timeline: the observed band is missing and −1 h says "newest radar frame not in yet"
**Shots:** 05, 06, 07 (zoomed: the US track has no shading at all; London 08 shades correctly)
**Problem:** At −1 h in Austin the caption reads `newest radar frame not in yet`. The sources doc measured IEM latency at 2–5 min, so a 1-hour-old frame should exist and the message is wrong. The empty track matches one cause: `frameMeta.latestRadar` becomes `NaN`. `Date.parse(undefined)` returns NaN, and NaN gets past `??`. `Math.min(now, NaN)` is NaN, so the gradient stop is invalid and the browser drops the whole gradient. `kindAt` then returns `none` for every past time, and `frameSourceAt` never returns the observed source. The result is that the 2 h US radar loop cannot be reached.
**Fix:**
- Guard with `Number.isFinite(latest) ? latest : now − 10 min`.
- Add a browser check that the US track shows three bands and that −1 h shows `Radar · IEM NEXRAD · observed 09:00`.
- If the latest-frame lookup really fails, say so: `radar frame list unavailable · past frames may be missing`. Never say "not in yet".

### 4. An empty map does not say whether it means dry, not loaded, or failed
**Shots:** 05, 06, 08, 11 (real weather was dry; HRRR +3 h, DWD +6 h and EUMETSAT −1 h 30 all show nothing over the site)
**Problem:** The banner names the source and the valid time but never says what the frame shows. An operator looking at an empty scene with `Forecast radar (model) · NOAA HRRR 13:00` cannot tell "dry" from "frame not drawn". The Layers wording for an empty image (`no data in this area right now`) reads as a data outage, not as dry weather. In 11 at 1280 the caption says `· loading`, but nothing shows when loading finishes.
**Fix:** Three explicit frame states in the banner and in the Precipitation row:
- `frame loaded · no rain or snow shown over the area` (ok, 0 % coverage)
- `loading frame…`
- `frame missing for 13:40 (EUMETSAT gap)` / `unavailable`

Then add the site forecast amount after it: `site forecast 0.0 mm/h`. Replace "no data in this area" with "none shown in this area" wherever the image loaded.

### 5. Ticks have no day, and NOW collides with the first tick at 1280
**Shots:** 05–08, 11; 1280-06 (`NOW3:00`), 1280-11 (`NOW9:00`)
**Problem:** The track spans about 50 h, and the tick labels repeat `13:00 19:00 01:00 07:00` twice with nothing to tell the days apart. At 1280 the 2 h exclusion around NOW is about 20 px, so the labels overlap. The observed segment is about 4 % of the width (a sliver left of NOW), which makes the most-used scrub range the hardest to hit.
**Fix:**
- Put day labels at midnight (`Sat`), in `--tt-text` weight 600; the other ticks stay dim.
- Drop any tick label within 48 px of NOW, measured in pixels rather than hours.
- Give the observed 2 h a fixed minimum width (for example 15 % of the track), a non-linear segment with a visible break mark. Coarse forecast hours then take less room.

### 6. Esc does not return to now while the slider has focus; Play floods screen readers
**Shots:** 06, 07 (state reached by scrubbing); code: page.js keydown
**Problem:**
- The Esc-to-now branch comes after `if (e.target.closest?.('input, select, textarea')) return;`. The range is an `<input>`, so right after a drag or an arrow-key scrub, which is exactly when the user wants it, Esc does nothing.
- The shortcut appears nowhere on screen.
- During Play, `.tt-wxtl-cap` has `role="status"` and is rewritten every 700 ms, so assistive tech gets a long sentence each tick.

**Fix:**
- Handle Esc in a `keydown` listener on the range itself (stop, `onChange(null)`), or exempt `.tt-wxtl-range` from the input guard.
- Label the button `Back to now (Esc)`.
- While playing, set the caption to `aria-live="off"` and announce once on Pause/stop (`Paused at Fri 15:00 CDT, forecast`).

### 7. Travelers are not dimmed while viewing another time (a plan item that was not built)
**Shots:** 06, 07, 08, 11 (HL, JO and the floor bars are at full strength next to forecast weather)
**Problem:** The plan says "people dimmed with 'positions now'". The banner says `travelers stay at their current positions`, but the scene shows live pins at full contrast inside a future or past weather scene. A quick look reads it as "these people at 13:00". The people panel and the floor list on the right do not change either.
**Fix:**
- While `wx.at != null`, set people pins and floor bars to 55 % opacity. Do not change their colour (no red/green on people).
- Add a small tag on the people group: `Positions now · live`.
- Add `· positions now` to the PEOPLE header in the left rail.
- The Live strip and Needs attention stay at full strength, since live data comes first.

### 8. The weather chip truncates the time at 1280 and overlaps Prototype
**Shots:** 1280-06 (`mostly cle… forecast 13:0…`), 1280-01 (`30 °F SIMULAT… prototype · te…`), 1280-11 (`PAST 14:30 · map frames only (the site forecast starts now)` runs under the Prototype button)
**Problem:** The truncation removes exactly the parts that carry honesty: the valid time and the word SIMULATED. In 11 the long fallback string has no truncation at all and collides with the next control.
**Fix:**
- Set the chip's priority order: badge (NOW/FCST/PAST/SIM) → time → temp → condition → source.
- At widths of 1440 px or less, drop the condition and the source first. The source moves to the tooltip and to the timeline caption, which already names it.
- Shorten the London fallback to `PAST 14:30 · map only`, keeping the full sentence in the timeline caption.
- Give the chip `min-width: 0; overflow: hidden; text-overflow: ellipsis` so it can never overlap.

## P2

### 9. 3D precipitation hides the people it should frame, and still snow reads as data points
**Shots:** 02 (rain streaks across the hologram, the people pins and the floor ticks), 03 (City: snow forms a solid speckled disc with a hard edge at the site radius), 01/04 (scattered white dots)
**Problem:**
- In Building view the particles fall through the hologram. That cuts the contrast of the people pins and floor labels, which are the main content.
- In City view the disc edge looks like a drawn zone, a geofence or hazard ring, which the operator could mistake for an area of trip data.
- With Reduce motion on (04), the flakes are still round white dots. Without motion they look like points of interest. "drawn still (Reduce motion)" appears only in the Layers row.

**Fix:**
- Keep a clear column over the hologram for particles too, as the clouds already have. At minimum, halve particle opacity inside the site radius.
- Feather the disc edge over the outer 30 % of the radius.
- Below a camera-distance threshold (City), drop the density by half.
- In still mode, draw short slanted streaks (rain) or six-point glyphs (snow) at about 40 % opacity rather than dots.
- Add a small in-scene caption near the compass: `Snow · drawn still · MET Norway amount` or `SIMULATED snow`.

### 10. The forecast and observed boundary blurs in past views, and model run times are not shown
**Shots:** 07 vs 11, 06, 08, 09
**Problems:**
- **Past views mix forecast data in:**
  - Austin −1 h (07) shows `PAST 64 °F cloudy · forecast 09:00 · MET Norway`, and the 3D clouds come from that forecast, while the banner says observed.
  - London −1 h 30 (11) says the opposite: `map frames only (the site forecast starts now)`. The two cities follow different rules.
- **Banners give the valid time but not the run:** `NOAA HRRR 13:00` and `DWD ICON-EU, 1 h totals 22:00` can be read as "issued at 13:00". Section C of the plan requires the run time.
- **Advance (09):** the chip gives no lead time and no issue time. When an event falls outside the forecast range, `syncAdvanceWeather` sets `wx.at = null` and the 3D weather silently goes back to *now* while the chip still says FCST.
- **The idle caption is fixed:** `Now · latest radar and the site forecast` appears even when Precipitation now is off.

**Fix:**
- Use one rule for past times. Show maps only; the chip reads `PAST 09:00 · radar only`; clouds and haze are hidden in the past (or labelled `clouds: forecast issued 08:17, not observed`).
- Banners read `NOAA HRRR run 08:00 CDT, valid 13:00` and `DWD ICON-EU run 06Z, valid 22:00 BST`.
- Advance chip: `FCST +33 h · issued 08:17`. Outside the range, show `No forecast this far ahead` and clear the 3D effects.
- Build the idle caption from the layers that are actually on.

## Sample clock vs real weather time (summary)
- **Weather timeline (real time):** the timeline, the chip, the banner and the Layers rows all use real wall-clock time in the site's zone (`hhmm` with `city.timezone`).
- **People and trip (fictional time):** the top-bar `21:32 local · sample` and the people data are fictional.
- **Advance events:** these are anchored to real dates ("sample dates start today"), so their weather is a real forecast.
- **What the screen says about it:** nothing visible states which clock is which. The 11-hour gap in 05–07 and the identical `21:32` in Austin and London are the clearest symptoms.

Finding 1 is the fix. Finding 7 (dim people) stops the two clocks from looking like one scene.

## Keep
- Amber `.away` treatment of the bar, the thumb and the caption, with the mode chip suffix `· VIEWING FORECAST` / `· VIEWING PAST` and the FCST/PAST badge. Away from now is impossible to miss.
- `— not live` and `travelers stay at their current positions` in the banner. Keep the wording and add the dimming in finding 7.
- The incident snap-back: an incident while scrubbed returns to now with the notice `Incident reported — weather is back to now`.
- Lightning is not drawn ahead of now, and the row says `Lightning has no forecast · shown for now and the past only`.
- London's honest gap: a `none` segment between the satellite's latest frame and NOW, with `satellite estimate runs about 1 h behind — no frame yet for this time`.
- `stepAt` never stretches a forecast past its period, and frames are never interpolated (502 means missing).
- `further out, less certain` beyond +24 h.
- Model maps are named as models: `Forecast radar (model)`, `Forecast rain (model)`.
- A native range input, so arrow keys and screen-reader value text work; `aria-valuetext` carries the time, the offset and the kind.
- Cloud and haze row: per-band cover %, fog %, "cloud shapes illustrative, kept clear of the site". Clouds never cover the hologram.
- Neutral white and grey particles and clouds, no blue: paths keep blue, and green stays reserved for approved places.
- Reduce motion is respected (still precipitation) and the Layers row says so.
- SIMULATED appears in the mode chip, the SIM badge, the Prototype `SIM ON` flag and the Layers rows. Fix only the source attribution in finding 2.
- Per-layer credits in the footer, updated for the frame source in view.
