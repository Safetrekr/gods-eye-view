# Phase 6 IA review: weather in 3D and the weather timeline

Reviewer: Information Architecture · 16 Oct 2026 · review only, no code edits.

Inputs: PHASE-6-PLAN.md; 06-layer-taxonomy.md (§2.2, §9, §12, §13); shots `1600-01…11`, `1280-06`, `1600-text.json`; code `page.js` (weather section ~2976–3510, Advance ~2050–2135, keydown ~3815–3880), `twinWeatherTimeline.js` (`KIND_TEXT`), `twinWeatherTime.js` (`precipRowText`, `timeSources`), `twinWeather.js` (`weatherChipParts`, `WX` labels), `twin.html` (help 330–394).

Result: **2 P0 · 7 P1 · 1 P2.** The timeline itself is well built: a native range, the honest banner, Back to now, and the incident snap-back. The IA problems are about time and data kind. Some surfaces do not follow the timeline (rows, lightning, the Advance map). Some words mix two things: the chip token mixes time position with data kind, "live" has three meanings, and two clocks are both called "local". The simulated snow chip credits its temperature to the wrong source.

## K1 · P0 · Layer rows and drawn layers do not follow the timeline time

**Shots:** 06 (+3 h, HRRR), 07 (−1 h), 09 (Advance), 10 (rows).

**Problem.**
- `setWxTime` re-renders the Layers panel, but `wxRowNote('precip')` never reads `wx.at`. At +3 h the row still says **"Precipitation now · Radar · IEM NEXRAD (latest, ~5 min)"**, while the map under it is the HRRR 13:00 model frame. At −1 h the map is a past frame, but the row text is the same.
- **Lightning:** `drawWeather` hides lightning only when the time is ahead of now. At "VIEWING PAST 09:00" the lightning on the map is the 10:02 image. Its row says "shown for now and the past only", but there are no past lightning frames.
- **Totals:** a fixed raster of the last 24 h to now, drawn at every timeline position. Nothing says it ignores the timeline.
- **Advance (09):** `wx.at` = Day 2 19:00, which is past HRRR's +18 h. `frameAt` returns null, so the rain map goes blank while its row still says "latest".
- Plan §B says "Each layer row names source and time". Today only the banner does this, and only in Live.

**Spec.** Every weather row note starts with the time that layer really shows. Use one helper, `rowTime(key, at)`.

| Row | At now | Timeline ahead | Timeline behind | Advance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lightning | `Lightning density · NOAA nowCOAST (8 km grid) · 10:02` | `Not shown · lightning has no forecast` | `Not shown · no past lightning frames` (hide it; do not draw 10:02 under a 09:00 banner) | `Not shown in Advance · Live only` |
| Lightning, all away states | add `· the lightning check stays live (rail)` | | | |
| Rain & snow map (K2) | `Radar (observed) · NOAA NEXRAD via IEM · 10:00 frame` | `Forecast map (model) · NOAA HRRR, run 08:00 · 13:00 frame` | `Radar (observed) · 09:00 frame` or `No map for 09:00 · newest radar 08:55` | `No map for Day 2 19:00 · forecast maps reach +18 h` |
| Rain & snow totals | `Last 24 h to 10:00 · NOAA MRMS via IEM` | same + `· does not follow the timeline` | same | same |
| 3D rows | already time-stamped through `precipRowText(at)`: keep | | | prefix `Day 2 19:00 ·` |

Sub-group summary while away from now: `Weather overlays · 2 of 5 on · at +3 h`.

## K2 · P1 · Row names: "now", "Precipitation" vs "Rain & snow", and "(3D, forecast)"

**Shot:** 10.

**Problem.**
1. "Precipitation now" is the only row with a time in its name, and it is the one layer the timeline moves. At +3 h it shows a forecast map but is still called "now". In London its source is a satellite estimate about 65 min behind (`timeSources('eu').observed.until = now − 65 min`).
2. Adjacent rows use two words for one thing, "Precipitation" and "Rain & snow". The operator cannot tell whether "Rain & snow (3D)" shows the same data. It does not: the 3D amount and type come from the site forecast, and only the location comes from the map.
3. Only one label carries the data kind. "Clouds & haze (3D, forecast)" says forecast; "Rain & snow (3D)" does not, although it is also a forecast. With SIM on, the label "forecast" sits above "SIMULATED · low 90 %…" (10).

Rule: the label names the thing and its form (a map, or 3D at the site). The note names the source, the data kind and the time.

**Spec: labels and order.**

```
Weather overlays · {k} of 5 on
  1 Lightning
  2 Rain & snow map
  3 Rain & snow at the site (3D)      note: "… falls where Rain & snow map shows it"
                                       (map off: "… whole site · not a map")
  4 Clouds & haze at the site (3D)    note: "Site forecast 13:00 · MET Norway · low 90 % …"
  5 Rain & snow totals, last 24 h     note: "Map · to 10:00 · does not follow the timeline"
```

Why this order:
- Lightning stays first because it is the safety hazard.
- Rows 2–4 are coupled: the 3D precipitation falls only where row 2 shows rain. So row 3 sits directly under its map.
- Totals is history and ignores the timeline, so it goes last.

Never use in a layer label: "Precipitation", "now", or "radar" for a model or satellite product.

## K3 · P0 · Simulated snow: the chip credits the temperature to MET Norway, the SIMULATED word is truncated, and the drawer allows rain + snow

**Shots:** 01, 03, 04, 10. Chip text: `SIM · 30 °F · SIMULATED snow · prototype · temperature MET Norway`.

**Problem.**
- `wxSummary()` sets the temperature to −1 °C (30 °F) for simulated snow. `weatherChipParts` still adds "temperature MET Norway", so a fixed simulated value is credited to a real source. Simulated rain keeps the real temperature, so the line is true there; only the snow branch is wrong.
- At 1600 the chip cuts the condition to "SIM…" but keeps "prototype · temperature MET Norway" (01, 10). The part that survives the cut is the false part.
- The 3D snow has no SIMULATED caption on the canvas (01, 03, 04), so a screenshot of the twin carries no label. Plan §A says "everything simulated is labelled SIMULATED".
- The drawer has Simulated rain and Simulated snow as two separate checkboxes. If both are on, snow wins with no message.
- The timeline disappears while simulated precipitation is on (`timelineModel` returns null), with no message.

**Spec.**
- Chip, simulated snow: `SIM · 30 °F · SIMULATED snow · prototype · all values simulated`.
- Chip, simulated rain: `SIM · 65 °F · SIMULATED moderate rain · prototype · temperature MET Norway 10:00`.
- Truncation order: drop the source first. Never cut the SIMULATED condition.
- Drawer: one radio group, `Simulated precipitation: Off · Rain · Snow`.
- In the timeline slot, while simulated precipitation is on, show a one-line bar: `SIMULATED snow (prototype) — not real weather · timeline off while simulated weather is on · Prototype › Simulated precipitation: Off`. This also gives the scene its in-canvas label.

## K4 · P1 · The weather chip token mixes time position and data kind

**Shots:** 05 `NOW · 65 °F cloudy · forecast 10:00`; 06 `FCST …`; 07 `PAST · 64 °F cloudy · forecast 09:00`; 11 `PAST 14:30 · map frames only (the site forecast starts now)`; 09 `FCST · Day 2 19:00`; 10 `SIM`.

**Problem.**
- NOW and PAST are positions on the time axis. FCST and SIM are data kinds.
- At −1 h the chip is PAST and also "forecast 09:00". The value is the forecast that was issued for 09:00, not a reading.
- London at −1 h 30 follows a different rule ("map frames only…"), because MET's series starts at the current hour. The same position renders two ways depending on the minute.
- "FCST" is a new abbreviation that is not in 06. The mode chip already spells out "FORECAST".
- §9 says never "now" for a forecast hour, but NOW now sits next to "forecast 10:00".

**Spec.** One axis per slot: the token is the time position, the source is the data kind.

| State | Token | Temp · condition | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Live, at now | `NOW` | `65 °F cloudy` | `site forecast 10:00 · MET Norway` |
| Timeline ahead | `+3 h` | `68 °F mostly clear` | `site forecast 13:00 · MET Norway` |
| Timeline behind | `−1 h` | (no value) | `09:00 · map only · no site reading for past hours` |
| Advance | `DAY 2 19:00` | `62 °F clear` | `site forecast · MET Norway, issued 08:17` |
| Simulated | `SIM` | see K3 | see K3 |

- In the past, never show a MET value. It would be an old forecast shown under a past token.
- Retire FCST.
- Update the §9 row "Live · forecast HH:MM" to the NOW row above.

## K5 · P1 · Data-kind vocabulary: one name per kind across the legend, banner, rows and credits

**Shots:** 05–08, 11. Code: `KIND_TEXT`, `timeSources().*.label`, `radarCover().label`, `WX.*.label`.

**Inventory today:**
- **US radar:** "observed" (legend), "Radar · IEM NEXRAD · observed" (banner), "(latest, ~5 min)" (row), "radar" (3D note), "latest radar" (caption).
- **EU satellite:** "observed" (legend; this overclaims, it is an estimate), "Rain rate · satellite estimate · EUMETSAT H SAF" (banner).
- **Model:** "model forecast map" (legend), **"Forecast radar (model)"** (US banner and credit), "Forecast rain (model)" (EU banner), "the forecast map" (3D note).
- **Site forecast:** "site forecast", "forecast HH:MM · MET Norway", "MET Norway forecast for the site", "MET Norway forecast HH:MM".

**Problem.**
- "Forecast radar" contradicts itself: radar observes, and HRRR simulated reflectivity is a model output. A reader who trusts radar will over-trust the model map.
- The track shades "model" and "site" as if they excluded each other. In fact the site forecast applies at every future time, because it drives the chip and the 3D.
- In London (08) the legend lists "site forecast" although no site segment is drawn: ICON-EU runs the full +48 h, so the site segment has zero length, and the legend filter does not check length.
- The "no data" gap (US last ~10 min, EU last ~65 min) is transparent and not in the legend.
- At −1 h (07) the caption reads "newest radar frame not in yet". That is odd one hour back. Check how `latestRadar` is parsed, and name the times.
- The banners leave out the model run time. Plan §C requires "forecast says forecast and the run time".

**Spec.** The track shades the **map** kind only. The site forecast is drawn as a separate thin line under the track, from NOW to its end.

| Kind | Legend | Banner / row prefix |
|---|---|---|
| Radar (observed) | `radar (observed)` | `Radar (observed) · NOAA NEXRAD via IEM · 09:00` |
| Satellite estimate | `satellite estimate (past)` | `Satellite estimate · EUMETSAT H SAF · 14:30` |
| Forecast map (model) | `forecast map (model)` | `Forecast map (model) · NOAA HRRR, run 08:00 · 13:00` · `Forecast map (model) · DWD ICON-EU, run 06:00 · 1 h totals to 22:00` |
| No map | `no map` | `No map for 09:00 · newest radar 08:55` |
| Site forecast (line) | `site forecast · MET Norway` | `site forecast 13:00 · MET Norway, issued 08:17` |

- Credits: `Forecast map: NOAA/NCEP HRRR via IEM` (not "Forecast radar").
- Show a legend item only when its segment has non-zero length.

## K6 · P1 · Two clocks: the fictional sample clock and the real weather time

**Shots:** 05–08. The top bar shows `21:32 local · sample`. Beside it, the chip shows `forecast 10:00`. The timeline puts NOW at about 10:00, with ticks `13:00 19:00 01:00 07:00 13:00 …`. The banner reads `VIEWING FORECAST 13:00 (+3 h)`.

**Problem.**
- One bar shows two clocks 11 h apart, and both read as "local". People, ages and the sample clock run on scenario time. Every weather time is real site-local time (`hhmm` in `city.timezone`).
- Against "21:32 local", "13:00" reads as 8½ hours ago. The only place that explains this is the chip tooltip ("the sample clock is fictional").
- The ticks are clock times with no day, so 13:00 and 19:00 each appear twice on the 48 h bar (05).
- Plan §B specified relative marks (−2 h · NOW · +18 h · +48 h); the build uses clock times instead.

**Spec.**
- Ticks are relative: `−2 h · NOW · +6 h · +12 h · +18 h · +24 h · +36 h · +48 h`. Add a weekday divider (`Sat`) at real local midnight.
- Show the clock time only in the caption or banner, and always tag it: `Now · 10:02 Austin time (real) · latest radar and the site forecast · MET Norway, issued 08:17`. Away from now: `WEATHER AT 13:00 Austin time (+3 h) …`.
- Top bar: `21:32 sample time`. Drop "local", which is the word the real weather times also use.

## K7 · P1 · "Live" means three things

**Shots:** 06, 07, 08, 11. Also the help text, and the shot 10 legend "Live / stopped · current".

**Problem.**
- "Live" is the mode (Live | Advance), a position state ("Live / stopped"), and now also a weather time: the banner says "— not live" and the help says "Esc or Back to now returns to live".
- In 06 the Live button is pressed while an orange banner says "not live".
- The mode chip "MONITORING · VIEWING FORECAST" does not say what is being viewed. It reads as if the whole twin were a forecast, but people, incidents and the lightning check stay live.
- Plan §B also asked for "people dimmed with 'positions now'". This is not built; only the banner says "travelers stay at their current positions".

**Spec.**
- Banner: `WEATHER AT 13:00 Austin time (+3 h) · forecast — people and incidents are shown now`.
- Mode chip suffix: `· WEATHER +3 h` / `· WEATHER −1 h`, instead of VIEWING FORECAST / PAST.
- If people are dimmed later, label them `positions now`.
- Help: "Back to now (Esc) returns the weather to now".
- Keep "live" only for the mode and the position state. Weather time uses `now`, `+n h` and `−n h`.

## K8 · P1 · Help overlay and Esc

**Code:** `twin.html` 377–386; `page.js` 3847–3858.

**Problem.**
1. **Esc does nothing while the timeline slider has focus.** The keydown handler returns on `e.target.closest('input, select, textarea')` before the weather Esc branch, and the slider is an `<input type=range>`. So Esc is ignored at exactly the moment a keyboard user is scrubbing.
2. The help Esc row lists panel → path → step back → selection. The code inserts "weather back to now" after panels and before path. The help puts that step in a separate row whose `dt` is "Weather timeline", which is not a key.
3. No key reaches the timeline. The ← → keys (plan §B) and the slider's Home / End keys are not documented.
4. "Shown while a weather layer is on" is false in Advance and while simulated precipitation is on.

**Spec: help rows, in key order.**
- `T`: Weather timeline. Focuses the bar; if no weather layer is on, opens Layers › Weather. T is unused.
- `← · →` (timeline focused): 15 min back / forward. `Home · End`: −2 h / end of forecast.
- `Esc`: `Close panel → weather back to now → remove the path → step back (place or person › floor card › site) → clear the selection (never exits)`.
- Below the key list: `Weather timeline: Live only · shown while a weather layer is on · in Advance the weather follows the picked event · off while simulated weather is on`.
- Code: handle Esc on the slider itself, in the timeline module's keydown → `onChange(null)`.

- Away from now, collapsing must never hide the away state.

## K10 · P1 · Advance moves the weather time but does not say so

**Shot:** 09. Code: `syncAdvanceWeather`, `advanceChipParts`.

**Problem.**
- Picking an event moves the 3D rain and clouds to the event time. But the timeline is hidden, the mode chip says only PLANNING, and the only statement is the chip (`FCST 62 °F clear Day 2 19:00 · MET Norway`). The site brief has no weather line.
- **Event already passed** (e.g. Day 1 15:00 later today), or **beyond the forecast:** `wx.at` stays null, so the 3D shows now. `advanceChipParts` returns null, so the chip falls back to `FCST — forecast loading`, which is wrong: nothing is loading, the time is out of range.
- The rain map goes blank for events past +18 h (K1).
- Good, and should be stated: switching to Live resets the weather to now (`setAppMode → syncAdvanceWeather`).

**Spec.**
- Add a WEATHER line to the site brief, under the kicker: `Weather at Day 2 19:00 (concert start) · site forecast · MET Norway, issued 08:17 · 62 °F clear · 3D rain and clouds show this hour · no map this far ahead`.
- Out of range: `Day 2 19:00 is beyond the site forecast (to Mon 06:00) · 3D weather shows now`.
- Passed: `Day 1 15:00 has passed · 3D weather shows now`.
- Chip token: `DAY 2 19:00` (K4). Out of range: `DAY 4 10:00 · no forecast yet`. Never "loading" when the time is out of range.

## Keep list

- Banner shape: a relative offset in parentheses, then the source, then a statement about people. Only the words change (K7).
- `Back to now` as the button label. It is hidden at now and shown only when away.
- An incident snaps the weather back to now, with the notice `Incident reported — weather is back to now`.
- `precipRowText` honesty: `Forecast rain · none on radar over the site now`, `… · not a map`, `… where radar shows it · amount: MET Norway forecast`.
- `cloud shapes illustrative, kept clear of the site`; `drawn still (Reduce motion)`.
- `further out, less certain` beyond +24 h. The forecast is never stretched past its last step (`stepAt` returns null).
- Lightning stays static and is not drawn ahead of now; extend this to the past (K1).
- The timeline is a native `<input type=range>` with `aria-valuetext` giving time, offset and kind. The caption has `role=status`.
- The weather sub-group sits under Hazards with an "on" count. All five rows are off by default.
- The credits line updates for each active source, including the frame source on the timeline.
- Switching Live ↔ Advance resets the weather time. Advance uses the itinerary as its time axis, not the timeline; say so (K10).
- `SIMULATED` on rows, `· SIMULATED DATA` on the mode chip, `Prototype · SIM ON` on the pill.
- Frames are loaded lazily and cached; missing frames are shown as missing, never interpolated.
