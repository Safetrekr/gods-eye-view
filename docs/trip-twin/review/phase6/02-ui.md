# Phase 6 — UI / visual review (weather in 3D + weather timeline)

Reviewer: UI. Review only, no code changed. Sources: shots in `review/phase6/shots/` (1600 and 1280), `twinWeatherFx.js` (KIND table, clouds, haze), `twinWeatherTimeline.js`, `tripTwin.css` phase 6 block (the second `:root` overrides the `--tt-wx-*` values; those are the values judged here).

How it was measured: pixel samples and crops of the shots (PIL); WCAG contrast computed against the timeline panel fill as rendered, (7,33,43), which is about `--tt-glass`.

## Verdict on the 3D weather

- **Rain at Building scale (02) works.** The thin grey streaks read as rain straight away and don't hide pins, floor labels or the path.
- **Snow at Neighborhood scale (01, 04) looks like scattered data dots** more than snow, especially when still.
- **Snow at City scale (03) is a blocking problem.** The flakes cover the site disc: the hotel hologram is lost and the path is broken up.
- **Clouds don't clear the site.** They avoid a vertical column over it, but the camera looks at an angle, so clouds 1 km up land on top of the disc on screen. They also float in empty dark space, which makes them look like decoration.
- **The haze has a hard edge.** It looks like a zone or geofence ellipse rather than haze.

## P0

### P0-1 · Snow and rain at City scale cover the site
- **Shots:** 1600-03, 1280-03 (snow, clouds and haze at City).
- **Problem:**
  - ParticleSystem `imageSize` is in screen pixels and `emissionRate` doesn't depend on the camera. As the disc gets smaller on screen, the flakes pack together.
  - Measured share of near-white pixels in the site disc: 16 % at 1600 and 21 % at 1280, against 5 % for the same view with no snow (1600-05).
  - The hotel hologram disappears. The blue path is broken up by flakes. The pins survive only because they ignore depth.
  - This breaks the rule that weather must not hide the site, people or paths.
- **Spec:**
  - Scale `emissionRate` (and the still-billboard count `n`) by view preset or camera height: Building 1.0×, Neighborhood 0.5×, City 0.12×.
  - Target at any preset: no more than 5 % of disc pixels covered by precipitation.
  - Above the City camera height, cap live particles at 150.
  - Rain at City: `imageSize` 1 × 12 px (now 1.5 × 20).
  - Re-check with a crop of the disc at both sizes after the fix.

### P0-2 · The US timeline track is blank, but the legend lists three data kinds
- **Shots:** 1600-05, 1600-06, 1600-07, and 1280-05/06/07 (Austin).
- **Problem:**
  - Every pixel inside the Austin track is (7,33,43), the same as the panel fill, from `NOW` to `+48 h`. The London track (08, 11) does render.
  - The legend still shows "observed / model forecast map / site forecast", so it describes shading that isn't there.
  - Likely cause: one bad colour stop invalidates the whole `linear-gradient`, and the declaration then falls back to `unset`. The `var(--wxtl-bg, …)` fallback only covers an *undefined* variable, not an *invalid* one.
  - Two candidates for the bad stop:
    - `NaN%`: the HRRR `until` returns `null` when `meta.run` isn't loaded yet.
    - A `kind` that is missing from `KIND_COLOR`.
- **Spec:**
  - In `render()`, drop segments where `from` or `to` isn't finite, or where `to <= from`.
  - Clamp `a` and `b` to 0–100 and round them to 2 decimals.
  - Use `KIND_COLOR[s.kind] ?? 'transparent'`.
  - Give `.tt-wxtl-track::before` a base `background-color: var(--tt-muted)` with the gradient as a separate `background-image`, so the bar is never invisible.
  - Legend: list only kinds that have a drawn segment at least 2 px wide.

## P1

### P1-3 · Data-kind shading fails 3:1 and relies on lightness alone
- **Shots:** 1600-08, 1600-11, 1280-08, 1280-11 (where the track renders).
- **Problem:** contrast against the panel fill and between neighbouring kinds:

  | Pair | Contrast | Needed |
  |---|---|---|
  | Site forecast vs panel fill | 1.77:1 | 3:1 |
  | Model vs panel fill | 3.31:1 | 3:1 (passes, just) |
  | Model vs site forecast (adjacent) | 1.87:1 | 3:1 |
  | Observed vs model (adjacent) | 2.34:1 | 3:1 |
  | Amber away-thumb vs observed fill | 1.0:1 | 3:1 |

  - The amber thumb is visible only because of the WebKit `box-shadow` ring.
  - Firefox gets no ring and no amber away state: `::-moz-range-thumb` has `border: 0` and there is no `.away` rule for it.
  - Once you leave now, nothing on the track marks where now is; only the `NOW` tick label does.
- **Spec:** use pattern plus lightness rather than three greys.
  - **Observed:** solid `var(--tt-text-2)` (9.25:1 against the panel).
  - **Model forecast map:** `repeating-linear-gradient(135deg, var(--tt-edge-ctl) 0 2px, transparent 2px 5px)` (stripes 5.3:1).
  - **Site forecast:** hollow, with no fill and `box-shadow: inset 0 1px var(--tt-edge-ctl), inset 0 -1px var(--tt-edge-ctl)` (5.3:1). Hollow reads as "less certain".
  - **No data:** panel fill with a 1px dashed `var(--tt-border)` top edge.
  - Retire the `--tt-wx-*` rgba tokens, or redefine them as these patterns. Legend swatches go from 14 × 6 to 14 × 8 and use the same patterns.
  - **Thumb:** 6 × 24 px, radius 2, with a 2px `var(--tt-field)` ring in both engines. For Firefox use `border: 2px solid var(--tt-field)` plus a `.tt-wxtl.away .tt-wxtl-range::-moz-range-thumb { background: var(--tt-attention) }` rule.
  - **Now marker:** a fixed 2 × 16 px `var(--tt-text)` mark at the NOW position on the track, always drawn.

### P1-4 · The timeline covers the compass caption at both sizes
- **Shots:** 1600-05/06/07/08/11 (caption reads "viewing from S"); 1280-06/08/11 (caption reads "g from S · looking N").
- **Problem:**
  - `.tt-wxtl` sets `right: calc(var(--rcol) + 2*var(--g) + 110px)`.
  - `.tt-compass-caption` is `nowrap`, right-aligned to the compass, and about 140 px wide (measured: 1087 → 1226 at 1600). It reaches about 30 px under the bar.
  - The bar's `z-index` is 12 and the compass's is 9, so the bar wins.
- **Spec:**
  - Set `right: calc(var(--rcol) + 3 * var(--g) + 150px)`.
  - Win back the width by moving Back to now onto the caption row:

    ```css
    grid-template-columns: auto 1fr;
    grid-template-areas: 'play track' 'cap now' 'legend legend';
    ```

  - Net effect at 1280: the track goes from about 329 px to about 390 px, and nothing overlaps.

### P1-5 · Ticks collide at 1280, and the past window is about 13 px wide
- **Shots:** 1280-06 ("NOW3:00"), 1280-08/11 ("NOW9:00"); 1600-05 is tight ("NOW 13:00", about 10 px apart).
- **Problem:**
  - Ticks near now are skipped by time (within 2 h), not by pixels. A label is about 30 px wide (10px mono), and 2 h is about 13 px at 1280.
  - The same 13 px also holds the whole observed window: 24 radar steps of 5 min. You can't scrub that precisely.
  - The hours that matter most (−2 h to +6 h) take about 16 % of the track.
- **Spec:**
  - Skip a tick when `|x_tick − x_now| < 36px`, measured from `track.clientWidth`.
  - Make the scale piecewise: −2 h to +18 h across the first 65 % of the track, +18 h to +48 h across the remaining 35 %, with a 1px `var(--tt-border)` divider at the break.
  - Ticks every 3 h in the first part and every 12 h after.
  - Under this scale the past window is about 35 px at 1280.
  - `aria-valuetext` stays in real time, so nothing changes for screen readers.

### P1-6 · Clouds land on top of the site on screen and float in empty space
- **Shots:**
  - 1600-05/07: clouds at about (870,440) and (900,540) sit on the disc.
  - 1280-03: two clouds on the east edge of the disc.
  - 1600-03/05/07: a cloud behind the "+3 more" and Embassy chips; a cloud behind the "Long Center · Day 2 19:00" label.
  - 1280-03: a cloud behind the compass.
- **Problem:**
  - The clear column is vertical (`d ≥ R·1.3`). The camera is oblique, so a low-layer cloud south of the site at 1 km height lands on the disc on screen.
  - `d` goes up to R·4.5, out past the rendered ground, so the clouds hang over nothing.
  - `brightness` is 0.85 (low) and 1.0 (high), which makes the clouds the brightest objects after the hologram. They pull the eye, so they read as decoration.
- **Spec:**
  - Clear cone instead of column: `d ≥ R·1.3 + altitude` (45°), and `d ≤ R·3`.
  - On `camera.changed` (`percentageChanged = 0.05`), set `cloud.show = false` for any cloud whose projected centre falls inside the site disc's screen box plus 40 px.
  - `brightness` 0.55 / 0.6 / 0.7 for the low / mid / high layers, `color` = `#b8c3c7` (`--tt-text-2`).
  - At most 10 clouds visible.

### P1-7 · The haze looks like a zone boundary
- **Shot:** 1600-03, 1280-03.
- **Problem:**
  - The haze is one cylinder (R·1.6, 60 m high, flat top). Its fill measures (22,41,49) against (6,26,35) outside, with a sharp elliptical edge.
  - That is the same look as the site-radius and group-zone ellipses, so operators could read it as a boundary.
- **Spec:**
  - Replace it with a ground ellipse with no extrusion: semi-axes R·2.0.
  - Material: `ImageMaterialProperty` with a 256 px radial-gradient canvas in `#dfe6e8`.
  - Alpha: centre `0.14·k`, 60 % radius `0.08·k`, edge 0, where `k = min(1, fog/30)`.
  - No outline and no visible edge at any preset.

### P1-8 · The weather chip in the top bar breaks in away and simulated states at 1280
- **Shots:** 1280-11, 1280-06, 1280-03, 1600-11.
- **Problem:**
  - **1280-11:** the PAST chip falls back to plain text ("PAST 14:30 · map frames only (the site forecast starts now)"). It loses the boxed badge and the temperature, and the text runs under the "Prototype ▾" button.
  - **Other states at 1280:** text is cut mid-word ("68 °F mostly cle… forecast 13:0…", "SIMULAT…").
  - The badge layout used in 06/07 ([FCST] / [PAST]) is not kept in all states.
- **Spec:**
  - The chip is always: badge + temperature + condition.
    - Badge: mono 10px/600, 1px border, padding 1px 4px. Amber for FCST/PAST; amber dashed for SIM, as it is now.
  - The secondary text ("forecast 13:00 · MET Norway", "map frames only…") moves to the chip's `title` and into the timeline caption below 1440 px.
  - When there is no site forecast: "[PAST] 14:30 · map only".
  - Container: `min-width: 0; max-width: 280px; overflow: hidden`, with an ellipsis on the condition only, so the chip never runs into the Prototype button.

## P2

### P2-9 · Too much amber in the away caption
- **Shots:** 1600-06/08/11, 1280-06/08/11.
- **Problem:**
  - The whole caption, about 150 characters over 2 lines, is `--tt-attention`. It competes with the real attention items (Not current 4, the Jay Patel bar).
  - At 1280 the panel is 112 px tall, about 15 % of the viewport height.
- **Spec:**
  - Only the lead stays `var(--tt-attention)`, weight 600: "VIEWING FORECAST 13:00 (+3 h) — not live".
  - The rest (source · run · "travelers stay at their current positions") goes in `var(--tt-text-2)`.
  - Clamp to 2 lines (`-webkit-line-clamp: 2`), with the full text in `title`.
  - Keep the amber 1px border, the 3px inset bar and the amber thumb.

### P2-10 · Flakes look like data points, in the same white as the UI
- **Shots:** 1600-01, 1600-04 (still), 1280-01.
- **Problem:**
  - KIND.snow is a uniform 5 × 5 px dot, `#eff7f3` at 0.9 alpha. That colour is exactly `--tt-text`, the white used for hologram edges and pin rings.
  - Evenly spread, same-size dots read as point markers or stars, especially in reduce-motion. Flakes also sit on pin rings (AK, SD in the 01 crop).
- **Spec:**
  - Snow: colour `#b8c3c7` (`--tt-text-2`), alpha 0.6.
  - Size varies from 3 to 7 px (`minimumImageSize` (3,3) / `maximumImageSize` (7,7)). For the still billboards, randomise `width`/`height` 3–7 and alpha 0.35–0.7 per flake.
  - Softer flake texture: gradient stops 1.0 → 0.5 at 40 % → 0.
  - Rain: `#b8c3c7` at 0.45. The look stays the same, but it now shares the snow token.

## Keep

- **Rain streaks at Building scale (02, 1280-02):** neutral grey, thin, alpha about 0.5. They read as rain instantly and leave the pins, floor labels and path readable.
- **Neutral white-grey for all weather:** no blue (blue stays for paths), no green, no red. Amber appears only for not-live.
- **The set of away cues:** amber 1px border, 3px inset bar, amber thumb, FCST/PAST badge, and "VIEWING FORECAST / PAST" in the mode chip. Together they tell the operator clearly that this is not live.
- **Back to now appears only when away.** Play/Pause uses `aria-pressed`. The native range input with `aria-valuetext` ("13:00 +3 h · model forecast map") stays.
- **Caption content:** it names the source, the model run or frame time, "not live", and "travelers stay at their current positions". Change only how it is coloured (P2-9), not what it says.
- **Reduce motion (04):** the same particles drawn still, not removed.
- **Keeping the sky over the site clear:** keep the idea, just widen it to a cone (P1-6).
- **London track (08, 11):** the observed / gap / model sequence reads, and the satellite gap shows as empty rather than filled in.
- **Layers rows (10):** "Rain & snow (3D)" and "Clouds & haze (3D, forecast)" name the source and "SIMULATED", and say the cloud shapes are illustrative and kept clear of the site.
- **Advance (09):** reuses the chip ("FCST · Day 2 19:00 · MET Norway") instead of adding a second time bar.
