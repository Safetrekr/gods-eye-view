# Phase 4 review: UX (custom paths)

Reviewer lens: can an operator plan a path around a hazard quickly and confidently? I looked at discoverability, what the editor does to other clicks and keys, waypoints in a 3D scene, the avoid-area flow, what happens when the router fails, finding saved paths later, what "reviewed" means, fit at 1280×720, keyboard use, and copy.
Evidence: `shots/1600-*` and `1280-*` (11 states each), `1600-text.json`, and code: `twinPathEditor.js`, `twinCustomPaths.js`, `twinCustomPathsLayer.js`, `page.js`, `twinHud.js`, `server/tripTwin/routingProxy.js`. I don't reopen recorded decisions: vocabulary is "path", "suggested · unverified", the colours, and "the camera never moves by itself".

## Verdict

**Not yet confident. Three P0s before the fix list closes.** With a mouse, the basic flow works and reads well. You open the editor from a card, click to add a waypoint, the router re-asks, you save, and the path shows up in the Day N brief. The framing is mostly honest ("Suggested · unverified", "No checks raised (not a verification of the path)").

But the feature's main promise, "plan around a hazard", breaks in three places:
1. A path that goes through an avoid-area can be saved with one Enter, and it then reads "avoids construction".
2. The trip-wide avoid-area says it applies to "every path on this trip", but the suggested paths (PATH HERE, rally path, advance walking path) are never checked against it.
3. While the editor is open, keyboard use is unsafe:
   - Enter on a focused button saves the path instead of pressing the button.
   - The panel is rebuilt after every router reply, which drops focus.
   - With focus dropped, the next keys act as editor shortcuts: Backspace deletes a waypoint and A arms an avoid-area.

Waypoint placement in 3D is also ambiguous: the click lands on the ground plane behind what you clicked, the marker number isn't drawn, and the list shows raw coordinates.

## Findings

### P0

**1. A path through an avoid-area is saved and then labelled "avoids construction".**
- Evidence:
  - `1600-05`: Valhalla refuses and the OSRM fallback goes straight through the Construction disc. It is flagged "Enters 1 avoid-area".
  - `1600-07` and the saved card text read: "1.9 km · walk 30–39 min · **avoids construction · ENTERS 1 avoid-area** · draft · not reviewed".
  - `1600-10`: the Long Center path runs west and never comes near the northern disc, but still reads "avoids construction".
  - Cause: `savedPathLines` (`twinCustomPaths.js:256`) builds "avoids …" from `areaIds`, which is every trip area that existed at save time. It doesn't use what the router actually did.
- Problem: "avoids" is a claim of verified avoidance. It's the same class of word as "safe", and here it contradicts the flag next to it. The same line goes into the copied Day N brief (`twinAdvance.js:379`).
- Also: Save accepts a crossing path with a single Enter. Once saved, the map draws it as a plain blue long-dash with no amber, so the map alone shows nothing (compare `1600-07` with `1600-05`).
- Fix:
  - Drop "avoids …" entirely.
  - Say "Routed around: Construction (router)" only when the engine is Valhalla, the area was sent to it, and no crossing is found. Otherwise say "Enters avoid-area: Construction".
  - Keep "suggested · unverified" on every line.
  - When a crossing or "a stop is inside an avoid-area" flag is present, Save becomes "Save anyway — enters Construction" and needs a second click (same pattern as Delete). Enter alone does not save it.

**2. Avoid-areas say they apply to "every path on this trip", but suggested paths are never checked.**
- Evidence:
  - Layers legend in `1600-08`: "Avoid-area · applies to every path on this trip".
  - Avoid-area card (`page.js:1802–1808`): "Applies to every path planned on this trip".
  - `avoidCrossings` is called only from `pathChecks` and `savedPathLines` (both in `twinCustomPaths.js`). None of these run it: PATH HERE / `runRoute`, the standing rally path, or Advance "Walking path from Fairmont Austin".
  - Result: the hospital PATH HERE walk from Fairmont Austin takes the same OSRM shape as `1600-05`, goes through the disc, and gets no flag.
- Problem: the operator marks a hazard once and reasonably expects every line on screen to respect it. The paths most likely to be used live (rally, PATH HERE) are exactly the unchecked ones.
- Fix:
  - Run `avoidCrossings(geometry, pathStore.areas)` on every drawn path: rally, PATH HERE, advance, saved.
  - Flag it in that path's card and brief line: "Enters avoid-area: Construction — re-plan with Edit this path".
  - Draw the crossing segment in amber (see #12).
  - If that can't land now, change the copy to "applies to paths you plan or save". Don't leave the wider promise in place.

**3. Keyboard handling in the editor is unsafe and blocks keyboard-only use.**
- Evidence:
  - `handleKey` (`twinPathEditor.js:423–448`) returns `true` for every key unless focus is in an input, select or textarea. `page.js:2464` then calls `preventDefault()`.
  - Tab, Space and Enter on buttons are therefore swallowed. Walk/Drive, ↑ ↓ ×, "Add avoid-area (A)" and "Cancel (Esc)" can't be pressed from the keyboard.
  - Enter on any focused button runs `saveEdit()`. Enter on "Add avoid-area" saves the whole path and the area is never added. Enter on "Cancel (Esc)" saves.
  - `render()` replaces the whole panel (`p.replaceChildren`, line 270) on `loading` and on `ready` after every reroute, so focus inside the name or notes field drops to `body`. After that, typed letters are swallowed, **A** arms avoid-area placement, **Backspace deletes the last waypoint**, and **Enter saves**.
  - While the avoid-area form is open, each re-render resets Size, Reason and Note to the defaults, and `r.focus()` (line 328) pulls focus back to Size.
  - An avoid-area can only be placed by clicking the map; there is no keyboard way.
- Problem: a common sequence silently corrupts the plan: click to add a waypoint, start typing a name, the router answers. This fails WCAG 2.1.1 (Keyboard) and repeats the phase-3 G7 problem ("panel rebuilds only when content changes; scroll and focus kept").
- Fix:
  - Shortcuts only fire when `e.target` is the map canvas or `body` and focus wasn't just lost to a re-render. Exclude `button` from Enter/Space handling, and never `preventDefault` Tab.
  - Update the result block in place (keyed nodes) instead of rebuilding the panel; keep input values, focus and scroll.
  - Keep the avoid-area form state in `arming` (`{center, radiusM, reason, note}`).
  - Backspace removes a waypoint only when the map has focus.
  - Add a keyboard way to place an area: "Centre on: waypoint 1 · Start · End · map centre".

### P1

**4. When the router can't avoid an area, the message guesses the cause and the editor offers no way out.**
- Evidence:
  - `1600-05`: "Valhalla: no path avoids every area — the areas block all ways through; move or shrink one".
  - `requestPath` maps any Valhalla "No path could be found" to that sentence (`twinCustomPaths.js:131`), even when the real cause is a through-waypoint that can't be reached.
  - There is no move or shrink control anywhere; the only option is × and re-add.
  - The fallback quietly substitutes a path that goes through the area, and the whole line turns amber.
  - "Valhalla (FOSSGIS)" and "OSRM fallback" are engine jargon.
- Fix:
  - New copy: "No path found that avoids Construction with these waypoints."
  - Offer three operator actions in the result block: [Remove waypoint 1] [Shrink Construction to 50 m] [Show the path anyway (enters the area)].
  - The fallback path is drawn only after "Show anyway".
  - Plain engine words: "Router: main — avoid-areas honoured" and "Backup router — can't avoid areas; checked instead". Keep the engine name in a tooltip.

**5. Waypoints in a 3D scene: placed behind what you clicked, unnumbered, and listed as coordinates.**
- Evidence:
  - `groundAt` (`tripTwin.js:155`) intersects the click ray with the ground plane. Clicking a roof, a tower face or a raised pin puts the waypoint on the ground far behind it.
  - `1600-03`: the waypoint is drawn on the tower's face, but its coordinates are on the ground north of the hotel.
  - A zoom crop of `1600-03` shows the marker with **no number**. The label has no background and sits under the 14 px point.
  - The path doesn't visibly pass through the marker: the router snapped it, and nothing shows that.
  - The list reads "1 · 30.26356, -97.73866" (`1600-03`).
  - The remove target is 14 px.
  - Any map click becomes a waypoint, including clicks on a person, a place, an avoid-area or a saved path (`tripTwin.js:486`).
- Fix:
  - While editing, show a ground ghost ring with a vertical drop line under the cursor.
  - After routing, draw a thin tether from each waypoint to its snapped point, with a note like "snapped 35 m to Congress Ave".
  - Number on a plate drawn in front of the point.
  - Hit area ≥ 28 px.
  - List items read "1 · near 3rd St & Congress Ave · 240 m along the path"; hover or focus highlights the marker.
  - Add "Undo last change" to the banner and panel.

**6. You can't see the whole path while editing or reviewing it.**
- Evidence:
  - In every editor shot (`02`–`06`) the destination is 1.7 km north, off-screen, and the "End ·" marker never appears.
  - The editor and the saved card have no "Frame path" (`twinPathEditor.js` render; `page.js:1709` actions).
  - The saved label "Saved · name · draft" sits at the geometry midpoint and is off-screen in `07` and `10`.
- Fix: add "Frame path (F)" to the editor and the saved card. It's an operator action, the same as phase-3 G11, so it keeps the "camera never moves by itself" rule. Anchor the saved label near the start, where the operator usually looks.

**7. Modality leaks: lost work, mode switching, and live supervision while editing.**
- Evidence:
  - Esc discards everything with no confirm, even from inside the name field (Esc is checked before the input test).
  - `switchSite` (`page.js:882`) and Exit (`page.js:2612`) cancel silently.
  - `setAppMode` (`page.js:1559`) never touches the editor. Clicking Advance mid-edit leaves a Live edit (Day "Any day") open over Advance, which breaks phase-3 G11 ("path cleared on Live/Advance switch").
  - The incident panel follows the editor in the right column (`twin.html:108–119`), so a new report lands **below** the editor and shares its height.
  - People and places can't be selected on the map while editing. Their clicks become waypoints, and `#tt-card` is hidden.
- Fix:
  - Unsaved-change guard: "Discard this path? Esc again / Keep editing". The same guard applies on site switch, mode switch and Exit.
  - On a mode switch, keep the editor and set Day to the Advance stop's day, with a one-line notice.
  - Put `#tt-incident` above `#tt-editor`. When an incident is raised, collapse the editor to a one-line "Editing path · Resume" bar.
  - Banner adds "other shortcuts paused".

**8. The avoid-area flow is blind and has trip-wide side effects.**
- Evidence:
  - `1600-04`: after the centre click, nothing is drawn on the map while you choose 50/100/200 m. The disc appears only after "Add avoid-area".
  - The centre shows as raw coordinates.
  - No move or resize after adding.
  - × in the editor list removes a **trip-wide** area with no confirm (`twinPathEditor.js:338`), while the avoid-area card asks once (`page.js:1781`).
  - Adding an area persists immediately (`deps.persist()`), so Cancel (Esc) on the path does not undo it, and the panel doesn't say so.
  - The card's "used by N saved paths" counts every path saved after the area existed, not the paths it actually shaped.
- Fix:
  - Live preview disc while the form is open; it follows the Size select.
  - Centre as "near <street>".
  - Drag-to-move and size buttons on the area card.
  - × asks once ("Remove for the whole trip — click again").
  - A note under the list: "Avoid-areas are saved for the whole trip as soon as you add them."
  - Count only paths routed with the area applied.
  - Optional "Days" on an area (construction and crowds are usually dated).

**9. "Reviewed" is one click, ignores the flags, and survives changes that matter.**
- Evidence:
  - `1600-10` → `1600-11`: one click turns the kicker into "SAVED PATH · REVIEWED" while "Walked after dark at the planned time" is still flagged.
  - An edit clears the review (`twinPathEditor.js:377`, good). Adding or removing avoid-areas, or a new heat detection, changes the path's flags but keeps "reviewed".
  - In a kicker, "REVIEWED" with no qualifier reads like approval (06 §10: "a review is a person's check, not an approval").
- Fix:
  - Store the flag keys seen at review. Button: "Mark reviewed (1 flag: after dark)". Kicker: "REVIEWED BY YOU · 1 FLAG".
  - When the current flags differ from the reviewed set, show "Changed since review — review again" and drop the status to draft in the brief.

**10. The "after dark" check is wrong in both directions.**
- Evidence:
  - `isDark(day)` (`page.js:1614`) returns false when Day is "Any day", which is the default in Live. A walking path drawn at 21:32 local, after sunset, gets no dark flag (`1600-02`/`03`).
  - With a day set, it flags if **any** stop that day ends after dark, whatever time the walk happens.
  - The copy says "at the planned time", but a saved path has no time.
- Fix:
  - Add "Leave at" (default: the event start in Advance, now in Live).
  - Flag when leave time + walk minutes passes sunset: "Walked after dark — sunset 18:58, arrives ~19:35".
  - Day defaults to today's trip day in Live.

**11. Saved paths are hard to find later by day or by site.**
- Evidence:
  - Layers › Paths has one "Saved paths (operator)" row (`1600-08`). The plan's per-day rows ("Saved paths · Day N") aren't there.
  - Live draws every day's saved paths at once (`renderCustom`, `day == null`).
  - The only index is the Advance brief, per day and per site (`1600-10` text: "SAVED PATHS · DAY 2 · 1"), and it's below the fold at both widths.
  - A path whose ends aren't within 300 m of a site, such as a person-origin path to a hospital, gets `siteKeys: []` and appears in **no** brief. It can only be found by clicking its line.
  - A person-origin path keeps that person's name after they move.
- Fix:
  - Under Layers › Paths, add a "Saved paths" list grouped by Day (count per day, Any day last). Each row opens the card; there's a per-day show toggle.
  - Live shows today's paths plus Any day by default ("other days hidden · 2").
  - Name person-origin paths "from Jay Patel's position at 21:32" and offer "Start from a site instead".

**12. Saved and suggested paths look nearly the same, and the warning colour hides where the problem is.**
- Evidence:
  - `1600-10`: the saved long-dash (#93bafa) and the standing short-dash path run along the same street west of the hotel. They can't be told apart.
  - While editing, a warning turns the **whole** line amber (`twinCustomPathsLayer.js:100`, `1600-05`/`06`). That's the same amber as the disc and the heat-proximity legend, and it doesn't show which segment crosses.
  - After saving, the amber is gone.
  - Other suggested paths stay at full strength during editing (`1600-09`: two solid blue lines leave the hotel).
- Fix:
  - Paths stay blue. Overlay amber ticks or hatching only on the crossing or near-heat segments of any path (edit, saved or suggested), plus a "!" label at the first crossing.
  - Saved paths get a second cue besides dash length: an end cap with "S" and the start-anchored label.
  - Dim all other paths to about 35% while editing.

### P2

**13. Copy clarity.**
- The PATH section shows "Draw your own path" above "Pick where from, then Walk or Drive. Nothing is routed until you choose" (`1600-01`), but Draw routes immediately. Change the note to "Draw your own path starts from the selected origin and asks the router at once."
- "avoid-areas applied" appears when the trip has 0 areas (`1600-02`). Say it only when at least one area was sent.
- The saved card repeats itself: "draft · not reviewed" appears in both kicker and body, and "reviewed by you · Fri, 00:01 CDT" twice (`1600-11`).
- Time stamps use the wall clock ("Fri, 00:00 CDT") while the bar reads "21:32 local · sample". Use the sample clock in the sample trip.
- The name input cuts off at both widths ("…Ascension Dell Seton Me"). Use a 2-line textarea, or show the full name as the title under the field.
- The brief still says "From the hotel · route not checked" when a saved path for that pair exists (`1600-10`). Point to it: "see saved path below".

**14. Fit at 1280×720.**
- The banner wraps to two lines and splits "A / avoid-area" (`1280-02`, `05`, `09`). It also covers the fire-station and rally labels at 1600 (`1600-02`).
- The saved card's Delete button is clipped at the right edge (`1280-08`, `10`, `11`).
- "Plan your own path" is half under the sticky Copy footer (`1280-09`, `10`).
- The avoid-area form pushes Save below the fold (`1280-04`).
- Fix:
  - Below 1440, use a shorter banner: "EDITING PATH · click: add · click waypoint: remove · A area · Enter save · Esc cancel".
  - Saved actions in a 2×2 grid.
  - Scroll the focused advance button clear of the sticky footer.
  - Make the editor's Save / Cancel row sticky at the bottom of the panel.

**15. Errors and edge states.**
- When both routers fail you get "Path unavailable (…) — change the waypoints or try again". There's no Retry button and Save is disabled, so the whole plan (waypoints, notes, day) can't be kept while the routers are down.
- During every reroute, "Asking the router…" replaces the previous result, so the panel jumps.
- After 10 waypoints, more clicks are ignored without any message (`twinPathEditor.js:356`).
- There is no general "New path" from a map point or to an arbitrary point (the plan's site-card entry). Entry points are only place/person cards and venue stops in Advance.
- Fix:
  - Keep the last result dimmed with "updating…".
  - Add a Retry button.
  - "Save waypoints only (not routed, not checked)", stored as draft and flagged in the brief.
  - A cap note: "10 waypoints is the limit — remove one to add another".
  - "New path" in the site card.

## What works — keep

- **The honest framing:** "Suggested · unverified · …", "No checks raised (not a verification of the path)", "Paths stay suggested and unverified until someone reviews them", draft as the default, and "Draft for review" in the brief. Keep the same voice in every new line.
- **The fallback is disclosed:** the engine, "avoid-areas checked, not applied", and the crossing are named in amber in the editor. Fix the cause wording (#4), keep the disclosure.
- **The client-side crossing check always runs,** densified to 8 m, and it is re-run against current areas whenever a brief line or saved card is drawn. That's the right base for #2.
- **"A stop is inside an avoid-area" is handled up front:** those areas are excluded from the router request and named as a flag, rather than causing an unexplained routing failure.
- **The banner states the editing mode in one line, and the crosshair cursor reinforces it.** The editor replacing the selection card in the same column keeps the layout stable (no new panel location to learn).
- **Entry points sit where the question is asked:** "Edit this path" on a shown path, "Draw your own path" in PATH HERE, "Plan your own path" in the stop brief, with Day prefilled from the Advance stop (`1600-09`: "Day 2" and the name "Fairmont Austin → Long Center · Day 2 · walk").
- **An edit clears the review;** Delete and Remove avoid-area use a click-again confirm.
- **Saved paths flow into the Day N brief and the copied draft,** each with its review status, and clicking a brief line opens the saved card.
- **The camera never moves by itself in the editor,** and the router is asked at most once per change (debounced, one request in flight).
- **Vocabulary follows 06 §10:** "path" throughout, "Walk / Drive", "avoid-area". There's no "route" in operator copy except the brief's existing "route not checked", which is worth a sweep.
