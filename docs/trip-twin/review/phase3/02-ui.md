# Phase 3 · UI / visual review

Reviewer: factory-ui-designer · 15 Oct 2026 · review only, no code touched.

**Inputs**
- `00-CONTEXT.md` (COLOR AND BRAND), `PHASE-3-PLAN.md`
- `review/phase2/02-ui.md` and `review/phase2/00-FIX-LIST.md`
- `tripTwin.css` (phase-3 sections, lines 1293–1618), `twin.html`, `twinCompass.js`, `twinSiteBeacons.js`, `twinNumbering.js`
- Screenshots: 18 states × 1600×900 and 1280×720 in `shots/`, plus `1600-text.json` and `1280-text.json`

**How contrast was measured.** Ratios are WCAG 2.x. They are computed from the CSS tokens, blended over `--tt-card #07212c` or the field `#061a23`.

## Verdict

The phase-3 parts are built in the established visual language:
- dark brand field, Inter text and mono numerals;
- blue only on paths, and green only on approved places;
- no red or green on people.

The site switcher, beacons, compass, route section and Advance panel all read as one product.

Two things fail at the target sizes:

1. **The top bar has run out of room.** The site switcher, the main new control, is clipped mid-word at 1600 in Advance ("Fairr", no ▾). At 1280 in Advance it is gone entirely ("Spr"). The focus crumb is "…" at both widths.
2. **City-scale framing ignores the safe area.** Beacon labels and route destinations sit under the rail, the right column or the top bar. The "trip view" therefore doesn't show the trip.

A third issue sits outside my lane but is visible in the shots: the London numbering answer produces a false zero (P0-2).

Also fix these:
- The Advance top bar contradicts itself (`LIVE 81 °F` next to a 62 °F planning forecast).
- A live incident during Advance shows only inside the panel.
- Fixed-height buttons overflow at 1280.
- The compass needle points at the look direction, not at north.
- The label collisions that Part F was meant to fix are still there.

No flashing. One motion glitch: the compass dial spins a full turn when the heading crosses north (P1-7).

### P0-1 · Top bar: the site switcher is clipped or disappears; the focus crumb is always "…"
**Evidence**

| Shot | What the bar shows |
|---|---|
| 1600-10 to -14 (Advance) | `World View › Spri… › Fairr`: the name is cut mid-glyph and the ▾ is gone |
| 1280-10, -11, -14 (Advance) | `TRIP TWIN  Spr`: no site name, no switcher |
| 1280-06, -07, -09 (Live) | `Fairmont Aust`, no ▾ |
| 1280-15, -16 | `Park Plaza London Wa` |
| 1600-06 to -09, 1600-18 | focus crumb is `As…` / `…`; the text JSON says it should be `› Jay Patel` or `› Floor 1` |

Every other bar item is `flex-shrink: 0; white-space: nowrap` (lines 1436–1439). Only `.tt-crumbs` can shrink, and it clips with `overflow: hidden`, so the site button is cut by its parent. The fixed-width items are:
- the mono age and clock, "Traveler data 11s old 19:40 local · sample", about 315 px;
- in Advance, the mono sentence "No live positions in this view", about 220 px;
- the redundant `TRIP TWIN` kicker, about 85 px. The Exit button already names the view.

**Problem:** The site switcher is the only on-screen way to change sites besides `S`, and the site name is the answer to "which place am I looking at". At 1280 both are lost exactly when the operator is planning a site.

**Fix: free about 300 px, then protect the switcher.**
- **Copy changes.**
  - `Traveler data 11s old` becomes `Data` in Inter plus `11s` in mono.
  - `19:40 local · sample` becomes `19:40` in mono, with `local time · sample trip` in `title`. "Sample" is already in the footer and in the Prototype pill.
  - In Advance, drop "No live positions in this view" from the bar. The chip and the panel title already say it. Put it in the chip's `title`.
- **Below 1600, hide the kicker:**
  ```css
  @media (max-width: 1599px) { .tt-bar > .tt-kicker { display: none; } }
  ```
- **The switcher never shrinks, and the ▾ survives truncation:**
  ```css
  .tt-bar > .tt-crumbs { flex: 0 1 auto; min-width: 200px; }
  .tt-crumbs .tt-bar-hotel { flex: 0 0 auto; display: inline-flex; align-items: baseline; gap: 4px; max-width: 240px; }
  .tt-crumbs .tt-bar-hotel .name { min-width: 0; overflow: hidden; text-overflow: ellipsis; }
  .tt-crumbs .tt-bar-hotel::after { content: '▾'; flex: none; }   /* remove ▾ from the text node */
  .tt-crumbs .tt-crumb-trip { flex: 0 1 auto; min-width: 0; }     /* the trip name truncates first */
  .tt-crumb-focus { flex: 0 1 auto; min-width: 56px; }
  ```
- **Live / Advance:** raise to `font: 500 12px/26px` with `padding: 0 12px`, a 28 px target that matches the other bar controls (today it is 22 px).

Expected at 1280: `Spring choir… › Fairmont Austin ▾ › Jay Patel`, all readable.

### P0-2 · London: the operator's numbering answer is applied off by one, giving a false zero (cross-lens; route to UX and supervision)
**Evidence (1600-16, 1280-16)**
- The card says `ground floor = 0 · 9 floors above ground`. With ground = 0 and 9 floors above ground, the top floor is 9.
- The cutaway's top label is **8**, and there is no bar for floor 9, the reported floor (zoomed crop: bars 8, 4, 3, 2, 1, 0; 5–7 quiet).
- The card footer contradicts both: `? floors · ground floor = 0`.
- The incident hero reads `0 may be at or above floor 9`. The rail top line reads `no one at or above by phone estimate or room list`.

**Problem:** This is the false zero that F2 was written to prevent, now caused by the prompt meant to remove it. Visually, the reported floor has no bar, so the 2D read shows nothing at the incident.

**Fix**
- Ask for the unambiguous number. Relabel the input from `Floors above ground (optional)` to `Top floor number [ ] (optional)`. It means the same thing in UK and US numbering.
- Draw floors from the ground label up to that number.
- If the reported floor is above the top floor (or the top is unknown), keep the F2 guard. Do not show a count. The hero should read: `Reported floor 9 is above the top floor entered (8) — check the numbering` (pink text, `Change` link next to it).
- Card line: `Floors 0–9 · ground floor = 0 · operator entry, not verified`. Never show `?` when the operator has entered a value.

### P1-3 · Framing ignores the safe area: beacons and route ends hide under the panels
**Evidence**
- **Beacons:**
  - 1600-03: the Texas Capitol beacon label sits under the top bar (y≈38).
  - 1280-03: `Fairmont Au…` is cut by the right column, and the Capitol beacon shows only as a ring under the bar.
  - 1600-07, -08, -12, -13: `er · Day 2 19:00`, the Long Center beacon with its name under the rail.
  - 1280-07: `ay 2 19:00`.
  - 1280-18: the Fairmont beacon ring peeks out to the right of the right column.
- **Routes:**
  - 1600-07 and -08: the hospital end leaves the frame behind the chips.
  - 1600-09 and 1280-09: Jay Patel, the route's origin, is off-screen bottom-right, and the route runs under the toolbar.
  - 1600-12: the Long Center route ends at the rail's edge.

**Problem:** The trip view (Part A) exists to show how spread out the trip is. Today it frames the current site and lets the other sites fall under chrome. A route whose destination isn't visible can't be checked.

**Fix (one helper for both cases)**
- Add `fitToSafeArea(points)`. It frames the given points inside a rectangle inset by:
  - left `var(--rail) + 2·g`;
  - right `var(--rcol) + 2·g` when the right column is open, else `g`;
  - top `bar + 44 px` (the chips row);
  - bottom `foot + 66 px` (toolbar plus gap).
- Call it in two places:
  - **City scale / trip view:** all site positions plus 400 m.
  - **On route draw:** the route bounding box plus both ends.
- Reduced motion: jump, don't fly.
- **Off-screen beacons.** If a beacon still falls outside the rectangle, pin an edge chip along its bearing: `Long Center · Day 2 19:00 · 1.3 km W ←`. Style: Inter 12 px, `#eff7f3` on `#061a23` α 0.9, 1 px `#80959b` border. It is a button that switches site, it is static, and it uses the same pattern as the phase-2 lightning pointer.
- **Route end labels.** Give the destination end a label plate in the beacon style: `Ascension Dell Seton · 1.9 km`.

### P1-6 · Fixed 32 px buttons with wrapping labels: the numbering options overflow at 1280

- **Advance buttons.** Shorten to `Go to site` and `Walk route from hotel`, and add `.tt-btn { white-space: nowrap; }`. Both then fit on one line in 274 px at 1280.

### P1-7 · The compass needle points at the look direction, not north; the dial spins on wrap
**Evidence**
- 1600-04 and 1280-04 (view from north): the triangle on the centre button points up at **S**.
- 1600-05 (view from east): it points at **W**.
- The CSS comment says the needle "always points to screen-up = look direction" (line 1374). A needle on a compass reads as north, so an operator glancing at it in 1600-04 gets the bearings reversed.
- **Motion.** `.tt-compass-dial` has `transition: transform 120ms` but `update()` sets `rotate(-h)` with h normalised 0–359. Crossing north (h 359 → 0) animates the dial 359° the wrong way in 120 ms. That is a full-revolution spin, and the letters counter-rotate without a transition, so they wobble.

**Fix**
- Put the north pointer on the dial so it turns with it:
  ```css
  .tt-compass-dial::before { content: ''; position: absolute; left: 50%; top: 1px; transform: translateX(-50%);
    border: 5px solid transparent; border-top: 0; border-bottom: 8px solid var(--tt-text); }
  ```
- Delete `.tt-compass-center::after`.
- Make the centre button 28 px and label it `N↑` (mono 10 px 600, `--tt-text-2`).
- Set `.tt-compass-dial { transition: none; }`. `postRender` already updates every frame, so the transition adds lag and the spin.
- Raise the rim from `--tt-border` (1.61:1 on the field) to `rgba(128,149,155,.7)` (3.41:1, meets 1.4.11).
- Hit targets otherwise pass: sides 26 px, centre 22 px with spacing. Keep the white "on" disc for the side the camera is on.

## Motion and flashing

Nothing flashes. The camera orbit and site dissolve respect reduced motion, the live-incident banner is static, and the beacons don't pulse. The one defect is the compass dial's 120 ms transition, which spins 359° when the heading crosses north (P1-7). Removing the transition fixes it. Keep the proposed edge chips and the Advance incident chip static.

## Brand and colour check

- Blue only on paths: the route lines, path cards and `Walk to…` titles.

## What works — keep

- **The site switcher popover (1600-02, 1280-02).** One row per site with its day, time and headcount (`Long Center (Day 2, 19:00) · 20 here`), a left rule for the current site, and the trip-wide kicker `SITES ON THIS TRIP`. It is clear and calm. Once it fits (P0-1), it is the right control in the right place.
- **Beacon styling.** A white-outline point on the field colour, a stalk, and Inter 12 px on a `#061a23` α 0.85 plate (`Fairmont Austin · hotel · 3 here`). Legible on dark ground and on the hologram. It only needs placement (P1-3).
- **The Advance chip.** Dashed white `ADVANCE · PLANNING`, with no people drawn in the scene. Dashed already means "not live / draft" in the legend, so the mode reads correctly at a glance.
- **The brief items.** A checkbox, a bold label and a reason line. An amber rule appears only where something needs attention (after dark, draft rally point, official alert), and `Checked by you · 22:18` gives provenance. Honest copy: `straight line … estimate ×1.3 … route not checked`.
- **Path cards for any destination.** Blue rule and title, the standard `Suggested · unverified · not checked for closures, crowds or lighting`, and `no live traffic` / `no parking or drop-off checked` on driving. `Clear route (Esc)` as a link.
- **Compass at 1600.** Placement clear of the right column and toolbar, upright letters on a rotating dial, the camera's side as a white disc, and N in full text colour. Fix the needle (P1-7) and keep everything else.
- **Glass dims during floor focus (1600-18, 1280-18).** The slab and its people now read first, and people not on that floor are ghosted. This closes phase-2 P1-5's contrast problem.
- **The venue cutaway with "possible / likely" text in the bars**, and London's honest `Floor count not mapped` state before an answer (1600-15).
- **Demo B's split-group line.** `Group across 2 sites: Long Center 20 · Fairmont Austin 3 · between sites 2 · …` puts trip-wide attention in the rail without filtering anyone out.
