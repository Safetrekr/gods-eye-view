# Phase 3 review: UX (operator task flows)

Reviewer lens: task flows under time pressure and in calm advance work. Evidence: `shots/1280-*` and `shots/1600-*` (18 states each), `1280-text.json` and `1600-text.json`, read-only code in `src/operations/tripTwin/`. Recorded decisions are not reopened: no red/green on people, green only for approved places, blue only for paths, the 10 km lightning radius, a session-only checklist, a fictional scenario clock, and a site switch that rebuilds the hologram.

## Verdict

Phase 3 has the right building blocks, and its copy is mostly honest. "Suggested · unverified", "no live traffic", "Nothing is routed until you choose" and "draft · not approved" all appear where they should. It is **not ready to ship** for three reasons:

1. **One P0.** The floor-numbering answer can produce a false zero during an incident: `0 may be at or above floor 9` on a building whose drawn top floor is 8.
2. **The Advance brief and the screen describe different sites.** The brief covers the selected event's site, but the 3D view, card, chips and breadcrumb stay on the current site, and a route from one day stays drawn when another day is selected.
3. **Two key tasks don't fit on screen.** The City "trip view" loses its beacons behind the panels, and a route's destination ends off-screen or under a panel at 1280.

The site switcher itself disappears from the top bar in Advance mode at both widths.

---

## Findings

### P0 — blocks the task

**1. The numbering answer creates a false zero at or above the reported floor.**
*Evidence:* `1280/1600-16-numbering-after-answer`. The operator answered `ground floor = 0 · 9 floors above ground`. The Floors list tops out at **8**, the reported floor 9 has no row, and the hero reads `0 may be at or above floor 9`. The top line adds `no one at or above by phone estimate or room list`.
*Why:* in code, `levels_above_ground` is a count that **includes** the ground floor: `twinData.js floorOccupancy` loops `level = levels-1 … 0`. In UK usage, "9 floors above ground" means floors 1–9 plus G, so the top floor should be 9. Nothing checks that the reported floor exists in the building, and neither the hero nor the top line says the numbering came from an operator entry. This is the failure phase-2 F2 was written to prevent.
*Fix:*
- Ask for the top floor, not a count: `Top floor number (as on the lift buttons)`. Derive `levels = top − ground + 1`.
- If the reported floor is above the top floor or below the ground label, show no count. The hero reads `Reported floor 9 is above the top floor you entered (8). Check the report or the floor numbering.` with a `Change numbering` link. The top line matches.
- Whenever the numbering is an operator entry, add a line under the hero, in the top line and in Copy for responders: `Floor numbering from operator entry · not verified`.
- Add a unit test: London, ground = 0, top = 8, reported 9 → no number, the above-top wording.

### P1 — slows or misleads

**2. The numbering form saves on the first click, and during an incident it sits below the fold.**
*Evidence:* `1280-15`. The prompt is the bottom card in the right column, under the incident panel, and its Floors field and note are cut off. The hero says `(floor numbering not mapped)` but gives no way to fix it. In `twinNumbering.js`, each ground-floor button calls `onSave` at once, and the optional floors field is rendered **after** the buttons. An operator who reads top-down and clicks `Ground floor = 0` first loses the floor count and has to use `Change`.
*Fix:*
- Make it one form: ground floor as a radio pair (`Ground floor is 0 (UK / Europe)` · `Ground floor is 1 (US)`), then `Top floor number (optional)`, then a `Use this numbering` button. `Not sure` stays a separate link.
- In the hero, after `(floor numbering not mapped)`, add a `Set floor numbering` link that scrolls the card into view and focuses the first radio.

**3. The Advance brief and the 3D view show different sites.**
*Evidence:*
- `1280/1600-11`: the brief is `Long Center … Day 2 · 19:00`, but the breadcrumb, the scene and the right card all show `Fairmont Austin`, and the chips (`Hospital 1.7 km N`) are measured from the hotel while the brief says `hospital … 2.5 km NE`.
- `1280-13`: the Day 3 Texas Capitol brief is open, but the Day 2 walking route to the Long Center is still drawn.
- After Esc clears that route, the brief still says `suggested route shown` (`page.js` keeps `adv.routes[site]`).

*Fix:*
- When the brief's site is not on screen, the kicker reads `SITE BRIEF · DAY 2 · 19:00 · not on screen`, and `Go to this site` stays the primary button.
- Hide the place chips in Advance unless they belong to the brief's site, or prefix them with the site: `Near Fairmont Austin: Hospital 1.7 km N`.
- Changing the event clears the drawn route.
- Clearing the route resets the item text to the straight-line estimate with `route not shown`.

**4. The site switcher, the main way to change sites, is truncated or missing.**
*Evidence:*
- Advance at 1600 (`1600-11/12/14`): the breadcrumb ends in `Fairr`.
- Advance at 1280 (`1280-10…14`): it shows only `Spr`, and the switcher is gone.
- Live at 1280 (`1280-06…09`): it reads `Fairmont Aust` with no ▾.

*Fix:*
- The site button gets `flex-shrink: 0` and a min-width that fits `{short name} ▾`.
- Shrink in this order: `World View ›`, then the trip name (to an ellipsis), then the `TRIP TWIN` wordmark (hidden below 1440).
- Below 1440 the mode chip shortens to `ADVANCE`.
- The focus segment (`› Ascension Dell Seton…`) truncates before the site button does.

**5. The City "trip view" doesn't show the whole trip.**
*Evidence:*
- `1280-03`: Texas Capitol's beacon is under the top bar, and Fairmont Austin's label is clipped by the card (`Fairmont Au`).
- `1600-03`: the Texas Capitol label is cut by the top bar.
- `1280/1600-07`, `-12`: the Long Center label is clipped by the rail (`er · Day 2 19:00`).

Beacons are canvas-only, and off-screen sites get no chip, so the operator can't tell how spread out the group is.
*Fix:*
- City scale (key 3) frames the bounding box of all sites inside the unobstructed viewport (insets: rail width, card width, top 96 px, bottom 72 px).
- Each off-screen site gets a chip in the chip row, which switches site on click: `Texas Capitol · Day 3 · 1.4 km N`.
- Add a first row to the switcher: `Whole trip (City view)`.

**6. Advance mode hides trip-wide attention unless there is an incident.**
*Evidence:* `1280-10…13`. The rail's `6 to check on — first: Jay Patel, unaccompanied outside group zone` is gone, and only an incident brings back a banner (`1280-14`). This breaks the Part A rule that Needs attention is never hidden.
*Fix:* keep a neutral one-line live strip at the top of the Advance panel at all times: `Live: no incident · 6 to check on — first: Jay Patel · Switch to Live`. Use the existing amber attention style for the count, and keep red for incidents only.

**7. The Advance panel is rebuilt on every feed update, so keyboard focus and scroll position are lost.**
*Evidence (code):* each update in `page.js` runs `if (appMode === 'advance') applyAppMode()`. That calls `renderAdvance()`, which calls `panel.replaceChildren(…)`. Only the notes textarea gets its focus back. A keyboard user ticking checklist items, or dragging the timeline slider, loses their place every few seconds. The panel's scroll position also resets, which matters at 1280, where Copy sits below the fold.
*Fix:*
- Re-render the brief only when Advance state changes (event, check, route, forecast loaded).
- Update the live strip and the incident banner in place.
- Before any rebuild, save the focused element's key and `scrollTop`, and restore both after.

**8. Route destinations are not framed or labelled, and the standing rally path is drawn in the same blue.**
*Evidence:*
- `1280-07/08`: the hospital end of the route is off the top edge.
- `1600-07`: the end is under the chip row and has no label.
- `1280/1600-12`: the end at the Long Center is under the rail.
- `1280-07`, `-09`, `-12`: the solid and dashed blue path to R1 is drawn next to the new route, so two blue routes share the screen, against "one route set visible at a time".

*Fix:*
- Frame the route's bounds inside the unobstructed viewport, with the same insets as finding 5.
- Draw an end label in white text on the field colour: `Ascension Dell Seton · 1.9 km`.
- While an on-demand route is shown, fade the standing rally path to 25 % and hide its label. Restore both when the route is cleared.

**9. Routing a person to a hospital takes a hidden two-step.**
*Evidence:*
- `1280-09`: the person card offers only `To Fairmont Austin · To nearest rally point`.
- `1280-06`: on the hospital card, `From selected person` is disabled, and the only explanation is a hover title (`Select a person first`).

The working path is to select the person, then open the hospital (it uses `lastPersonId`). Nothing on screen says so.
*Fix:*
- Add `To nearest hospital · {name} · {distance}` to the person card, preferring `emergency=yes`.
- Show the disabled origin's reason as visible text: `From a person — select them in the rail or on the map first`.

**10. The compass key N and the compass letter N do opposite things, and the highlighted letter doesn't help orientation.**
*Evidence:*
- `1280-04`: after "view from north", the dial reads S at the top, and the highlighted N sits at the bottom.
- Every default view highlights **S** (`1280-01`, `-06`).
- In code, key `N` calls `viewFromSide(0)`, which looks north from the south side, while the letter N calls `lookHeadingFrom(0)`, which looks south from the north side. Pressing N therefore lights up S.

*Fix:*
- Keep Jesse's click meaning.
- Add a caption under the dial: `Viewing from S · looking N`.
- Label the centre button `N↑`, and change the help text to `N — north up (view from the south)`.
- Mark the active letter with an outer tick on the ring rather than a filled disc, so it doesn't read as a selected button.

**11. The venue floor card carries hotel context.**
*Evidence:* `1280/1600-18` at the Long Center. The back link reads `‹ Hotel`, and rows read `Likely floor 1 (1–2) · assigned floor 11` (a hotel room floor on a 2-floor venue).
*Why:* in code, `twinHud.js:772` hard-codes `'‹ Hotel'`, and `assessSnapshot` merges `room_assignments` from **every** place. During a venue incident, hotel room floors would also feed the venue's `assigned at or above` group.
*Fix:*
- The back link reads `‹ {site short}`.
- Room assignments apply only when the active place is the lodging. At venues, drop `assigned floor N`, and drop the "Assigned here" sections.

**12. Roster buckets read as a concern when the group is simply at another site, and the per-site counts can't be clicked.**
*Evidence:*
- `1280-17`, Air and Space: `Away 22`, although the 22 are at their DC hotel.
- Demo B (`1280-01`): `Away 6`.
- The line `between sites 2` is plain text. To find those two people, the operator has to scan the rail.

*Fix:*
- Split the bucket: `At other trip sites 22` (neutral weight) and `Away from all sites N`. This matches the plan's `At this site · Nearby · Elsewhere`.
- In the per-site line, each site name becomes a button that switches site, and `between sites 2` becomes a button that filters the roster to those people.

### P2 — polish

**13. Advance copy and timestamps.**
- `estimate ×1.3 for streets` → `about 26–34 min walk (straight line + 30 % · route not checked)`.
- `After dark at 19:00 · sunset 18:58 · ends 21:30` → `Dusk at 19:00 (sunset 18:58) · dark by 19:24 · ends 21:30 after dark — plan the return walk for darkness`.
- `Checked by you · 22:21` sits next to the top bar's `21:32 local · sample`, and the stamp has no date for multi-day advance work. Change it to `Checked by you · Thu 15 Oct · 22:21 (your clock)`.
- In Advance, the weather chip still says `LIVE 81 °F` (`1280-10`). Change it to `Now · 81 °F` so the planning screen carries no "LIVE" badge.

**14. Switcher copy, accessibility and keys.**
- `on screen · 20 here · select the building` → `Showing now · 20 here · Enter selects the venue`.
- `#tt-hotel` needs `aria-haspopup="dialog"`, `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls="tt-sites"`.
- Live / Advance has no key. Add `V` (toggle Live / Advance) and list it in `?` help.

**15. Overlaps and small formatting.**
- At 1280 the compass covers the rally label (`Rally point (prim`, `1280-15/16`). Lower it to 80 % opacity when not hovered, and move it 16 px clear of the right column.
- Initials stacks still collide with the ruler (`1600-01` `A.N.P. M.S. S.D.H.K.V.`, `1600-16` `N.P. M.R. O.H.`).
- `Likely floor 1 (1–1)` → `Likely floor 1` when the range is a single floor.
- OpenStreetMap hospitals and police are drawn as **green** chips, while their card says `OpenStreetMap place · not verified by SafeTrekr`. If 06's "unvetted style" applies, use it. If green-for-OSM was settled earlier, ignore this item.

## What works — keep
- **Honest route copy:** `Suggested · unverified · not checked for closures, crowds or lighting`, `no live traffic`, `no traffic, parking or drop-off checked`, `Nothing is routed until you choose`. Esc clears the route first, then the card.
- **The hospital chip gets to a route in three clicks** (chip → card with the hotel pre-selected → Walk / Drive), and off-screen chips open the same card as on-screen places.
- **The per-site group line** (`Group across 2 sites: Long Center 20 · Fairmont Austin 3 · between sites 2 · elsewhere 1 · not located 1 · of 27`) adds up to the whole group, and in Live, Needs attention stays trip-wide.
- **Advance is plainly a planning view:** a dashed `ADVANCE · PLANNING` chip, `No live positions in this view`, and a red `LIVE INCIDENT … switch to Live` banner that works as a button.
- **The brief's honesty lines:** `None on file for this time (checked now; alerts are issued close to the time)`, `emergency department not confirmed`, `draft · not approved`, `Not mapped · answer the numbering question on the site card`, and checklist stamps that say who checked.
- **The numbering prompt's framing:** `Operator entry · not verified · used only for this trip … nothing is sent anywhere`, a `Change` link, and `Not sure` keeping the phase-2 "can't be matched" wording.
- **The compass basics:** real buttons with `View from …` labels, `,` and `.` rotate the view 90°, and the dial turns with the camera.
- **The venue card kicker** `TRIP VENUE · DAY 2 · 19:00` and the `2 floors · US numbering · lobby = 1` line give context at a glance.
