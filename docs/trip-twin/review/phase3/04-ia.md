# 04 IA review, phase 3: Sites & Advance

Owner: information architect. Review only; no code changed.

**Inputs**
- Shots: `review/phase3/shots/` (1600-01…18, 1280-02/09/11/18), `1600-text.json`.
- Markup and wiring: `twin.html`, `tripTwin/page.js` (renderSites, switchSite, routeEnds, selectPlace, renderAdvance, applyAppMode, keydown), `twinHud.js` (floor, building, renderRouteSection), `twinSites.js`, `twinAdvance.js`, `twinData.js`, `twinNumbering.js`, `twinCompass.js`.
- Plans and earlier reviews: 06 (§3 PTH, §9), ROUND-2 §6–§10, PHASE-3-PLAN, phase-2 `04-ia.md` and `00-FIX-LIST.md`.

**Not reopened.** These decisions stand:
- one hologram at a time;
- the site switcher lives in the breadcrumb's site segment;
- Advance replaces the rail;
- the checklist is session-only;
- the compass is bottom-right;
- the 10 km lightning radius;
- Needs attention is trip-wide.

## Verdict

The model is right. Every trip place is a **site**. The breadcrumb's site segment is the switcher, the list is scoped "SITES ON THIS TRIP", beacons appear at City scale, the rail follows the site, and attention stays trip-wide. Advance is a separate mode, it says so in the chip, and a live incident breaks through it. The URL carries `site` and `mode`. Phase 4 and the SOC can build on this.

Three P0s, all about place identity:
1. Venue Floors show the hotel's room list.
2. The numbering prompt's "Floors above ground" field is read as a count of storeys. This removes the reported top floor and produces a false zero.
3. In Advance, and at 1280, nothing in the bar names the site on screen, while the brief can describe a different site.

The P1s are vocabulary and seams:
- "route" vs "path", and the banned phrase "Clear route";
- keys that collide with the compass letters;
- three different populations all counted as "at this site";
- a brief whose items are keyed per site rather than per event and leg. Phase 4 needs per-event, per-leg keys.

## Findings

### P0-1: Venue Floors carry the hotel's room list and the hotel's words

**Evidence**
- 1600-18 and 1280-18, Long Center › Floor 1. The rows read `Likely floor 1 (1–2) · 50s ago · assigned floor 11` (also floors 9 and 8).
- The back link is `‹ Hotel`. The footer reads `… assignments are floor only`.
- `twinData.js` l.750–754 builds `assignedById` from **every** place's `room_assignments`, with no place filter.
- `twinHud.js` l.772 hard-codes `‹ Hotel`.

**Problem**
- At a 2-storey concert hall, "assigned floor 11" means a hotel room, but it reads as a venue fact. It will also reach "Copy floor for hotel desk" (an incident-only button that already says "hotel desk").
- If any hotel assignment is on floor 1 or 2, a venue floor would show `Assigned here, floor not known` and turn amber. That is a false "should be here but isn't". The plan says: "rooming exists only for lodging".

**Fix**
- Filter assignments by `place.id === activePlace.id`. At a venue, drop the assignment note and the three `Assigned here…` sections.
- Footer at a venue: `Not a headcount · phone-barometer estimates, not verified · no room list at venues`.
- Back link: `‹ {site.short}` (`‹ Long Center`).
- Copy button at a venue: `Copy floor for venue staff`. Copy header: `{site.label} · floor {N}`.
- Scope line, as phase-2 P1-7 planned: `At Long Center (20): floor unknown 1 · not shared 0 · last estimate only 0`. Keep `Whole group (27)` only when the site is the only site. Venue bars count only people at this site (today floor 1 shows `16 likely` while Inside (likely) is 10).

### P0-2: "Floors above ground" is read as a storey count, so the reported top floor disappears and the count is a false zero

**Evidence**
- 1600-16, London. The operator answered `ground floor = 0 · 9 floors above ground`.
- Floors then lists 0…8, and floor 9 does not exist.
- The incident panel shows `0 may be at or above floor 9`. The card footer reads `? floors · ground floor = 0`.
- `twinNumbering.js` stores the field as `levels_above_ground`, OSM `building:levels` semantics (storeys including the ground floor).

**Problem.** In a UK lift showing G, 1 … 9, the natural answer to "floors above ground" is 9. The model then assumes 9 storeys (0–8). The phase-2 F2 "no false zero" guard is removed by a label.

**Fix**
- Change the field label to `Top floor number (as shown in the lift)` with placeholder `e.g. 9`. Derive `levels = top − ground + 1`.
- Store `top_floor_label`, not a storey count. Record it in 07: production field `top_floor_label`, `levels_source: 'operator'`.
- When the reported floor is above the top floor (mapped or entered), never show a count. Hero: `Floor 9 is above the top floor on record (8) — check the floor numbering`. Floors lists the reported floor anyway, as a pink bar.
- Card line: `Ground floor is 0 · top floor 9 · operator entry, not verified`. Never `?`.

### P0-3: The site on screen is unnamed in Advance and at 1280, while the brief can show another site

**Evidence**
- 1600-11 and 1600-14 (Advance): the crumb is cut to `World View › Spri… › Fairr`.
- 1280-11 (Advance): `TRIP TWIN Spr`. The site segment is gone completely.
- 1280-09: `Fairmont Aust`. 1280-18: `Long Center ▾`, with no `› Floor 1` segment, so phase-2 F9 is not met at 1280.
- In 1600-11 the brief is `SITE BRIEF · DAY 2 · 19:00 · Long Center for the Performing Arts`, while the scene and the card are Fairmont Austin (`GROUP HOTEL`). The only cue is the button `Go to this site`.

**Problem**
- The breadcrumb is the only label for the hologram. Where it is lost, the operator has two "current sites": the brief's site (timeline) and the scene's site (switcher), and no label telling them apart.
- On a multi-site trip that is how a building, its Floors or its rally point gets attributed to the wrong site.

**Fix**
- **Collapse order for the bar.** The site segment is never truncated. It uses `short`, max ~22 ch; see P2-12 for shorter hotel names.
  1. Hide `World View ›`.
  2. Shorten the trip to `Trip ›`, with the full title in a tooltip (phase-2 P1-6).
  3. Ellipsize the focus segment (`› Floor 1`, `› Jay Patel`).
- **Advance chip.** In Advance the chip reads `PLANNING`, because the toggle already says Advance. That frees about 90 px.
- **Brief header** when the brief site differs from the scene:
  - Add the line `Not on screen · on screen: Fairmont Austin`.
  - Rename the button to `Go to Long Center` (name the site; never "this site").
  - When they match: `On screen`.
- **QA.** Add a check that `#tt-hotel` is fully visible at 1280×720 in both modes.

### P1-4: "Route" vs "path". "Clear route" is a banned phrase, and route results don't say where they start

**Evidence**
- 06 PTH-01 sets the term as **path** (`Suggested path (unverified)`) and lists "clear route" under *Don't use*. PTH-16 reserves "route" for `Official route · {authority}`.
- Phase 3 copy uses "route" throughout:
  - `ROUTE HERE`, `ROUTE FROM THIS PERSON`, `Clear route (Esc)`, `Show walking route from the hotel`, `route not checked`;
  - next to phase-2 `Path to rally point (primary)` and Layers › Paths.
- Result lines read `Walk to Ascension Dell Seton … · 1.9 km · walk 30–39 min` (1600-07). No origin is named, although `routeCards` accepts `fromLabel` (twinRoutes.js l.99) and page.js never passes it.
- `From Jay Patel` gives no fix age. 06 PTH-10 requires `From last known position, {n} min old`.

**Problem.** "Clear route" reads as "the route is clear". Two nouns for one object weaken the 06 rule that a computed line is never a "route" (official or evacuation).

**Fix.** Operator copy uses "path" everywhere. Code may keep `route`.

| Now | Change to |
|---|---|
| `ROUTE HERE` | `PATHS TO HERE` |
| `ROUTE FROM THIS PERSON` | `PATHS FROM THIS PERSON` |
| `Clear route (Esc)` | `Remove path (Esc)` |
| `Show walking route from the hotel` | `Show walking path from the hotel` (see P1-8) |
| `route not checked` | `path not checked` |
| `Walk to X · 1.9 km · …` | `Walk · Fairmont Austin → Ascension Dell Seton Medical Center · 1.9 km · 30–39 min (group pace)` |
| `From Jay Patel` (not current) | `From Jay Patel · last known 2h ago` (PTH-10) |
| `From group centre (19)` | `From group centre (19 here)`, tooltip unchanged |

Phase 4 then has free names: `Suggested path` (computed) vs `Custom path · {author} · {time}` (operator-drawn). "Route" stays reserved for official routes.

### P1-5: The person's path ends depend on the site on screen, not on the person

**Evidence**
- `routeEnds` (page.js l.821–852) offers `To {city.short}` and `To nearest rally point`. The rally point is chosen among the **on-screen site's** points (`location_id === here`).
- At the Long Center there is no "To the hotel". The plan (Part C) says "Route to the hotel / site / nearest rally point".
- 1600-09: Jay Patel is 850 m from the hotel. If the twin were on the Long Center, his "nearest rally point" would be the venue's, 1.3 km away.

**Fix.** Fixed ends, each naming its target:
1. `To Fairmont Austin (hotel)` (always).
2. `To Long Center` (only when the on-screen site is a venue).
3. `To nearest rally point · {site short}`, the nearest approved primary rally point of **any** trip site to the person. Use a `(draft)` suffix as today.

### P1-6: The compass letters and the keys mean different things

**Evidence**
- The compass buttons are `N E S W` = "View from north…" (twinCompass.js l.45).
- Key `N` = `viewFromSide(0)` = look north, which is the view from the **south** (page.js l.1943). It does the opposite of the N button.
- After `N`, the compass highlights **S** (every shot).
- Key `S` opens Sites and key `E` toggles elevation, while the compass also prints S and E.

**Problem.** A ring of letters on a compass reads as key hints. Three of the four mislead.

**Fix**
- Keep `N` = north up (map convention).
- Make the ring letters passive bearing labels.
- Move the click targets to four wedges outside the ring, titled `View from the north side (looking south)` and so on.
- Under the compass, a one-line readout: `Looking north · from the south side`.
- Help: `N · North up (looking north)`. `S` (Sites) and `E` (Elevation) stay, because no letter on the compass is clickable any more.

### P1-7: "At this site" is counted three ways, and "Away" overlaps "elsewhere"

**Evidence**
- 1600-06 baseline:
  - the site line says `Fairmont Austin 22`;
  - `From group centre (19)`;
  - the buckets add up to 19 current people (Inside 15, At hotel undetermined 3, Outside nearby 1);
  - `siteCounts` (twinSites.js l.298) counts any coordinates, including the 4 `Not current`.
- Demo B (1600-01): `Away 6` = Fairmont 3 + between sites 2 + elsewhere 1, so "Away" in the buckets ⊃ "elsewhere" in the site line.
- The line is sorted by count, so in DC (1600-17) the site on screen comes second: `Hilton … 22 · Air and Space 0`.
- "between sites" means within 200 m of the straight line from the hotel to **any** venue, on any day.

**Fix**
- The site line counts current positions only:
  `Group across 2 sites: Long Center 20 · Fairmont Austin 3 · between sites 2 · elsewhere 1 · not current 0 · not located 1 · of 27`
  - The on-screen site comes first, then the others by count.
  - Baseline reads `Fairmont Austin 19 · … · not current 4 …`.
- The bucket `Away` becomes `Not at this site` on multi-site trips. Its sub-line repeats the split: `Fairmont Austin 3 · between sites 2 · elsewhere 1`.
- "between sites" uses only legs for **today's** events. Tooltip: `Within 200 m of the straight line between the hotel and today's venue · not a tracked journey`. Never "in transit" (it claims movement).
- Definitions for 06 (see P2-15):
  - `At {site}` = current fix ≤ 150 m;
  - `Between sites`;
  - `Elsewhere` = current fix, not near any site or leg;
  - `Not current`;
  - `Not located`.

### P1-8: The brief is keyed per site. Phase 4 needs it per event and per leg, including the walk back

**Evidence**
- `adv.checks[${site.key}:${item}]`, `adv.notes[site.key]` and `adv.routes[site.key]` (page.js l.1019–1024, 1067, 1187, 1204). A site visited twice (the hotel every night) shares one checklist.
- `From the hotel` covers only the outbound leg. The Day 2 concert `ends 21:30` and is flagged `After dark` (1600-11), yet the return walk, the risky one, has no item.
- After Esc removes the drawn path, the brief still says `suggested route shown` (1600-12).
- In Advance the live top line (`6 to check on`) has no home. Only an incident breaks through (1600-14).

**Fix**
- Key checks and notes by `event.key`. Key paths by leg: `${event.key}:out|back:${profile}`.
- Replace `From the hotel` with leg items:
  - `Hotel → Long Center · arrive by 19:00`
  - `Long Center → hotel · after 21:30 · after dark`
- Leg status text, in this order: `straight line only · path not checked` → `suggested path viewed {HH:MM} · unverified` (an event, so it survives Esc) → phase 4 `custom path saved by {name} · {HH:MM}`.
- Buttons: `Show walking path · out`, `Show walking path · back`.
- The copied brief lists both legs with their status. That is where phase 4 "included in the advance brief" will slot in.
- 07 production fields:
  - `advance_items(trip_id, event_id, place_id, item_key, checked_by, checked_at, note)`;
  - `saved_paths(trip_id, day, event_id, leg, profile, source suggested|custom, waypoints, avoid_areas, author, at)`.
- Live strip at the top of Advance, same pattern as the incident banner, whenever the trip is in progress: `LIVE · no incident · 6 to check on — switch to Live`.

### P1-9: On-demand paths sit outside Layers, and the Paths row now means "rally only"

**Evidence**
- `twin.showRoute` draws regardless of Layers › Paths (tripTwin.js l.1052).
- The Layers row `Suggested walking paths` (page.js l.1352) toggles only the rally path.

**Fix now**
- Rename the row `Paths to rally point`.
- One rule: the Paths group governs the visibility of every drawn path, and the card governs which path is drawn.
- With Paths off, the card shows `Hidden — Layers › Paths is off · Show`.
- While an on-demand path is drawn, a line under the group: `Also drawn: walk · Fairmont Austin → Ascension Dell Seton… · remove in its card (Esc)`.
- No toggle for on-demand paths: they belong to the card that asked for them.

**Phase 4.** Saved paths are persistent and per day, so they do belong in Layers:
- `Saved paths · Day 2 (2)`, filtered by the Advance timeline day, or by today in Live.
- 06 C5 (hazard-check dependency) applies to them unchanged.

### P1-10: Esc, help and keys don't match the mode or the site

**Evidence**
- The actual Esc order is: popover → drawn path → place card → person-from-floor → floor → selection (page.js l.1901–1915).
- The help row (twin.html l.343) still reads `person › floor card › hotel`.
- `H` is documented as "Hotel and its Floors" but selects the **venue** on a venue (`twin.site.hotel`).
- In Advance, `A`, `[`, `]` and `/` select hidden people, and `R` opens a live incident form.

**Fix**
- Help Esc row: `Close panel, then remove the drawn path, then close the place card, then step back (person › floor card › site), then clear the selection (never exits)`.
- Help H row: `H · This site's building and its Floors`.
- In Advance:
  - `[` `]` step the timeline events (help shows mode-specific rows);
  - `A`, `/` and `R` show the status `Live only — switch to Live`;
  - the toolbar `Add reported floor` is hidden.

### P2-12: One name, and one name-plus-time pattern, per site

**Evidence.** The same site appears under several names:
- `Long Center` (crumb), `The Long Center for the Performing Arts` (card, OSM name), `Long Center for the Performing Arts` (brief);
- `Texas Capitol` vs `Texas State Capitol`;
- the hotel `short` is the full label (`Hilton Washington DC Capitol Hill`, `Park Plaza London Waterloo`), which is why it clips first.

The time stamp has four formats: `Long Center (Day 2, 19:00)`, beacon `Texas Capitol · Day 3 10:00`, kicker `TRIP VENUE · DAY 2 · 19:00`, timeline `Day 2 · 19:00–21:30 · Concert · Long Center`.

**Fix**
- Names:
  - `short` in all chrome (crumb, switcher, beacons, rail, path labels);
  - `label` as card and brief title;
  - the OSM name only in the source line.
- Hotel shorts: `Hilton Capitol Hill`, `Park Plaza Waterloo`. In production this is a `places.short_name` field.
- One pattern: `Long Center · Day 2 · 19:00` and `Fairmont Austin · hotel`.
- Kickers: `GROUP HOTEL` / `VENUE · DAY 2 · 19:00` (drop "TRIP").
- Switcher meta: `On screen · 20 here`.
- Brief labels:
  - `Light` → `Daylight`;
  - `Official alerts` → `Official weather alerts` (only NWS/MeteoAlarm are checked);
  - `Rally point` → `Rally point (primary)`.
- Advance toggle tooltip: `Plan each site before the trip · no live positions`.

### P2-13: Floor numbering is phrased four ways

**Evidence**
- Brief: `Mapped · 37 floors · ground floor = 1`.
- Card: `37 floors · US numbering · lobby = 1`.
- Prompt: `Ground floor = 1 (US)`.
- After an answer: `? floors · ground floor = 0`.

**Fix.** One sentence everywhere: `Ground floor is 1 (US numbering) · top floor 37 · mapped`, or `Ground floor is 0 · top floor 9 · operator entry, not verified`. Drop "lobby = 1", because the lobby is not always the ground floor. With no top floor: `top floor not entered`.

### P2-14: SOC seams to cut now

## What works — keep

- **Site is the umbrella word.** "SITES ON THIS TRIP", "Group across 2 sites" and "Go to …" make "site" the parent of hotel and venue without inventing a new layer group.
- **The switcher is the breadcrumb's site segment.** No second site name appears; `S` opens it; the list shows day and time plus counts ("3 here").
- **Beacons at City scale** read `Fairmont Austin · hotel · 3 here`, and clicking one switches site.
- **Needs attention stays trip-wide** while buckets follow the site. "At venue, undetermined" is parameterised.
- **Advance is clearly a mode.** It has its own chip, `No live positions in this view`, and no people are drawn. `LIVE INCIDENT … — switch to Live` breaks through.
- **The brief is honest:**
  - `straight line · about 26–34 min walk (estimate ×1.3 for streets) · route not checked`;
  - `None on file for this time (checked now; alerts are issued close to the time)`;
  - `draft · not approved`;
  - `emergency department not confirmed`;
  - copies are per day, with as-of time, sources and SIMULATED.
- **The place card** gives distance and bearing from the site, `OpenStreetMap place · not verified by SafeTrekr`, and the draft kicker.
- **Paths only on demand.** `Nothing is routed until you choose.` One path set at a time; Esc removes the path before closing the card.
- **Driving is labelled honestly:** `no live traffic` and `no traffic, parking or drop-off checked`.
- **The numbering prompt** offers `Not sure` (keeps the honest failure) and `Operator entry · not verified · used only for this trip`. It is changeable.
- **An incident at another site** gets its own chip (`Reported … at {site} … — show`), so it is never hidden by the site switch.
