# Phase 12 review: protective operations lens

Reviewer: protective-operations lead, SafeTrekr operations room. Scenario: school choir of 27 at the Fairmont Austin, outdoor concert at the Long Center (0.8 mi W). Evidence: all 16 screenshots in `shots/`, read against `00-BRIEF.md`.

## Verdict

Phase 12 gets the storm seen early by everyone in the room. The strip lands on all six walls and the remote pill at the same moment, and every element carries SIMULATED, a source and a time. It does not yet help the room time the protective call. The display times rain reaching the hotel, but the call is set by lightning reaching the people who are outdoors. Those people, their nearest indoor place and walk time, and whether chaperones have been told are not on any storm surface. Four display problems can cause a wrong or late call: a warning that appears to expire in one minute, a weather wall whose OFFICIAL row says no alerts, an ETA that does not count down, and a walk time that points to an outdoor rally point.
