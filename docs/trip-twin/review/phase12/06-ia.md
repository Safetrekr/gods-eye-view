# Phase 12 review 06: labels, wording and information structure

Reviewer lens: one name per thing, the order of parts, length per surface, time formats, units, and what a first-time supervisor would misread. Read: 00-BRIEF, 06-layer-taxonomy §3.9–3.11, §18, §19, all 16 shots, and the strings in `twinNowcast.js` (approachText, etaText), `page.js` (approachLine, officialLines, ltgLine, radar3dNote, alertAreasNote, lensLine, weatherPanel, showInspect), `twinStorm3d.js`, `twinWeather.js:643`, `twinWeatherTimeline.js`, `twinScreen.js` (wxStrip), `remote/remote.js` (refreshWx, tile pills), `remote/stations.js` (weather).

## Verdict

The core sentence is honest. It has a source, a frame time and a conditional "on this track", and the SIMULATED marking holds on every surface. Three things will mislead a supervisor on day one. S4, S3 and S5 say "no active alerts" while the simulated warning is drawn on the same wall. The real weather clock (20:21 CDT) sits beside the sample clock (21:33 local) with no ages, so the radar looks 72 minutes old and the lens looks like it points into the past. While the lens is away, the strip appends the live "~25 min" to a view of +30 min. Fix those three, rename the codename "See it coming" and the "lightning watch", and put the arrival first in the line. The rest is consistency work, listed below.

## Vocabulary for §20

| Term (as shown) | Meaning | Never call it |
|---|---|---|
| `Rain core` · `Moderate` / `Heavy` / `Intense` | Connected radar area at or above yellow (~40 dBZ), by class; green is light rain, never a core | storm cell, cell, thunderstorm (unless NWS says so), storm (alone) |
| `Storm approach` | The section, and the one sentence about the nearest core | See it coming (on screen), ETA, nowcast, prediction |
| `reaches the hotel in about N min on its current track` | Conditional arrival from observed motion | will reach, arrives, impact, hit |
| `Overhead` / `over the hotel now` | A core within reach of the site | OVER (alone), on top, hitting |
| `Track` (`+15 min` ticks) | The drawn line of the core's current motion | path, route, storm path, forecast track |
| `Projection on the current track` | Radar moved forward along the track, at most 60 min | forecast, prediction, will; "projected" never for fire (§3.11) |
| `Forecast` | MET Norway site forecast or the model forecast map only | the projection |
| `Observed radar` / `satellite estimate` | Real frames, with source, CDT time and age | live (for radar), observed map |
| `SIMULATED` | Demo element; always the first word | test, fake, example, sample (reserved for people data) |
| `Time lens` · `VIEWING +30 MIN · 20:51 CDT` | One weather time for every wall; people stay at now | replay, rewind, time travel, a bare clock time |
| `Official alert` · `alert area` · `inside the area` | NWS or MeteoAlarm product, its polygon or zones, and whether the site is inside | our warning, danger zone, affected area; Watch/Warning for our computations |
| `Lightning ring 6.2 mi (10 km)` | The fixed 30-minute-rule distance around the site | lightning watch, watch ring, safe distance |
| `Nearest grid cell` · `getting closer` / `moving away` / `steady` | Lightning density cell and its trend | strike (for real data), bolt, closing |
| `Rain column` | 3D column whose height encodes the intensity class | cloud, tower, storm height |
| `Frame the storm` | The operator's camera action (All-in-one chip, remote) | zoom to storm, follow, track storm |
| `radar 20:21 CDT, 1 min old` | Every weather number's source, real time with zone, and age | a bare time, a sample-clock time |
