# Phase 8 — UI / visual review (wider area: context ring + corridors)

Review only. Shots: `review/phase8/shots/{1600,1280}-NN-*.png`. Code: `twinContext.js` (ring/corridor tokens), `twinScene.js` (`TWIN_PALETTE`, `setContextDim`), `twinDeclutter.js`, `tripTwin.js`.
Fixture numbers below come from `scripts/fixtures/trip-twin/{austin,london,dc}.context.json`, measured from the corridor start (hotel) against the 400 m core.

**Verdict:** The idea works. At Neighborhood (02) the city no longer stops at the core, and at Building scale (04) the context stays out of the way, with corridor labels correctly gated by the 300 m display distance. The hierarchy is inverted at City scale, though. Ring and corridor **streets** are lighter and wider than core streets. Corridor **buildings** are more opaque than core glass and use the hotel's subject hue. In London during an incident (08), the city lattice outweighs the red incident. Nothing dims the context in Ways out or during an incident. Fix 1–2 (P0) before this is on by default.

## P0

### 1. City-scale street hierarchy is inverted, so the ground outshines the twin
**Shots:** 1600/1280-01, 05, 06, 07; the crop around the hotel shows it best.
**Problem:** Core streets use `#415e6b` α.90 at 1.5 px. Ring streets use the lighter `#80959b` at 2 px, and corridor streets use `#b8c3c7` (the hotel's subject hue) at 2.5 px α.85. As a result, the brightest lines on the canvas are the corridor up Congress Ave to the Capitol and the hospital (01 top), and the Mall (07). Around the hotel, the core reads as a darker hole. The blue path (01 and 05, from the hotel to GS/R1) is thinner and dimmer than the grey streets beside it.
**Spec (`twinContext.js` `roadPrim` callers):**
- Corridor: major `#415e6b` α.90 w1.5; minor `#415e6b` α.70 w1.2; walk/steps `#415e6b` α.50 w1 (see #9). This is the core street token, so a corridor reads as "the detailed area continues along the route" without competing with it.
- Ring: major `#415e6b` α.55 w1.5; minor `#415e6b` α.35 w1.
- Rule to test in review: no context line may be lighter than `TWIN_PALETTE.street` or wider than 1.5 px. Paths, places and incidents stay the only saturated strokes.

### 2. Context never steps back: not in Ways out, not during an incident
**Shots:** 1600-05 (Ways out), 1600/1280-08 (London incident).
**Problem:** `twin.setContextDim(on)` fades only the core's surrounding-building materials, to 0.35. The phase 8 primitives have no dim path. In Ways out (05), the core neighbours fade while the corridor and ring blocks to the north keep full strength, so the ground ends up brighter than the twin. In London (08), a 1.2 km disc of 3,707 outlined buildings plus 1,740 main ways fills the canvas between the rails, leaving the red smoke mark and path as a small speck. That fails "incidents must stay the figure."
**Spec:**
- Add `contextLayer.setDim(on)` and call it everywhere `twin.setContextDim(hotelStepBack)` is called. Also call it whenever the mode chip is `INCIDENT`.
- Dimmed state: ring edges and ring roads `show=false`. Ring and corridor solids ×0.35 (move the solids onto `glassMaterial`, see #3, so they share a `fade` uniform). Corridor roads hidden, because Ways out draws its own streets.
- Exception during an incident: keep the **hospital** corridor (`kind === 'hospital'`) at normal strength. That is the one context element that is actionable then.

## P1

### 3. Corridor buildings outrank core buildings and borrow the hotel's hue
**Shots:** 1600-01/06 (Capitol and hospital corridor block), 1600-07 (the Mall).
**Problem:** Corridor fill α.14 is higher than core glass α.12. The corridor edge `#b8c3c7` is `TWIN_PALETTE.subjectGlass`/`subjectRim`, the colour reserved for the selected hotel. On top of that, the flat `PerInstanceColorAppearance` has no fresnel, so corridor volumes read as solid slabs next to the core's glass.
**Spec:** Render ring and corridor solids with the core's `glassMaterial` (fresnel, `fade` uniform):
- Corridor: glass `['#415e6b', .07]`, rim `['#80959b', .16]`, edge `#80959b` α.28.
- Ring: glass `['#415e6b', .04]`, rim `['#80959b', .10]`, edge `#80959b` α.14.
- `#b8c3c7`/`#e3e7e9` must not appear anywhere in `twinContext.js` (the corridor label text is the only exception, see #8).

### 4. The ring is wireframe-dominant and busier than the core
**Shots:** 1600-02 (top band of the ring beside the core towers), 1600/1280-08 (striped London terraces), 1600-01 (East Austin residential, right side).
**Problem:** `PolygonOutlineGeometry` draws a vertical edge at every footprint vertex. Curved and terraced OSM footprints become hatched columns, so per unit area the ring has more edge ink than the core's glass. At Neighborhood (02), the eye goes to the lattice, not the hotel.
**Spec:** Use edge density as the scale cue:
- Ring: roof outline only. One closed `PolylineGeometry` at `top` height, no verticals, no footprint.
- Corridor: roof plus footprint rings, still no verticals.
- Core: unchanged, with full edges.

This also cuts outline vertices by about 60% on the ring.

### 5. The outer edge is ragged: roads overshoot a hard building disc
**Shots:** 1600-01/05 (left and right street spokes past the buildings), 1600-07, 1600/1280-08 (hard circular disc).
**Problem:** Ring buildings stop at about 1.2 km, but ring ways are not clipped. The furthest vertex is 2,591 m in Austin, 2,138 m in DC and 1,922 m in London. The edge therefore reads as a cut-out puck with stray spokes rather than a city fading into dark.
**Spec:**
- Clip ring roads to `meta.ringM` (1200 m), in the fixture build or client-side, by splitting at the circle.
- Fade the last 300 m. Polylines: `colorsPerVertex: true`, α × (1 − smoothstep(900, 1200, d)). Buildings: per-instance α × the same factor at the centroid.
- Corridors beyond `ringM` (Kennedy Center ends at 3,934 m, DC hospital at 2,502 m, Austin hospital at 1,683 m) are exempt from the clip, but beyond `ringM` they drop to ring tokens. They should read as a thin thread out of the disc, not a second city.

### 6. Overlap and z-fighting at the core/ring seam
**Shots:** 1600-07 and 1600-08 crops around the hotel (doubled street lines inside the core); 1600-02 seam at the top of the core.
**Problem:**
- `contextPlan` dedupes only corridors against the ring. Nothing is checked against the core.
- Ring major/minor ways that touch the 400 m core: Austin 106 (81 fully inside), London 294 (274 inside), DC 164 (132 inside). They draw at +0.5 m `#80959b` over core streets at +0.3 m `#415e6b`, which doubles alpha and makes the lines shimmer at City distance. Corridor roads have no core filter at all.
- 15–37 ring buildings per city straddle the edge: a node ≤ 400 m from the hotel, centroid > 390 m. Core Overpass `around` returns them too, so they are drawn twice with coplanar walls.
- Context roads are lifted only at their vertices. They are not draped, so long segments sink into Austin's relief.

**Spec:**
- Pass the core's building and road OSM ids into `contextPlan` and skip any id already in `site.buildings` or `site.roads`.
- Clip ring and corridor roads to segments **outside `radius + 60`**, the exact complement of the core street clip at `twinScene.js:341`.
- Position context roads with `drape(coords, projector, field, 0.5, 25)`.

### 7. Corridor labels duplicate the venue beacons
**Shots:** 1600-02 ("Long Center · Day 2 19:00", "Group shelter · chaperones only" and "Corridor · Long Center" stacked within 100 px), 1600-01/06 (Texas Capitol), 1600-07 (Kennedy Center, Air and Space), 1600-08 (Festival Hall, Westminster Abbey).
**Problem:** Every venue now has two labels about 24–90 px apart. They read as two different places and double the label count at City scale. The corridor name adds nothing that the beacon and the drawn path do not already say.
**Spec:** Do not add an end label when a venue or safety beacon is within 150 m of `c.end`. Name the corridor in the Layers row and the venue tooltip instead. Keep an on-map label only for a hospital corridor that has no on-map beacon (Austin, DC).

### 8. The hospital label collides with the chip row and is off-canvas at 1280
**Shots:** 1600-01/05/06 ("Corridor · Ascension Dell Seton Medical Center at The University of Texas" struck through by the Hospital/Police/Embassy chips at y≈75), 1280-01 (not visible at all). The Texas Capitol beacon label also sits under the chips in 1280-01, with its own ring glyph over the text ("Texas Capito◯").
**Problem:**
- The label is 70 characters long.
- `createDeclutter` bounds only have `left` and `right`, so nothing keeps labels out from under the chip row or the dock.
- Corridor labels take the default priority, so they compete with the place beacons.

**Spec:**
- Text: `Hospital · {shortName}`, at most 24 characters (for example "Hospital · Dell Seton"). Font `500 11px Inter`, fill `#b8c3c7`, background `#061a23` α.80 (unchanged).
- Extend the bounds to `{left, right, top: chipRow.bottom + 8, bottom: dock.top − 8}` and add the two horizontal panel obstacles the same way as the side ones (`twinDeclutter.js` ~l.175).
- Give corridor labels the lowest `declutterPriority`, so they always yield to beacons and people.
- Pin the beacon glyph's `pixelOffset` so it sits beside the venue text, not over it.

## P2

### 9. Corridor walkways become bright triple-line bars at City scale
**Shots:** 1600-01 (top: Capitol and hospital corridor streets render as thick white bars), 1600-07 (the Mall).
**Problem:** Corridors carry 1,837 (Austin), 1,125 (London) and 2,687 (DC) footways, mostly sidewalks mapped beside each street. At 1 px `#b8c3c7` α.45, each street becomes three lines.
**Spec:** Give walk and steps instances `DistanceDisplayConditionGeometryInstanceAttribute(0, 1500)` and `#415e6b` α.50 w1 (#1). At City scale, a corridor is then carried by building tone, not line count.

### 10. Layers copy ignores the display units
**Shot:** 1600-03 (footer "Units: feet and miles"; rows say "Wider area (1.2 km)" and "about 150 m either side").
**Spec:** Use `formatLength(meters, unit)` from `twinUnits.js`:
- Imperial: "Wider area (0.75 mi)" and "about 500 ft either side of a suggested walking path".
- London keeps km/m.
- Use the same short hospital name as #8 in the "To …" list.
