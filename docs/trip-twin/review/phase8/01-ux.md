# Phase 8 UX review: the wider area and walk corridors

Reviewer: UX (review only, no code edits) · 16 Oct 2026
Inputs:
- Jesse's ask and BACKLOG.md ("More of the city in 3D": options A/B/C and the production rule).
- Shots 1600/1280 01–08 and 1600-text.json.
- twinContext.js, tripTwin.js (`loadContext`, the click handler, `setHotelStepBack`), page.js (Layers › Site rows and legends, `WAYS_KEYS`), twinSafety.js (`inView`), twinPeople.js (`beyond`), twinDeclutter.js.
- scripts/capture-trip-twin-context.mjs and the three `*.context.json` fixtures. Walk lengths below come from each corridor's recorded `path`.

## Verdict

Jesse asked for three things: (1) no more "limited 3D area then nothing", (2) the routes from the hotel to the venues, or a wider radius around the hotel, and (3) a model that covers the rally points and the emergency places.

**1. No more darkness: mostly answered at Neighborhood scale, partly at City scale.**
- Neighborhood (02) now fills to the edges.
- City (01, 07, 08) shows a clear disc with black beyond it. London (08) shows it most plainly.
- In Austin the river reads as a gap in the data.
- At a venue, the context is still centred on the hotel. A far venue (Kennedy Center, 3.9 km away) sits in black apart from a 150 m strip.

**2. Following the walk to a venue or the hospital: not answered.**
- Each corridor records the full walking path (56–326 points), but only its last point is used, for a label.
- On screen, a corridor is a band of slightly brighter buildings and streets. In Austin it shows only as "the bright streets up north". In London (08) it cannot be told apart from the ring.
- The operator sees where the venue is. They cannot see the way there.

**3. Rally points and emergency places inside what is drawn: half answered, and the UI contradicts it.**
- Austin police (0.4 mi) and the embassy (0.5 mi) now sit on drawn buildings. They are still shown as edge chips, next to a chip that reads "4 outside the mapped area".
- The DC relocation site (2.6 mi) has no corridor.
- The hospital corridor comes from the nearest OSM hospital at record time, not from the trip's approved hospital.

**The core / context boundary is not visible, and clicking across it fails silently.**
- Nothing on the map marks where the detailed, selectable twin ends.
- In the default view the corridor streets are the brightest lines on screen, brighter than the detailed core around the hotel (01).
- Clicking a plain building clears the current selection with no message.

**Overall:** the build removes most of the darkness, which is the cheap half of the ask. The valuable half, "show me the way from here to there, and where the safety places are on it", needs the fixes below. Most of them use data that is already recorded.

Note: **shot 06 ("Capitol venue, city") shows the same view as 01.** The hotel card is still open and the camera has not moved. Venue-scale behaviour was therefore not captured. Re-shoot it before the fix round (see 6).

## P0

### 1. The walk itself is not drawn: corridors are bands, not routes
**Shots:** 01, 02, 05, 07, 08 (1600 and 1280)

**Problem:**
- `contextPlan()` keeps `c.path` only as `end` (the last point) for the "Corridor · {place}" label. The recorded paths are real foot routes from `/api/route`:

  | City | Destination | Walk |
  |---|---|---|
  | Austin | Long Center | 1.07 mi |
  | Austin | Texas Capitol | 1.10 mi |
  | Austin | Dell Seton (hospital) | 1.12 mi |
  | DC | Kennedy Center | 2.77 mi |
  | DC | Air and Space | 1.04 mi |
  | DC | Howard University Hospital | 1.75 mi |
  | London | Festival Hall | 0.69 mi |
  | London | Westminster Abbey | 0.84 mi |

- What the operator sees instead:
  - Corridor buildings are drawn at fill alpha 0.14, against 0.08 for the ring.
  - Corridor main streets are 2.5 px at 0.85 alpha, against 2 px at 0.75 for the ring.
  - Inside the ring, which covers most of every corridor in London and Austin, the difference is almost invisible (08).
- The only blue line on screen is the suggested path to rally point R1. The operator can easily read it as "the way to the Long Center" (02: the blue path runs toward the "Corridor · Long Center" label and stops at R1).

**Fix:**
- Draw each corridor's `path` as its own line:
  - Token `path-walk-corridor`: 2 px, neutral `color-text-secondary` (#b8c3c7) at 0.9, dash 8/6.
  - It must not be blue, because blue stays reserved for suggested and saved paths (phase 7 rule).
  - Draw it on top of the context and below people and places.
- Put the facts at the far end in one label, replacing "Corridor · X":
  - Venue: `Long Center · 1.1 mi walk · ~22 min · Day 2 19:00`.
  - Hospital: `Hospital · Dell Seton · 1.1 mi walk · ~23 min`.
  - Walk time = path length ÷ 80 m/min, the same rate the rally-path card uses.
  - Mark it `Suggested · unverified`, as for every other suggested path.
- Make the corridor line selectable as a path. It then opens the same path card that the "Select any path" backlog item specifies (from → to, distance, time, source and run), and **Edit this path** comes later.
- **Acceptance test:** shown 01-after for 5 seconds, an operator can trace with a finger the walk from the hotel to the Capitol and to the hospital, and say which is longer.

### 2. Places and people inside the drawn area are still "outside the mapped area"
**Shots:** 01–06 (chips: `4 outside the mapped area`, `Police 0.4 mi NE`, `Embassy 0.5 mi NW`, `+3 more`), 07 (`Embassy 0.5 mi SW`)

**Problem:**
- `twinSafety.update()` pins a place only within `site.radius + 100` (about 500 m). Anything further becomes an edge chip.
- `twinPeople` counts anyone beyond the same limit as `beyond`, and they are not drawn.
- The drawn area now reaches 1.2 km, plus corridors. So:
  - Austin police (about 640 m) and the embassy (about 800 m) sit on visible buildings with no pin.
  - The chip still says "outside the mapped area" while the map visibly goes on for half a mile past them.
- The demo puts rally points up to 900 m away (`open … p.d < 900` in demoTripTwin.js). In other trips, rally points will fall in exactly this unpinned 500–1200 m band.
- This is the core of Jesse's assumption ("built out to encompass the rally points and emergency stuff"). The drawing covers them, but the UI says it does not.

**Fix:**
- One shared predicate: `isDrawn(ll) = d(hotel) ≤ ringM || distanceToAnyCorridorPath(ll) ≤ corridorM`.
- Pin every safety place, and draw every located person, for whom `isDrawn` is true. Chips are only for places beyond the drawn area.
- Rename the people chip:
  - `4 beyond the drawn area` if some of the 4 are still beyond it.
  - Otherwise the chip goes away, and the people show on the map.
- Pins in the wider area use the same glyphs at 0.85 scale. They are selectable and open the same place card. Places are trip data, not context geometry, so they keep their full rules: green only when approved, `· not approved` for drafts.
- Clicking a chip flies the camera to the place, or to the corridor end if it has one.

## P1

### 3. What gets built out is not driven by the trip's safety plan
**Shots:** 07 (`Relocation site 2.6 mi N`, no corridor), 01 (hospital corridor label only), Layers note in 03

**Problem:**
- The recorder builds one ring around the hotel. It then adds corridors to (a) each venue and (b) the nearest emergency hospital in `*.pois.json` at record time.
- It ignores the trip's `safety_points`. As a result:
  - The relocation site (4.1 km by design) never gets a corridor.
  - An embassy up to 3 km away gets none.
  - Police get none, although BACKLOG option C named them.
  - If the operator approves a different hospital, for example an adult A&E instead of London's Evelina Children's Hospital, the corridor still leads to the OSM pick.
- Only hotel → X corridors exist. The likely emergency walk starts at the venue (venue → hospital, venue → rally point), and none of those are recorded.
- The BACKLOG production rule says "site cores + path corridors + rings around safety places". The build has no rings around places or venues.

**Fix (recorder input, then the Layers note):**
- Corridor targets come from the trip:
  - hotel → every venue;
  - hotel → every approved safety place beyond the ring: hospital, relocation, embassy, police;
  - each venue → its own hospital and primary rally point.
- Add a 300 m ring around each venue and each place beyond the hotel ring.
- When a place is added or approved after recording, show it as `Hospital · not in the 3D area yet · record again`. Do not fall back to an unrelated OSM hospital.
- Layers note: list each corridor with a state, for example:
  `To Long Center · 1.1 mi ✓ · Texas Capitol · 1.1 mi ✓ · Hospital (approved) · 1.1 mi ✓ · Relocation site · not recorded`.

### 4. The boundary between the detailed twin and the plain context is invisible, and clicking across it fails silently
**Shots:** 01, 02, 08 (no visible edge to the 400 m core); 03 (the only explanation is a grey note in Layers: "not selectable")

**Problem:**
- Nothing on the map shows where "selectable" ends.
- In 08 (London) the core and ring merge into one disc. In 01 the scale grid is the only hint, and it is optional.
- In the click handler, a context primitive has no `opsRecord`, so `select(null)` runs. Clicking a plain building therefore **clears the hotel selection and closes its card**, with no message. That is the "why can't I click that building" moment, and it costs the operator their context as well.
- "Surrounding buildings" (the detailed core) and "Wider area (1.2 km)" read as two sizes of the same thing. Neither row name says which one can be clicked.

**Fix:**
- Draw the core edge on the ground: token `boundary-core`, 1 px, `color-text-secondary` at 0.35, dash 2/6.
  - Add a single label at the near edge, facing the camera: `Detailed area · 0.25 mi · select buildings inside`.
  - It shows at Neighborhood and City scale, is hidden at Building scale, and is on whenever the wider area is on.
- Click on context geometry:
  - Do **not** clear the selection.
  - Show a 3-second hint near the cursor (`role="status"`, `aria-live="polite"`): `Outline only (OpenStreetMap). Details and selection within 0.25 mi of the hotel.`
  - Add one action, `Fly to detailed area`.
- Hover: a `default` cursor over context, and the pointer cursor only over selectable geometry. This tells the operator before they click.
- Rename the Layers › Site rows:
  - `Detailed area (0.25 mi) · selectable`
  - `Wider area (0.75 mi) · outline only`
  - `Walk corridors · outline only`

### 5. The visual order is inverted: corridor streets outshine the detailed core
**Shots:** 01 / 06 (the brightest lines on screen are the corridor streets north of the hotel, toward the Capitol and the hospital), 07 (DC row houses: the ring's outlines turn into a dense mesh), 05 (in Ways out the core steps back and the context does not)

**Problem:**
- With Streets off (the default), the core shows only faint base streets. The corridor's major streets are drawn at 0.85 alpha, 2.5 px. The plain context therefore reads as more important than the twin it surrounds.
- Every ring building has an outline at 0.22 alpha. In DC (3,648 ring buildings, mostly row houses), this adds up to a texture that competes with the people and place glyphs at City scale.
- `setHotelStepBack` dims the core (`setContextDim`) but leaves the ring and corridor primitives as they are. In Ways out, the plain context therefore gains relative weight.

**Fix (token order, brightest first):**
1. people and places;
2. paths (blue);
3. walk corridor lines (fix 1);
4. detailed core;
5. corridor buildings and streets;
6. ring.

As values:

| Token | Fill | Edge / line |
|---|---|---|
| `context-ring-fill` | 0.06 | no outline. Keep outlines only for buildings ≥ 30 m tall, so the skyline still reads |
| `context-ring-street-major` | — | 0.45 |
| `context-ring-street-minor` | — | 0.25 |
| `context-corridor-fill` | 0.10 | — |
| `context-corridor-edge` | — | 0.20 |
| `context-corridor-street-major` | — | 0.55, 1.5 px. Always below the core's base streets |

- In Ways out, step the ring and corridors back with the core: multiply alpha by 0.6, so streets inside the core lead.
- **Check:** in a 01-after shot at 1600 and at 1280, the brightest linework within 100 px of the hotel is the core's, not a corridor's.

### 6. At City scale the world still ends in a hard circle, and at a venue it barely exists
**Shots:** 08 (circular disc on black), 01 (Lady Bird Lake and Zilker Park read as missing data), 07 (Kennedy Center 3.9 km west, only a 150 m strip), 06 (should show the Capitol venue but repeats 01)

**Problem:**
- The ring is a hard-edged 1.2 km disc with nothing beyond it. Jesse's complaint is pushed further out, not removed.
- Water and parks are not drawn, so a river reads as "no data".
- Context is loaded per city and centred on the hotel. At a venue twin, the side of the venue facing away from the hotel is black beyond its own 400 m core: about 0.2 mi past the Capitol, and all around the Kennedy Center.
- BACKLOG option B (streamed buildings beneath everything) was the "never ends in darkness" part. It was not built.

**Fix:**
- **Ground beneath everything:**
  - A flat, dark 2D street basemap at about 0.25 opacity, extending to the far clip.
  - Or OSM 3D tiles styled to `context-ring-fill`, if the Cesium ion plan allows.
  - Either way, beyond the ring is "plain", never "nothing".
- **Soft edge:** fade the ring's alpha linearly over its outer 150 m, and add one ground label: `Wider area ends · 0.75 mi`.
- **Water and parks:** record `natural=water`, `waterway=riverbank` and `leisure=park` polygons in the ring. Draw them as flat fills (`context-water` at 0.10, `context-park` at 0.05) so a river reads as a river.
- **Venues:** a venue's own ring (300–600 m, fix 3). When a venue is active, the hotel ring stays and the venue ring is added.
- Re-shoot 06 with the Capitol venue active (venue card open, camera on the venue) at 1600 and 1280.

## P2

### 7. Corridor ends hide under the chip bar or are clamped to the panel edge; City view does not frame them
**Shots:** 01 / 06 (`Corridor · Ascension Dell Seton…` under the chips), 05 (the Ways out banner covers it), 1280-01 (hospital end not visible at all), 07 / 1280-07 (`Kennedy Center · Day 2 19:30` pinned at the left panel edge, although the venue is 2.5 mi west and off-screen)

**Problem:**
- The declutterer treats only the left and right panels as obstacles. The top chip bar and the Ways out banner are not obstacles.
- Off-screen ends are slid sideways into view. They then look like a pin at that spot.
- City view frames the 1.2 km ring, not the corridor ends. At 1280, the hospital is not on screen at all.

**Fix:**
- Add the chip bar and the map banner to `bounds()` as top obstacles.
- When a corridor end's projected point is outside the free area, do not slide the label. Show an edge marker with an arrow and the facts: `↑ Hospital · 1.1 mi walk`. Clicking it flies to the end.
- Add the City view option "Frame all walks", or make it the default when corridors are on, with padding for the panels.
- Keyboard: each corridor in the Layers note becomes a button (`Fly to Long Center walk`) reachable by Tab. Enter flies; Esc returns.

### 8. Two labels for one place, and a glyph over the text
**Shots:** 01 / 02 / 07 / 08 (`Long Center · Day 2 19:00` and `Corridor · Long Center` stacked; London has the same pairs), 01 (`Texas Capito◯ Day 3 10:00`: the venue beacon ring sits on the label)

**Problem:**
- The venue beacon and the corridor end label mark the same point. "Corridor" is a builder's word. At the neighbourhood scale (02), the Long Center stack becomes three labels with "Group shelter · chaperones only".
- The beacon glyph has no `declutterGlyph`, so its own label can overlap it.

**Fix:**
- Merge them: the venue beacon label gets a second line with the walk facts from fix 1 (`1.1 mi walk · ~22 min`). For a venue, there is no separate corridor label.
- Only the hospital end, or any end without a pin, keeps its own label. Give it the place glyph and wording, not "Corridor".
- Give the venue beacon `declutterGlyph: 24` so labels move off it.

### 9. Metric labels in an imperial UI, and builder vocabulary
**Shots:** 03 (`Wider area (1.2 km)`, `about 150 m either side`, under `Units: feet and miles`), 01 (`Corridor · …`)

**Problem:**
- The row label and note hard-code km and m. Austin and DC show ft and mi everywhere else, including the chips (`Hospital 1.0 mi N`).
- "Corridors" is a planning term. Jesse said "routes between the hotel and the venues".

**Fix:**
- Format through `formatDistance`:
  - `Wider area (0.75 mi)` / `(1.2 km)`;
  - `about 500 ft either side` / `150 m`.
- Row: `Walks to venues & hospital`.
- Note: `Plain buildings and streets about 500 ft either side of each suggested walk · outline only`.
- In every operator-facing string, use "walk" and "path", never "route to safety" (06 never-list).

### 10. Ways out does not say that detail drops beyond the core
**Shots:** 05 (bright core streets stop at the core edge; beyond it there are faint main streets only; the banner reads "Streets and walkways from OpenStreetMap…")

**Problem:**
- In the ring, footways, alleys and service roads are filtered out (`['major','minor']`). Corridors keep footways.
- An empty space beyond the core in Ways out can look like "no way through", when it means "not drawn".
- The banner describes the core's data only.

**Fix:**
- Add one line to the banner: `Full streets and walkways within 0.25 mi · main streets only to 0.75 mi · full streets along each walk`.
- The Streets legend gets a matching note row, `Beyond the detailed area: main streets only`.
- Pair this with fix 4's boundary line, so the change in detail has a visible edge.

## Keep

- **On by default, with neutral greys only.** The context never takes blue, green or amber, so status colours keep their meaning. Holding this rule is what keeps the added geometry from turning into noise.
- **Not selectable, merged primitives, built asynchronously.** This is the right call for the core/context split. It only needs to be made visible (fix 4).
- **Building scale (04) is unaffected.** The context sits behind the hotel and costs nothing at the scale where the floor work happens.
- **The Layers note is honest and specific:** building count, source, "not selectable", "heights mapped or estimated". Keep this pattern and add the corridor states (fix 3).
- **Corridors are taken before the ring,** so each building is drawn once with the right look, and nothing duplicates the core (`outsideCore`). Keep this when venue and place rings are added.
- **Incident view (08) still leads with the incident:** the reported hotel, JP and the path to R1 stay readable over 3,700 context buildings. Fixes 1 and 5 must keep it that way.
- **Ways out turns the context on and restores it on exit** (it is in `WAYS_KEYS`), consistent with "adds, never hides" from phase 7.
- **The recorder writes the attribution and `fetchedAt` into the fixture.** Show `fetchedAt` in the Layers note (`recorded 16 Oct`) as the streets layer does.
