Inputs: BACKLOG.md ("More of the city in 3D", options B and C); 06-layer-taxonomy.md (§2.2 Site rows, §3.5 SIT-07/SIT-08, §3.7, §3.11 never-list and synonym ring, §11–13). Code: `twinContext.js` (whole file; corridor labels 214–234), `page.js` (`WAYS_KEYS` 2712–2722, Views note 3023, legends `keys()` 2904–2933, `GROUPS` Site 2969–2981), `tripTwin.js` (`contextState` 197–228, people-beyond chip 948), `twinPeople.js` 446–455 (pin limit), `scripts/capture-trip-twin-context.mjs` (ring radius 15, corridor buffer 16, hospital pick 129–143), `twin.html` (toolbar title 173, help 346–408).

Result: **2 P0 · 5 P1 · 3 P2.**

The data choice is right. Plain, unclickable volumes give the view an edge instead of "nothing", and they are neutral grey with no new colour. The problems are about vocabulary and boundaries:
- the on-map "Corridor · {place}" label reads as the name of a route, and in the shots it sits beside the blue rally path;
- the map now visibly covers ground where people are **not** drawn, while the chip still says "outside the mapped area";
- Surrounding buildings, Wider area and Streets give the operator no distance to tell where detail ends.

## M1 · P0 · "Corridor · {place}" on the map reads as a route name, and it attaches to the wrong line

**Shots:** 02 and 03 (`Corridor · Long Center` about 40 px from the bend of the blue path to R1); 01, 05 and 06 (`Corridor · Texas Capitol` under the venue label; `Corridor · Ascension Dell Seton Medical Center at The University of Texas` clipped behind the chip row and the Ways out tag); 07 (`Corridor · Kennedy Center`, `Corridor · Air and Space`); 08 (London **incident**: `Corridor · Festival Hall`, `Corridor · Westminster Abbey` on screen while the incident panel says "If the group must move: rally point (primary)").

**Problem.**
- The only line drawn near the label is the blue suggested path, and that path goes to **R1**, not to Long Center. A label next to a line names that line, so the operator reads "this blue path is the corridor to Long Center". That is wrong twice: the destination is wrong, and a corridor is not a path.
- "Corridor" is route vocabulary in this domain ("safe corridor", "evacuation corridor", "humanitarian corridor"). 06 SIT-08 already forbids the word for interiors, and 01 §2 row 3 uses it for indoor corridors. On the map, outdoors, it does the job of "route", which 06 §3.11 bans.
- The label is pinned at the corridor's **destination** (`c.path.at(-1)`). For venues it repeats the venue's own label one line lower ("Long Center · Day 2 19:00" / "Corridor · Long Center"). For the hospital it is the only mark at that point, because the hospital itself is an edge chip ("Hospital 1.0 mi N"). The hospital is therefore named on the map only as a "Corridor".
- In an incident (08), the labels name **venues** as the visible far-off destinations, right beside the "If the group must move" guidance.

**Spec.**
- Remove the corridor end labels (`twinContext.js` 214–234). Venues already have their own label. Coverage does not need a name on the map; the legend lists it (M3).
- If the hospital at a corridor end is inside the City frame, draw its **Safety** glyph and its name, `Hospital · {short name}`, as for any approved place. It is a Safety place and should never show up as a "Corridor".
- Never put any label at or along the corridor strip. A corridor is coverage, not an object. (A label is also why the long hospital name collided with the chip row.)
- Add "corridor" to the 06 §3.11 never-list for outdoor use too (§16 rows below).

## M2 · P0 · "4 outside the mapped area" is now false: people inside the visible wider area are not drawn

**Shots:** every shot (`4 outside the mapped area`; London `1 outside the mapped area`); 01, 05 and 07 show mapped buildings out to about 0.75 mi.

**Problem.**
- The chip wording came from the phase 1 fix list ("outside the mapped area", not "beyond this view"). At that point the mapped area ended at the detailed twin. Pins are still cut at `site.radius + 100` (`twinPeople.js` 446, about 0.3 mi). The map now draws buildings and streets out to 1,200 m around the hotel, and further along the corridors.
- So a person 0.5 mi away, standing on a drawn street in a drawn corridor, has no pin, and the chip says they are outside the mapped area. That breaks Q1 ("Where are my people?"). The map looks complete exactly where it is not.

**Spec.** The chip and the map must use one boundary. Two options:
- **A (preferred):** draw pins for located people out to the wider-area edge (ring radius) and inside corridor strips, at Neighborhood and City scale, with the same pin rules. The chip then counts only people beyond that: `{n} beyond the wider area · not drawn`.
- **B (wording-only fallback):** keep the pin limit and change the chip to `{n} not drawn · more than {0.3 mi} from the hotel`. Make the chip open the list of those people (each `Away · {1.4 mi} NE of hotel`). Add `No people pins beyond {0.3 mi}` to the Wider area legend.
- Either way, retire "mapped area" (§16). Since phase 8, every visible building is "mapped", so the phrase no longer marks a boundary.

## M3 · P1 · Rename "Corridors to venues & hospital": the legend promises a path the operator cannot see

**Shots:** 03 and 1280-03 (legend: "Buildings and streets along the walk to each venue and the nearest hospital" / "To Long Center, Texas Capitol, Ascension Dell Seton Medical Center at The University of Texas · about 150 m either side of a suggested walking path").

**Problem.**
- 06 §11 defines "Suggested path" as a router path **drawn in blue**. The corridor's centre line is a foot-router path recorded once at fixture time (`capture-trip-twin-context.mjs` 147). It is **not drawn**, and it is never checked against avoid-areas, hazards or the C5 coupling. The legend still calls it "a suggested walking path", so the operator looks for it, and the only blue line on screen (to R1) becomes that path (M1).
- "Along the walk to each venue" says the group will walk this way. The code comment says the same: "a step brighter, with walkways — where the group would walk" (`twinContext.js` 199). That is a route claim made by styling.
- At 1280 the two legends take 9 lines and push Streets and Walkways below the fold (1280-03).

**Spec.**
- Row label: **`Toward venues & hospital`**. It describes coverage by direction, and it contains no path, route or corridor noun.
- Legend line: `More buildings and streets toward each venue and the hospital · map detail only, not a path`.
- Note line: `To {Long Center}, {Texas Capitol}, {Dell Seton Medical Center} · about {500 ft} either side of the walking streets · OpenStreetMap, recorded {date}`.
- Use the place's **short** name (the same string as its Safety card or venue label, ≤ 28 characters). Never use the raw OSM `name`.
- Never "a suggested walking path" or "the walk" for the undrawn centre line. If the operator wants the line, the existing verb applies: the venue's `PATH TO {venue}` / `Plan your own path`.

## M4 · P1 · Defaults: both on is right, but corridor emphasis applies in every mode, including incident and Ways out

**Shots:** 05 (Ways out, City: the core buildings step back; the corridor strip north to the Capitol stays the brightest street band beyond 0.25 mi); 08 (incident: corridors on, labelled).

**Problem.**
- Default on for **Wider area** is right. It answers Jesse's "then there is nothing", it is faint (fill α .08), it has no labels and it cannot be picked.
- Default on for **corridors** is right for monitoring and Advance, where the walk to a venue is the planning question.
- `WAYS_KEYS` (page.js 2712) forces `context` and `corridors` on. `setHotelStepBack` dims the core surrounding buildings, but the context layer has no dim (its API is only `set`/`dispose`). So in Ways out the corridor strips become relatively **more** prominent while everything else steps back. The view meant for "how do we get out" then highlights streets toward **venues**. Ways out adds and emphasises the street network, and it should not pick directions.

**Spec (defaults).**

| Layer | Monitoring | Incident | Advance | Ways out |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wider area | On | On | On | Unchanged (not in the forced set) |
| Toward venues & hospital | On, emphasised | On, **drawn in the Wider area style** (no step brighter) | On, emphasised | **Not forced on.** If on, drawn in the Wider area style |
| Corridor labels | None (M1) | None | None | None |

- Remove `context` and `corridors` from `WAYS_KEYS`. Phase 7 M3 stays: the view restores only what it touched.
- Add a dim/neutral state to the context layer, used by incident mode and Ways out.

## M5 · P1 · "Surrounding buildings" vs "Wider area": no distances, and "detailed area" is never defined

**Shots:** 03 and 1280-03 (`Surrounding buildings` / "Mapped height · fainter = approximate"; `Wider area (1.2 km)` / "Plain buildings and main streets beyond the detailed area").

**Problem.**
- Both names mean "around the hotel". Nothing on screen says which one is nearer, or that the first is clickable and height-coded while the second is not.
- "Beyond the detailed area" points at a term that appears nowhere else. The Surrounding buildings row does not call itself detailed.
- "(1.2 km)" does not say whether it is a radius or a diameter, or around what. It is a radius **around the hotel**, also at venue sites (capture caveat), so at the Capitol site the wider area is off-centre.

**Spec.** Keep both row names (Surrounding buildings is in 06 §2.2). Anchor both by a distance band from the hotel, and define "detailed area" once:
- `Surrounding buildings` · legend `Detailed area · within {0.25 mi} of the hotel · click a building for height and source` · key `Mapped height · fainter = approximate`.
- `Wider area` (no figure in the label) · legend `{0.25}–{0.75 mi} from the hotel · plain buildings and main streets · no details on click`.
- At a venue site, both legends say `of the hotel` explicitly, never "of this site", until venue rings exist.

## M6 · P1 · "1.2 km" and "150 m" ignore Display › Units

**Shots:** 03 (Units footer: "feet and miles · change in Display (D)"; chips "Hospital 1.0 mi N"; rows "Wider area (1.2 km)", "about 150 m either side").

**Problem.** 06 §13 says Display › Units governs "every distance on screen and in copied text". The new strings are hard-coded metric in a feet-and-miles session. They sit two rows from the Paths legend, which already uses `formatRadius` correctly.

**Spec.** Build both from the recorded meta (`ringM`, `corridorM`) with `formatRadius`: `0.75 mi` / `1.2 km`; `about 500 ft` / `about 150 m`. Render the legends on Display change, as the Paths key already does.

## M7 · P1 · Ways out is described four different ways, and none says where the detail ends

**Shots:** 03 (Views note); 05 (in-map tag); `twin.html` 173 (toolbar title) and 379–384 (help W row).

**Problem.**
- Views note: "Turns on streets, walkways, places, paths and avoid-areas". Toolbar title: "streets, walkways, entrances, places and paths". Help: "… suggested and saved paths stand out; floors and hazards stay". Tag: "Streets and walkways from OpenStreetMap…". None of them names buildings, the wider area or the corridors. The code forces all three today.
- Streets and Walkways cover only the detailed area (0.25 mi). In Ways out at City scale (05), the bright network stops there, and beyond it there are main streets only, with no walkways. With nothing to say so, the operator may read "no walkways out there" as a fact about the ground instead of a limit of the map.

**Spec.** Use one canonical description, with its distance taken from units:
- Views note: `Turns on buildings, streets, walkways, places, paths and avoid-areas; the scene steps back. Floors and hazards stay as they are. Streets and walkways in detail within {0.25 mi} of the hotel; main streets only beyond.`
- Toolbar title: `Ways out view (W) · streets, walkways, entrances, places and paths stand out · detail within {0.25 mi} · W or Esc returns`.
- In-map tag: insert `· detail within {0.25 mi} of the hotel, main streets only beyond` after "private access".
- Help W row: `Ways out view: buildings, streets, walkways, mapped entrances, places, suggested and saved paths and avoid-areas stand out; floors and hazards stay; street detail ends {0.25 mi} from the hotel · W or Esc (after cards and selection) returns`.

## M9 · P2 · Legend notes: "not selectable", "estimated" heights, "nearest" hospital, no recorded date

**Shots:** 03; code `twinContext.js` 13–17; capture script 129–143.

**Problem and spec.**
- "not selectable" is UI jargon. Use `no details on click`.
- "heights mapped or estimated": a building with neither `height` nor `levels` is drawn at a **9 m stand-in** (`contextHeight`). 06 SIT-07 forbids presenting that as a number. Use `heights mapped, from levels, or unknown (drawn {30 ft})`.
- "the nearest hospital": the recorder sorts OSM `emergency=yes` first, then distance. 06 §12 `Nearest listed hospital` means nearest in a straight line among hospitals on file, so the two can name different hospitals. SAF-27 also forbids implying ER status. Drop "nearest" and name it (`to {Dell Seton Medical Center}`). Either align the recorder to the §12 rule, or never show "nearest".
- Provenance: the source line shows only `OSM buildings · recorded {core date}`. The context fixture has its own `meta.fetchedAt`. Add `Wider area · recorded {date}` to the corridor/wider note, or to the source line when it differs.
