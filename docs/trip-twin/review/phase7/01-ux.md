# Phase 7 UX review: streets, walkways and the "Ways out" view

Reviewer: UX (review only, no code edits) · 16 Oct 2026
Inputs: BACKLOG.md (first section), Jesse's ask, shots 1600/1280 01–09 and 1600-text.json, twinStreets.js, page.js (`toggleWaysOut`, Layers › Site rows and legends, keyboard handler), tripTwin.js (`setHotelStepBack`), twinScene.js (`setHotelDim`), twinData.js (Overpass query and parser), twin.html (button, help), the end of tripTwin.css. Tag counts come from scripts/fixtures/trip-twin/{austin,london,dc}.overpass.json.

## Verdict

Jesse's question: "if I need to quickly see what the best escape routes are, I might toggle just the buildings and the roads." Ways out answers part of it. Streets are now named, sized by class and easy to find (02, 03). With the blue path on, an operator can trace the way to the primary rally point.

It does not yet answer "how do we get out, and where can we walk" in a few seconds:

- **It barely changes the picture.** 01 and 04 look almost the same. The view adds two layers, and the eye still lands on the glowing hotel and the people markers.
- **It does not answer "which way out of the hotel".** No hotel door is visible in any shot, and in Austin OpenStreetMap does not map the Fairmont's doors at all.
- **Some of the brightest lines cannot be walked.** I‑35 in Austin, the 3rd Street Tunnel in DC and London's tunnels are drawn as bright surface streets.
- **Sidewalks make the streets less defined.** Every street gets two extra dashed lines next to it.
- **In an incident (07) the view is the same as in calm (04).** The map does not lead with the move plan that the right panel already states.

The fixes below turn the preset into a real "ways out" picture: doors, then streets you can walk, then the path, then the rally point.

---

## P0

### 1. Ways out barely changes the picture; "just the buildings and the roads" is not what you see
**Shots:** 01 vs 04 (1600 and 1280), 05
**Problem:**
- `toggleWaysOut()` turns on streets, walkways, buildings, safety and suggested paths. It turns off grid, contours, floors and weather. In the default state (01), grid, contours and weather are already off. The real change is two added layers, so the screen gets busier, not simpler.
- "The hotel glass steps back" is `fade = 0.4` on the hotel material. With the glow halo, the group-zone disc and the people stack in front, the hotel in 04 and 05 is as bright as in 01. At 1280 (1280-04) it is the brightest object on screen.
- Both side panels stay as they are, and every place, venue and street label stays at full strength. Nothing on the map tells the operator the view is different. The only signs are the chip suffix `· WAYS OUT VIEW` and the footer `Grid and elevation off`.

**Fix (figure and ground swap):**
- **Surrounding buildings:** footprint outlines with a low fill (about 25% of today's opacity), no extrusion glow. The streets become the figure and the buildings the ground.
- **Hotel:** edges only, with no glass fill and no halo. Keep the reported-floor band if there is an incident.
- **People:** they stay but compact. Show one group chip at the hotel (`19 · hotel`) plus anyone outside it, instead of the stacked avatars that cover the doors (see 3). Keep the selection, Needs Attention and "never red or green on people" rules.
- **Labels:** only these stay at full strength: hotel doors, streets that touch the hotel or that a path uses, the rally points, and the hospital. Every other place or venue label drops to `--tt-text-2`.
- **Map banner:** a small in-map banner, top-left of the 3D area: `Ways out view · buildings, streets, walkways, places and paths · Esc returns`.
- **Acceptance test:** shown 04-after for 3 seconds, a new operator can point to (a) a hotel door, (b) the street it opens onto, and (c) the path to the primary rally point.

### 2. Roads you cannot walk on are drawn as the strongest "ways out"
**Shots:** 04, 07 (`North Interstate 35` and `Purple Heart Trail`, both I‑35, drawn in the widest and brightest "major" style next to the hotel); 09 (`3rd Street Tunnel`, an underground freeway, drawn and labelled like a surface street); 08 (London, 32 `tunnel=yes` ways drawn at ground level)
**Problem:**
- `roadClass()` puts `motorway` and `trunk` in MAJOR, the brightest class. Austin has 12 motorway ways and DC has 12.
- The parser (`parseOverpassElements`) keeps only `highway` and `name`. It drops `tunnel`, `layer`, `bridge`, `access` and `foot`. As a result:
  - tunnels are drawn on the surface;
  - `access=private` ways (Austin 16, London 22, DC 8) are drawn as walkable;
  - `foot=no` ways (London 8, DC 20) are drawn as walkable.
- In a view whose job is "where can we walk", a bright line is read as "go this way". That is a wrong answer, not just a missing one.

**Fix:**
- Keep `tunnel`, `layer`, `bridge`, `access`, `foot`, `footway`, `crossing`, `incline`, `wheelchair` and `ramp` in the parser.
- Add a road class `nowalk`: motorway, trunk without a sidewalk, `foot=no`, `access=no` or `access=private`.
  - Draw it as a thin dark-grey line (below `service` in brightness) with no name emphasis.
  - Its label gets a suffix: `I‑35 · no walking`.
  - Legend row: `No walking (motorway, private, foot=no)`.
- **Tunnels:** hidden in Ways out. In normal Streets view, draw them faint and dotted with a `tunnel` suffix on the label.
- **Bridges:** a short casing tick at each end. Bridges are pinch points on a way out, for example the Lady Bird Lake crossings near the Austin rally point. Austin has 30 `bridge=yes` ways.
- **Test:** in twinStreets.test.mjs, assert that `motorway`, `foot=no` and `tunnel=yes` never render in the major, minor or walk styles.

### 3. "Which way out of the hotel?" is not answered: no hotel doors, and none mapped in Austin
**Shots:** 04, 05 (Building view: no door glyph on the hotel's edge; the white dots that are visible belong to other buildings), 07, 08, 09
**Problem:**
- Entrances are fetched within 150 m of the hotel. The fixtures hold:

  | Site | Entrances within 150 m | Tagged `main` |
  |---|---|---|
  | Austin | 4 (shop 2, yes 1, secondary 1) | none; none is clearly the Fairmont's |
  | London | 4 | 3 |
  | DC | 3 | 2 |

- Doors are 5–7 px white dots at ground level. At the hotel they sit under the people stack, the zone disc and the glow, so they cannot be seen in any shot.
- An `entrance=emergency` door would look the same as a shop door.
- The suggested path starts at the hotel's corner (04, 07), not at a door.
- The first thing a chaperone needs is which door to use and which street it opens onto. The view does not show it.

**Fix:**
- Make hotel doors a first-class object in Ways out. Each door gets an outward arrow glyph on the hotel footprint edge, at least 12 px, high contrast on `--tt-bg`, with a kind: main, side, emergency or garage.
- Each door has a short label naming the street it opens onto: `Main door → Red River St`.
- Sources:
  - OpenStreetMap where it exists, labelled `OpenStreetMap · not verified`.
  - Otherwise an empty state in the Ways out card: `Hotel doors not mapped · Add doors from the hotel's evacuation plan`.
  - Operator entry follows the London floor-numbering pattern (`Operator entry · not verified · used only for this trip`). Click the hotel edge to place a door, or use Tab / arrow keys to step around the edge.
- Suggested and saved paths start from a chosen door. The path card names it: `From main door (Red River St)`.
- In Ways out, people markers declutter away from door glyphs.

## P1

### 4. Sidewalks double every street, so streets get less defined, not more
**Shots:** 03, 04, 08, 09 (each street becomes a "railroad track" of dashes; in DC and London the dashes cover most of the ground)
**Problem:**
- Most walk lines run along a street or cross one:

  | Site | Footways | Of which `footway=sidewalk` | Of which `footway=crossing` |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Austin | 550 | 290 | 126 |
  | DC | 623 | 204 | 155 |
  | London | 453 | 113 | 95 |

- The walkway colour (#eff7f3 at 0.75) is as bright as a major street (#dfe6e8 at 0.85), and both are pixel-width lines. So the street hierarchy Jesse asked for ("more defined") is buried as soon as walkways are on, which Ways out always does.

**Fix:**
- **Sidewalks:** do not draw them as separate lines in Ways out. Draw the street with a thin lighter edge on the side that has a sidewalk instead.
- **Crossings:** draw only `crossing=traffic_signals` and `crossing=marked`, as two short ticks across the street.
- **Footways:** standalone footways, paths and trails keep the dash. Add `path` to the Overpass `HIGHWAYS` list; `WALK` matches it but the query never fetches it, so park and river trails are missing.
- **Pedestrian areas** (`area=yes`, 14 in DC): a low-alpha filled polygon, not an outline.
- **Streets:** draw them as ribbons with real width in metres (for example major ≈14 m, minor ≈9 m, service ≈5 m), filled slightly lighter than the ground with a 1 px light edge. They then read as streets at Building and Neighborhood zoom and do not blur into blobs at City zoom.
- **Order by brightness:** major > minor > walkway > service > no‑walk. Check that each step is visibly different at 1280.

### 5. Street names land in the wrong places: on the hotel, through buildings, and not along the path
**Shots:** 04, 05, 07 (`Red River Street` sits on the hotel roof); 05 (labels float over the glass); 04 and 07 (the streets the blue path follows are not named along it)
**Problem:**
- `streetLabels()` puts one label at the midpoint of each street's longest segment.
- `disableDepthTestDistance = ∞` draws labels through buildings.
- The streets that matter most are not given priority: the one the hotel's doors face, and the ones the path uses. The operator cannot say "walk down X Street" without reading a map.

**Fix:**
- **Label priority:**
  1. Streets touching the hotel footprint, placed at the point nearest the hotel and pushed outside the hotel's screen rectangle.
  2. Streets used by the visible paths, placed along the path.
  3. All other streets.
- Run labels through the same declutter pass as the markers (twinDeclutter.js).
- In the path card, list the streets in order: `Main door → Red River St (south) → E Cesar Chavez St (west) → … → Rally point (primary) · 0.4 mi · 10–13 min`. This is the line a chaperone can read out over the phone.

### 6. Steps cannot be seen on the map, and paths do not know about them (wheelchairs)
**Shots:** 03–09 (no flight of steps can be told apart from a walkway in any shot)
**Problem:**
- Steps get a 4 px dash at width 3.5 in near-white, almost the same as the walkway dash. The fixtures have 44 flights in Austin, 45 in DC and 19 in London.
- Routing (twinRoutes.js, `foot` profile) never flags steps. Path cards cannot say whether a path is step-free.
- The data is partly there: Austin has 29 `wheelchair=designated` ways, 22 `ramp=separate` and 52 `incline` tags. The parser drops all of them.

**Fix:**
- **On the map:** a distinct shape that stays neutral, not amber. Steps become a "ladder" of short ticks across the line, labelled `Steps` at Building zoom.
- **On a selected path:** steps become an attention item in the path card (amber is allowed here, because it means attention):
  - `Steps on this path: 2 flights (Trinity St; near rally point)`, or
  - `Step-free: not known · OpenStreetMap has no step data here`.
- **In the path editor:** add `Plan a step-free path`. It avoids `steps` segments as temporary avoid-areas until a wheelchair routing profile exists.
- **Later:** a mobility need on the roster turns step-free on by default.

### 7. In an incident, Ways out looks exactly like calm, and avoid-areas are not forced on
**Shots:** 07 vs 04
**Problem:**
- The right panel already gives the plan: `If the group must move: rally point (primary) · 0.4 mi · walk 10–13 min · details below`. The map does not lead with it. The primary path, the alternate and the other venue paths all have the same weight, there is no direction of travel, and nothing connects the card line to the map.
- `toggleWaysOut()` sets `paths` on but leaves `customVis.saved` and `customVis.avoid` as they were. The backlog says saved paths stay on top. If an operator had turned avoid-areas off, Ways out keeps them hidden during an incident.
- Jesse asked about blocked or avoid areas. Today the demo has `avoid-areas 0`, and nothing in Ways out says so.

**Fix:**
- **In Incident mode, Ways out shows the move plan:**
  - The primary path is drawn thicker, with direction chevrons from the hotel door to the rally point.
  - The alternate is dashed. Other paths drop to 40%.
  - The rally point label is larger, and `· approved` comes from the existing approval data.
- **Ways out forces on** saved paths and avoid-areas. Paths within 1 km of a heat detection keep the existing amber treatment.
- **The right panel's "If the group must move" line becomes a Ways out card:** door → streets → rally point, time, steps (see 6), and `Avoid-areas on this trip: 0 · Add`. The empty state is explicit, and closures are honest: `Streets from OpenStreetMap · closures and access not checked`.
- **Inside the hotel** (smoke on floor 9), keep to what we know: `Inside the building: follow hotel staff and the posted evacuation plan`. Do not draw guessed indoor paths.

## P2

### 8. City view in Ways out ends in darkness; one-way and bridges
**Shots:** 06
**Problem:**
- At City zoom the twin is a disc of about 460 m. Streets stop at its edge, and street names are culled (display distance 1800 m for minor and 3200 m for major streets).
- The venues (`Long Center · Day 2 19:00`, `Texas Capitol · Day 3 10:00`) and the rally points float with no streets between them.

**Fix:**
- Switching on Ways out while in City view frames Neighborhood, around the hotel, the approved places and the path ends.
- If the operator chooses City anyway, show a note: `Streets shown within 0.3 mi of the hotel`.
- Corridors along paths are already in the backlog ("More of the city", option C). Ways out is the strongest reason to do them first.
- **One-way** (Jesse's example): it does not matter for walking. Show one-way arrows only when a drive or coach path (`car` profile) is selected. The data is there: 69 / 169 / 133 `oneway=yes` ways in Austin / London / DC.

### 9. At 1280, the map gets about 650 px and Ways out keeps both full panels
**Shots:** 1280-04, 1280-07
**Problem:** In calm, the right panel in Ways out is the hotel card with a 37-row floor stack, which is not what "getting out" needs. The 3D area between the panels is about half the screen.
**Fix:**
- In Ways out, the right panel leads with the Ways out card from 7: doors, path, rally point, steps, avoid-areas.
- The Floors section starts collapsed, except in Incident mode, where the reported-floor rows stay open.
- The left rail is unchanged.

### 10. State and feedback: what Ways out changed, what Esc restores, the key hint
**Shots:** 04 (footer `Grid and elevation off`; the Layers panel does not show which rows Ways out controls); twin.html (button title only)
**Problem:**
- Esc restores the saved layer state and silently throws away any layer changes made while in Ways out.
- The Layers panel gives no sign that Ways out is driving its rows.
- The button has no visible key hint, although the popover headers show theirs (`Layers L`).
- Nothing is announced to screen readers when the view changes.

**Fix:**
- In Ways out, the Layers header reads: `Ways out view is setting these layers · Esc returns to yours`. Rows driven by Ways out get a small `W` tag.
- A layer the operator changes during Ways out is kept on exit.
- The button gets a visible `W` kbd hint, the same as the popovers.
- An `aria-live="polite"` message on toggle: `Ways out view on: buildings, streets, walkways, places and paths. Esc returns.` and `Ways out view off: your layers are back.`

## Jesse's questions, answered in one line each
- **Which way out of the hotel?** Not answered today. See 3: doors with street names, from OpenStreetMap or operator-entered, as the start of every path.
- **The hotel's own entrances?** Not visible today, and not mapped in Austin. See 3.
- **Blocked or avoid areas?** Already exist as avoid-areas, but Ways out does not force them on and does not show an empty state. See 7.
- **One-way?** Not needed for walking. Show only for drive or coach paths. See 8.
- **Steps for wheelchairs?** Data partly exists. Make steps distinct, flag them on paths, add a step-free option. See 6.

## Keep
- **Colour discipline:** streets and walkways in neutral greys and whites, blue only for paths, green only for approved places. Steps were kept neutral rather than amber.
- **Wording:** "Ways out" in the button, chip and help. No "escape route" or "safe" anywhere on screen.
- **Honest legend notes:** `Street names from OpenStreetMap` and `OpenStreetMap · not checked for closures or access`.
- **Keyboard parity:** W toggles, Esc returns (after the weather "back to now" Esc, and not while a popover or the path editor is open), `aria-pressed` on the button, and an entry in the help overlay.
- **Exact restore:** the previous layer state (streets, walkways, buildings, floors, safety, paths, grid, contours, weather) is saved and restored.
- **People and incidents stay on.** The mode chip suffix works in both Monitoring and Incident (`INCIDENT · WAYS OUT VIEW`).
- **Street drawing:** major, minor and service read clearly by width on their own (02). One name per street with a dark label background avoids repeated-label clutter.
- **Cheap toggling:** one batched primitive per road class, plus a label data source.
- **Honest place labels:** the secondary rally point stays grey `not approved`, and `Group shelter · chaperones only` is unchanged.
- **The incident card line** `If the group must move: rally point (primary) · 0.4 mi · walk 10–13 min`. It is the right seed for the Ways out card.
