# Phase 7 · UI / visual review (streets, walkways, Ways out)

Reviewer: UI designer (review only, no code edits) · 16 Oct 2026
Inputs: shots `review/phase7/shots/1600-01…09`, `1280-01…09`; `twinStreets.js` STYLE + labels; `page.js` Site legend KEY + `toggleWaysOut`; `tripTwin.css` (Ways out pressed state, `.tt-swatch`); `twin.html` toolbar.

**Verdict.** The street layer is a real improvement: major streets read as the spine in Austin and London, and the names help orientation. **Ways out does not do its job yet.** It renders almost the same frame as Layers › Streets + Walkways (a pixel diff of 03 against 04 changes about 1 % of map pixels). Walkways are about as bright as major streets. The blue path, which should be the most visible line on the map in this view, is now less bright than every street. In DC and at City scale the view turns into a white mesh.

Measured contrast (line blended at its alpha over field `#061a23` / over building fill `≈#2a4a59`):

| Line | Now | Over buildings |
|---|---|---|
| major `#dfe6e8` .85, 4 px | 10.4:1 | 5.9:1 |
| minor `#b8c3c7` .8, 2.5 px | 6.7:1 | 4.0:1 |
| service `#9aa8ac` .6, 1.5 px | 3.4:1 | **2.4:1 (fails 3:1)** |
| walkway `#eff7f3` .75, 1.5 px dash | **9.5:1 (only 1.09× below major)** | 5.7:1 |
| steps `#e3e7e9` .95, 3.5 px | 13.0:1 | 7.0:1 |
| path `#3b82f6` | **4.8:1 (darker than every street)** | 2.6:1 |

---

## P0

**P0-1 · Walkways compete with the street hierarchy and create a white mesh in dense cities.**
Shots: 1600-04, 1600-08 (London), 1600-09 / 1280-09 (DC). In DC every street is drawn with a dashed sidewalk on each side, so each street looks like three lines and the blocks turn into a grid of white dashes. Walkways are 9.5:1, almost the same as major streets (10.4:1). Nothing tells the eye "road first, footway second".
Spec:
- `STYLE.walk`: `{ width: 1.25, color: '#b8c3c7', alpha: 0.5 }` (≈3.4:1 on field, clearly below minor at 6:1).
- Do not draw `footway=sidewalk` ways (OSM tag) when the Streets layer is on. If the fixture does not carry `footway`, drop walk ways that run within 12 m of, and within 15° of parallel to, a drawn street. Keep `footway=crossing` as 2 px solid `#b8c3c7` α .7 segments, because crossings are the useful part for ways out.
- Keep pedestrian areas, park paths and cut-throughs. Those are the actual "other ways out".

**P0-2 · The blue paths lose top billing in the view built to show them.**
Shots: 1600-04 and 1280-04 (the path runs into the major street at the hotel and reads as one more line), 1280-09 (the DC rally path is almost lost among white), 1600-06 (at City scale the path is swallowed). BACKLOG says "suggested and saved paths stay visible on top." Today they sit lower in the hierarchy than the roads.
Spec (Ways out only, blue token unchanged):
- Draw a dark casing under every suggested, alternate and saved path: `#061a23` α .9, path width + 4 px, depth-tested the same way. It cuts the street beneath, so the blue reads as a separate line.
- Path width +1 px while Ways out is on (with the glow core at `--tt-path-text` `#93bafa`, about 9:1).
- Draw order: casing, then path, above all street classes. Street labels must never draw over a path. If a label box intersects the path's screen polyline, hide the label.

## P1

**P1-3 · Ways out is nearly identical to "Streets + Walkways on". The network does not pop.**
Shots: 1600-03 vs 1600-04 (about 1 % pixel change, mostly the grid). The hotel "dim" cannot be seen at Neighborhood: the crown is still bright white. The tall translucent towers (the large tower left of the hotel in Austin) still veil Red River, Trinity and Davis streets.
Spec, in Ways out:
- Surrounding buildings: fill α × 0.4, edge colour `--tt-ring` `#365462` (from `#80959b`). Buildings become context and the streets become the figure.
- Hotel: fill α .15, edges `--tt-edge-ctl` `#80959b` at 1 px (not white). People pins and floor callouts stay at full strength.
- Streets in this view: major `#eff7f3` α .95, 4.5 px (14.8:1); minor `#b8c3c7` α .75, 2.5 px (6:1). This gives a 2.4× luminance step major to minor (today 1.55×).
- Acceptance check: a diff of "Streets + Walkways on" against "Ways out" should differ visibly in the building layer, not only the grid.

**P1-4 · City scale becomes a white tangle.**
Shot: 1600-06 / 1280-06. Line widths are fixed in pixels, so at about 3 km of camera range the 4 px majors, the dashed walkways and the entrance dots merge into one white blob around the hotel. The blue path and the rally markers disappear into it.
Spec: give each street class a `DistanceDisplayConditionGeometryInstanceAttribute`:
- walk, steps and entrances: hide when camera range > 1500 m.
- service: hide when range > 2000 m.
- At City scale, use major 2.5 px and minor 1.5 px. Alternatively, build a second, thinner set of primitives and swap on the scale change.

City-scale Ways out should show major and minor streets, places and paths only.

**P1-5 · The street classes are too close to each other, service fails 3:1, and the legend hides the hierarchy.**
Shots: 1600-03 / 04 (major and minor are separated only by 1.5 px and a small grey shift), 1600-02 (the legend: `.tt-swatch` draws every class as an identical 14 × 2 px line).
Spec:
- Apply the P1-3 majors in both normal and Ways out modes. Minor: 2.5 px `#b8c3c7` α .75. Service: 1.5 px `#9aa8ac` α .85, which gives 5.6:1 on field and 3.2:1 over building fill (today 2.4:1).
- Legend swatches should encode width. Add classes `.tt-swatch.w4 { border-top-width: 4px }`, `.w25 { 2.5px → use 3px }` and `.w15 { 1.5px → 2px }`, and pass the class from the KEY rows (`['w4', '#dfe6e8', 'Major street']`, and so on).

**P1-6 · Street labels sit on the hotel and the people pins, collide with the compass, and are too dense in DC.**
Shots: 1600-04 and 1280-04 ("Red River Street" sits on the hotel crown under the HL/AK pins), 1600-05 ("Red River Street" floats in the glass, "Purple Heart Trail" sits under the compass), 1280-04 ("Willow…" under the compass), 1280-09 (about 22 labels, with "North Capitol Street Northeast" and "Massachusetts Avenue Northeast" stacked over the hotel cluster). With `disableDepthTestDistance: ∞` a label always wins over the building it is behind.
Spec:
- Anchor rule: put the label at the midpoint of the longest run of the way that is **more than 60 m from the hotel centre** and outside the hotel footprint, not at the midpoint of the whole way.
- Screen-space cull after each camera move: project every label, accept majors first, then minors longer than 120 m. Reject any label whose box (plus 6 px padding) overlaps an accepted label, a people pin, a place marker, the compass (`#tt-compass` rect) or the toolbar. Cap at 12 labels at Neighborhood and 8 at Building.
- Abbreviate names in display text only: Street → St, Avenue → Ave, Boulevard → Blvd, Road → Rd, Northwest/Northeast/Southwest/Southeast → NW/NE/SW/SE, "North Interstate 35" → "I-35". Keep the full name in the title or aria text.
- Leave label type as is (major 600 11 px `#eff7f3`, minor 11 px `#b8c3c7` on a `#061a23` α .78 plate). Both are well above 4.5:1.

**P1-7 · The longer mode chip truncates the site name in the header.**
Shots: 1600-08 ("Park Plaza London Wat", cut with no ellipsis), 1600-09 ("Hilton Washington DC Capit"), and 1600-04 (breadcrumb shortened to "Spring choir tou…" compared with the full text in 1600-01). "MONITORING · WAYS OUT VIEW" adds about 90 px and takes it from the site name, which is how the operator knows where they are.
Spec:
- Shorten the suffix to `· WAYS OUT` (drop "VIEW").
- The site name never truncates: `flex: 0 0 auto` on the site button, `min-width: 0` plus ellipsis on the breadcrumb.
- Below 1440 px, the chip collapses the suffix to `· W/O` with `title="Ways out view"`.
- In Incident, the suffix should not take the hazard red. Render it as a separate `--tt-text-2` span so red stays with "INCIDENT".

## P2

**P2-8 · Steps and entrances cannot be told apart from their neighbours.**
Shots: 1600-04 crop (around Driskill/Davis). At this scale the 3.5 px steps with a 4 px dash read as dotted fragments, much like walkway dashes. Entrance dots (white, 5–7 px, dark outline) look like the path vertex dots and the white stem dot under people pins (London 08, Austin 04). Legend (1600-02 / 1280-02 text): the steps swatch is the same 2 px dashed line as walkways, and "Building entrance" is drawn as a dotted *line*, not a dot.
Spec:
- Steps: keep neutral, 4 px, `dashLength: 6` with a 50 % gap. Add a 10 px "stairs" glyph billboard (neutral `#e3e7e9`, dark plate) at the midpoint of flights longer than 6 m, shown only below 900 m camera range. Steps matter for wheelchairs, so they need a glyph rather than a dash variant.
- Entrances: draw a 6 × 6 px **square** (rotated 45°, as a door "notch") in `#eff7f3` α .9 with a 1.5 px `#061a23` outline, 8 × 8 px for `kind=main`. Squares do not clash with round person and vertex dots. Hide above 1500 m range (see P1-4).
- Legend: `.tt-swatch.steps { border-top: 4px dashed; width: 18px }` and `.tt-swatch.door { width: 7px; height: 7px; border: 0; background: currentColor; transform: rotate(45deg) }`.

**P2-9 · Toolbar: the pressed toggle, the selected segment and the focus ring look alike, and there is little spare room at 1280.**
Shots: 1600-04 and 1280-04. Three white-outlined boxes appear side by side: "Neighborhood" (segment on), "Layers" (focus ring left after closing the popover) and "Ways out" (pressed: `--tt-sel-bg` plus 1 px inset `--tt-text`). At 1280 × 720 the toolbar is about 635 px wide in a 670 px slot (313 → 948, compass and "Viewing from S" directly above), so there is about 35 px to spare.
Spec:
- Pressed state: keep `--tt-sel-bg` and font-weight 600, drop the inset outline, and add a leading 6 px filled square in `--tt-text` (`::before`, 6 px gap). The difference from a segment then shows in shape as well as border.
- Focus: `:focus-visible` only (not `:focus`), 2 px `--tt-focus` with a 2 px offset, so it does not stay on after a mouse click.
- Show the key on the button as `<kbd>W</kbd>` (mono 10 px `--tt-text-dim`) only at ≥ 1440 px.
- Budget rule: at < 1440 px, "Add reported floor" becomes "Report floor" (saves about 45 px). Any future toolbar item must fit in the remaining 35 px or go into Display.

**P2-10 · Walkway dashes conflict with the existing dash grammar.**
Shots: 1600-08 crop (the dashed blue alternate path next to dashed white walkways near Cosser St and the rally point), 1600-04 (the blue alternate dashes run beside the white sidewalk dashes on the hotel's street). Across the twin a dash already means *uncertain or provisional*: Alternate, Saved path (long dash), Rough range, Aging, Draft · not approved, the not-approved rally ring. Walkways and steps reuse it and are separated only by hue, which fails greyscale and colour-vision checks.
Spec:
- Walkways become **solid** thin lines (1.25 px `#b8c3c7` α .5, as in P0-1) with no dash.
- Dashes stay with paths and provisional states. Steps keep the only neutral dash, made distinct by its 4 px weight and the glyph from P2-8.
- Update the legend row to `['', '#b8c3c7', 'Walkway · pedestrian area']`.

---

What works and should stay: neutral greys only (no blue, green or red in the street layer); one label per street; the label plate and type; the Site legend note "not checked for closures or access"; the footer swapping to "Grid and elevation off"; Esc returning to the saved layers; the toolbar still fitting at 1280 × 720.
