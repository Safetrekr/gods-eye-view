# Phase 7 IA review: Streets, Walkways and the "Ways out" view

Reviewer: Information Architecture · 16 Oct 2026 · review only, no code edits.

Inputs: BACKLOG.md ("Next round: streets, walkways and a ways out view"); 06-layer-taxonomy.md (§2.1–2.2, §3.5 SIT-14, §3.7, §3.11 never-list and synonym ring, §5.1–5.3 D7, §11–13); shots `1600-01…09`, `1280-04`, `1600-text.json`; code `page.js` (mode chip 770–780, Ways out 2700–2754, legends `keys()` 2788–2832, `GROUPS` 2833–2868, keydown 4018–4153), `twinStreets.js` (`roadClass`, `STYLE`, entrances), `tripTwin.js` (`setStreets`, `setHotelStepBack`), `twin.html` (toolbar 135–197, help 339–408).

Result: **1 P0 · 5 P1 · 3 P2.** The structure is mostly right. Streets and walkways are mapped ground features, so they belong in Site, not in Paths. The legends never call an OSM way a "path". W is a free key. The problems are about what the view promises and where it says so:
- the red incident chip reads "INCIDENT · WAYS OUT VIEW";
- the OSM caveat is out of sight while the view is on;
- the preset quietly hides a hazard layer and undoes the operator's own layer changes;
- "Building entrance" drops the "mapped, unverified" wording that 06 requires.

---

## L1 · P0 · "INCIDENT · WAYS OUT VIEW" in the mode chip reads as an instruction

**Shots:** 07 (red chip `INCIDENT · WAYS OUT VIEW`), 04/05/06/08/09 (`MONITORING · WAYS OUT VIEW`).

**Problem.**
- The mode chip is the strongest status string on screen, and in an incident it is red. Read quickly or across a room on a wall display, "INCIDENT · WAYS OUT" sounds like an order to leave. 01 doctrine 1 says Trip Twin never declares an evacuation, and 06 §3.11 bans "evacuate" as a command. The chip suggests one by putting the two words side by side.
- Everything else the chip adds is about the **data**: `DATA DELAYED`, `SIMULATED DATA`, `WEATHER {time}`. "Ways out" is about the **display**, a choice of layers. Putting it in the chip mixes data state with view state. This is the same mixing K-series findings removed in phase 6.
- The view hides layers, but nothing on the canvas says which ones (06 C7: "{Layer} hidden"). The footer says only "Grid and elevation off" (04).

**Spec.**
- Remove `· WAYS OUT VIEW` from `#tt-mode`. The chip goes back to data and mode only.
- Add a view tag inside the canvas, top-left above the scene, in the same position at 1280 and 1600. It shows only while the view is on, and follows the neutral chip style (never red, never green):
  `WAYS OUT VIEW · streets and walkways from OpenStreetMap, not checked for closures · hidden: weather overlays, grid, contours · W or Esc returns`
  At 1280, truncate the hidden list first, then the caveat to `OSM · not checked for closures`. Never cut "not checked".
- Footer ground line: keep `Grid and elevation off`. No other change.
- On the incident panel or path card, the view never adds "way out" to the text.

## L2 · P1 · The name "Ways out": keep it, under rules that stop it becoming a claim

**Shots:** 02, 04, 07; `twin.html` 183, 382.

**Problem.** 06 §3.11's synonym ring lists "way out" as operator speech that **resolves to** Suggested walking paths. It is not a preferred on-screen term, and the ring's neighbours ("escape route", "exit route") are banned. Jesse's BACKLOG approves "ways out" / "paths out" and bans "escape" and "safe". The other names were checked as follows:

| Candidate | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Ways out** | **Keep** (button, key, help, view tag) | Operator speech (Jesse). It is plural and generic, so it names the operator's question, not any single line. Not on the never-list |
| Paths out | Reject | "Path" belongs to Suggested and Saved paths (computed or planned, §3.7, §11). It would suggest SafeTrekr computed the OSM ways as exits |
| Street view | Reject | Collides with Google Street View, an excluded source (06 C11, §2.2 2.4) |
| Street plan / Plan view | Reject | "Plan view" is the north-up camera (C8). "Plan your own path" is the editor verb |
| Streets & walkways | Fallback only | Neutral, but it repeats two row names and loses the intent. Use it if Jesse drops "Ways out" |
| Exit view, Evacuation view, Escape view | Never | §3.11 |

**Spec: rules for "Ways out".**
1. Use it only as the **name of the view**: toolbar button, key W, help row, Layers › Views row, view tag. Never as the label of a line, legend row, place or path card.
2. Never in the singular ("Way out") and never with a judgment word ("best ways out", "clear ways out", "ways out ranked").
3. Wherever it appears as a heading (view tag, Views row note), the OSM caveat appears with it (L1).
4. Tooltip and help use the same sentence (today they differ; see L9).
5. 06 §3.11 synonym ring: change the row to `route, escape route, evacuation route, way out, ways out, exit route, exits` → `Ways out view (streets, walkways, mapped entrances) · Paths group; Suggested walking paths`.

## L3 · P1 · The preset hides a hazard, leaves the operator's own paths off, and says "step back" when it switches layers off

**Shots:** 04 vs 03 (nearly identical), 07; code `toggleWaysOut` 2711–2753.

**Problem.**
- The code sets **every** `wx.layers` key to false, including **Lightning**, which sits under the Hazards group (`1600-text.json`: "HAZARDS … Weather overlays"). A view meant for planning how to leave should not take a hazard off the map. It also clears `wx.at`, so the weather timeline jumps back to now without a notice.
- Floor estimates are forced off. In an incident (07), the floor bands are what answer "who is above floor 9". C3 turns them on automatically for a reported floor, and the view undoes that.
- BACKLOG: "The suggested **and saved** paths stay visible on top." The code forces Suggested paths on but leaves Saved paths and Avoid-areas (`customVis`) as they were. An operator-planned saved path is the most considered path on the trip, yet it stays hidden if it was off.
- The help says "other layers **step back**", but they are switched off. Only the hotel glass actually steps back (`setHotelStepBack`).
- The tooltip lists "buildings, streets, walkways and entrances". The help adds "places and paths". These are two different promises.

**Spec: the preset, as one table (06 §2.4 style).**

| Layer | Ways out sets | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Travelers & chaperones | locked on | — |
| Floor estimates | **unchanged** | C3. Never forced off during an incident |
| Reported incidents | locked on | — |
| Satellite heat detections | **on** | A way out is read against hazards (C5) |
| Weather › Lightning | **unchanged** | Hazard. A view never hides a hazard |
| Weather › Rain & snow map, 3D rain/snow, 3D clouds, totals | off | Clutter over the street grid |
| Safety resources | on | Destinations (rally points) |
| Suggested paths | on | — |
| Saved paths | **on** | BACKLOG |
| Avoid-areas | **on** | Every path is checked against them |
| Hotel | locked, glass steps back | — |
| Surrounding buildings, Streets, Walkways steps & entrances | on | — |
| Scale grid, Elevation contours | off | — |
| Weather timeline position | **unchanged** (if away from now, the view tag adds `· weather at {time}`) | No silent jump back to now |

Rule for 06: **a view preset may hide context and decoration, never a Hazards layer.** Help wording: "… stand out; grid, contours and weather maps are hidden". Do not write "step back" for layers that are switched off.

## L4 · P1 · Ways out and Layers state: silent revert, no trace in the Layers panel

**Shots:** 02 (Layers panel), 03 vs 04; code 2703–2753, 4062.

**Problem.**
- While the view is on, the Layers panel shows the preset's checkboxes with no sign that a preset set them. If the operator then turns on Floor estimates or Elevation contours, W or Esc **restores the saved snapshot and undoes that change without saying so**. If they turn Streets off, the chip still says "WAYS OUT VIEW" while streets are off.
- The view is really a **Layers preset**, but it cannot be found from the Layers panel, the place an operator looks for "which layers are on".
- If Streets and Walkways are already on (03), pressing W changes almost nothing visible (04), so the toggle can look broken. The L1 view tag is the feedback.
- On the toolbar it sits after Display, between two popover triggers with carets, and looks like a third menu.

**Spec.**
- Toolbar: keep the one-click button (BACKLOG). Order: `Building · Neighborhood · City | Layers ▾ · Ways out · Display ▾ | Add reported floor`. The preset sits next to the panel it controls. On: pressed style plus a check glyph, `aria-pressed="true"`. Tooltip: `Ways out (W) · streets, walkways, mapped entrances, places and paths stand out · W or Esc returns to your layers`.
- Layers panel: a new block **above** People, kicker `VIEWS`, one row: `[ ] Ways out  W`. While it is on, show a note under it: `Showing the Ways out layers · W or Esc returns to your layers`. Every row the preset forced gets no extra mark. The note is enough.
- **Manual change rule:** if the operator changes any layer row while the view is on, the view ends, **the layers stay as they are now**, and a toast reads `Ways out ended · your layer change is kept · Back to my layers`. `Back to my layers` restores the snapshot taken before the view started. Esc and W never undo a change the operator made by hand.
- Persistence: the view does not survive a reload, a site switch (S) or Exit Trip Twin. On a site switch, end it the same way as the manual change rule.
- Esc order (one stack, in the help): close panel → weather back to now → **end Ways out** → path → place/person › floor card › site → clear selection. Today's code already does this. Only the help is missing it (L9).

## L5 · P1 · "Building entrance" drops the "mapped, unverified" wording, and 06 files entrances under Paths

**Shots:** 03, 04, 05 (white dots on building edges); `keys().walkways`; `twinStreets.js` 167–182.

**Problem.**
- 06 §3.11 replaces "fire exit, emergency exit, stairwell" with **"Mapped entrance (OSM, unverified)"**, and SIT-14 forbids "Exit, front door". The legend says `Building entrance`. That suggests all of the building's entrances are shown and that each one is real and usable. OSM has some entrances, unchecked.
- Main entrances are drawn larger (7 px vs 5 px) with no legend row, so the size difference carries meaning without being explained.
- 06 §2.2 row 4.4 files **Mapped entrances** under **Paths**. The build puts them under **Site** (in "Walkways, steps & entrances"). The build is right (see L7), but 06 must change too, or the two will disagree.

**Spec.**
- Legend rows: `● Mapped entrance (OSM, unverified)` · `● larger: mapped as main entrance`.
- No hover or label on an entrance ever says exit, door, way out or fire exit.
- 06 §2.2: delete row 4.4 from Paths. Add Site 5.x `Walkways, steps & entrances`, described as "OSM footways, pedestrian areas, trails, cycleways, steps and mapped entrance nodes · P1 · Off · on in Ways out".

## L6 · P1 · Walkways vs paths: the words are kept apart, but the drawing and one help line blur them

**Shots:** 04, 08 (London: white walkway dashes and blue saved/alternate dashes intertwine near Westminster Bridge Road and Kennington Road); 02 (legends).

**Problem.**
- **Words: mostly good.** The legend says "Walkway", never "path". But `roadClass` puts OSM `highway=path` and `highway=cycleway` into "walk". The legend does not say so, and the next person to name these in a hover or count could easily write "path".
- **Line style clash.** In the Paths group, a dash means "Alternate" or "Saved path". In People and Safety it means approximate or unconfirmed: "Rough range" (floor), "Aging", "Draft · not approved". Walkways are now **dashed `#eff7f3`**, the same colour and pattern as Floor estimates' "Rough range". Seen from above in London (08), operators cannot tell a white walkway dash from a light-blue saved-path dash without the legend.
- **Help line:** "buildings, streets, walkways, entrances, places and paths stand out". "Paths" here means Suggested and Saved paths, right after "walkways". A new operator could read "walkways … paths" as one list of OSM features.

**Spec.**
- **Vocabulary rule (06 §3.7 addendum):** on screen, "path" means only a Suggested path or a Saved path. OSM `path`, `footway` and `cycleway` are always called **walkway** (or "trail" in the note). Never "footpath", "path", "pedestrian path" or "walking path" for an OSM way.
- Legend: `Walkway · pedestrian area` plus note `includes trails and cycleways as mapped in OpenStreetMap`.
- **Line grammar (06 §5.1 addendum, for UI to apply):** dashes are for paths and for uncertainty. Mapped ground features (streets, walkways) are drawn **solid**, set apart by width and brightness. Steps get their own mark (short ticks across the line), not a dash pattern. If UI keeps a dash for walkways, it must differ from every dash in the Paths legend by pattern **and** colour, and the London shot is the test case.
- Help line: "Ways out: buildings, streets, walkways, mapped entrances, places and **suggested and saved paths** stand out; grid, contours and weather maps are hidden · W or Esc returns".

## L7 · P2 · Grouping: Streets and Walkways under Site is right; update 06 to match

**Shots:** 02.

**Problem / endorsement.**
- 06 §2.1 defines Paths as "computed, unverified street paths", things SafeTrekr computes or an operator plans. Site is "orientation; the hologram and fixed places". Streets and walkways are mapped, fixed and uncomputed, so they are **Site**. Putting them in Paths would collapse the very distinction L6 protects.
- With 6 rows (Hotel, Surrounding buildings, Streets, Walkways steps & entrances, Scale grid, Elevation contours), Site is inside 7±2. The order runs from the building outwards to measuring aids, and that order is right.
- 06 is now stale: §2.2 lists no Streets or Walkways row, keeps 4.4 Mapped entrances under Paths, and has 5.2's note "Off = streets visible on the grid floor". That note is now wrong, because the base street lines switch off when the Streets layer is on (`setBaseStreets(!streets)`).

## L8 · P2 · Legend wording: the Streets caveat, "Service road", "access", and inline style notes

**Shots:** 02 (Streets legend); `keys()` 2820–2831.

**Problem.**
- The Streets note is `Street names from OpenStreetMap`. It carries no closure caveat, but in Ways out the streets are what the operator reads as ways out. The walkways note has a caveat; the streets note does not.
- "Service road" means nothing in particular to an operator. In OSM it covers alleys, driveways and parking aisles, and alleys are exactly what matters on foot.
- "not checked for closures or **access**": "access" is vague (wheelchair access? legal access?). OSM `access=private` or locked gates is what is meant.
- `Steps (short heavy dash)` and `Saved path (long dash)` describe their line style in brackets. No other legend row does, and the swatch already shows the style.

**Spec.**

| Row | Today | Change to |
|---|---|---|
| Streets swatch 3 | Service road | `Alley or service road` |
| Streets note | Street names from OpenStreetMap | `OpenStreetMap · names and classes as mapped · not checked for closures` |
| Walkways swatch 1 | Walkway · pedestrian area | `Walkway · pedestrian area` + note `includes trails and cycleways` (L6) |
| Walkways swatch 2 | Steps (short heavy dash) | `Steps` (the swatch shows the mark) |
| Walkways swatch 3 | Building entrance | `Mapped entrance (OSM, unverified)` · `larger: main entrance` (L5) |
| Walkways note | OpenStreetMap · not checked for closures or access | `OpenStreetMap · not checked for closures, locked gates or private access` |

Steps note (optional, since BACKLOG says steps matter for wheelchairs): `Steps: not step-free`. Never "accessible route" or "wheelchair route".

## L9 · P2 · Help overlay and key: W is fine; the Esc row and tooltip lag behind

**Shots:** `twin.html` 380–395, 183; keydown 4019, 4062, 4138.

**Findings.**
- **W: no conflict.** It is free in the main keymap and is not used by the path editor (`handleKey`: Esc, X, Enter, Backspace/Delete). Cmd/Ctrl/Alt+W fall through to the browser (`if (e.metaKey || e.ctrlKey || e.altKey) return`), so Ctrl+W still closes the tab as expected. The compass shows a "W" for west, and N means North up. But S (Sites) and E (Elevation) already break the compass reading, so W follows existing practice. **Keep W.** It is a mnemonic for both "Ways out" and "Walkways".
- **Esc row** still reads "Close panel, then the path, then step back…". It leaves out the two steps the code now takes first: weather back to now (phase 6) and ending Ways out.
- **Tooltip vs help** list different contents (L3).

**Spec.**
- Help, W row: `Ways out: buildings, streets, walkways, mapped entrances, places and suggested and saved paths stand out; grid, contours and weather maps are hidden · W or Esc returns to your layers`.
- Help, Esc row: `Close panel, then weather back to now, then end Ways out, then the path, then step back (place or person › floor card › site), then clear the selection (never exits)`.
- Place the W row directly under the L row (Layers), since it is a Layers preset. Today it sits between D and G.

## Keep

- Streets and Walkways in **Site**, between Surrounding buildings and Scale grid. The reasoning is right and the order reads outwards.
- The legends never call an OSM way a "path". The OSM caveat sits on the Walkways legend, and the streets are neutral grey (blue stays for paths, green for approved places).
- The Walkways legend note format (`OpenStreetMap · not checked for …`) matches PTH-03.
- Streets replace the faint base street lines while on (`setBaseStreets`), so the ground is never drawn twice.
- A one-click toggle plus a key, with `aria-pressed` and Esc to return, as the BACKLOG asked.
- The modifier guard on the keydown handler: Cmd/Ctrl+W are never swallowed.
- The hotel glass steps back in the view, so streets behind the tower read (05).
- The footer ground line `Grid and elevation off` states what the view turned off.
- One label per street, major and minor only, at the middle of the longest way, with no labels on service roads or walkways. It stays legible in London (08).
- The help overlay has a W entry with its return keys.
