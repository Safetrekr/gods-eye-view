# Phase 11 review 01: operator UX (after-shots)

Lens: can the operator at a desk and the supervisor 3 m from the wall answer the five questions faster: where are my people, what is the threat and where, where is safety, how do they get there, how fresh is this? Review is from stills only. Motion tiers cannot be judged from stills except where a state label shows them ("drifting" on S1).

## Verdict
The pass moved the wall in the right direction on S3 (the floors ladder is gone; "changed in the last 10 min" and "next on the plan" are new), S2 (neighbourhood plan view, headcounts are now the largest numerals) and S6 (a board that fills the wall). It left W1, W4 and W5 mostly unresolved and made the S1 people picture weaker. The larger type also broke two things that matter in an incident: at 2560 the incident board now pushes the Official slot and the main path below the fold, and S1 has replaced a person count with the word "Lobby". On the remote the structure is unchanged: the empty rail well, the gap between target and scope, and controls that still look live in view-only mode all remain, so the target → station → action → confirm flow is no clearer on an iPad than before.
