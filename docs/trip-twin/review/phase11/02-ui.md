# Phase 11 review: UI (visual craft)
Reviewer: factory-ui-designer, 16 Oct 2026. I reviewed the screenshots in `shots/` against `before/` and the "Instrument" brand decision. I did not open any code. I estimated contrast from sampled pixels. All sizes are in CSS px at 2560×1440 unless the row says otherwise.

## Verdict
The Instrument skin is in place. The field is teal-black #040c12, and steel text reads at 5.6:1 (#80959b) to 10.9:1 (#b8c3c7). The Manrope 200 headcounts (about 62 px on S2 and S3), the S6 clocks and the remote's Manrope headings all carry well at 3 m.

What still keeps this below investor grade is unfinished layout, not colour:
- rosters clipped through the middle of a line of text
- the compass caption cut off by the timeline
- two headers printed over each other on the remote preview
- large empty glass wells on S3, S4, S5, S6 and the remote
- a floors ladder that still wears the old flat, bordered skin

Second-order problems:
- Map labels and pins are below wall size.
- Panel headings are split between Inter and Manrope.
- Selection has three different treatments.
- The dot grid can't be seen at 3 m.

## What holds
- Field #040c12, panel glass #07131b with a 1 px top highlight (#131f28), and panel borders around 18 % alpha (#2e3a41), all as specified.
- Contrast is fine everywhere I measured except the items in rows 23 and 25. Secondary steel (#80959b on glass) is 5.6–5.9:1, primary steel is 10.4–10.9:1, and green map labels are 6.1:1.
- Manrope numerals on S2 and S3 (about 62 px) and on S6 (clocks about 130 px, titles about 56 px) are now the largest things on those walls. The header kicker is 30 px mono, and the source line is 11–12 px dim, both on target.
- Meaning colours stay in their lanes on the walls: amber for attention numerals and bars, green for the approved chip, blue for paths, red for the S6 incident tile.
