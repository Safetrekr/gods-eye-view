# Phase 11 · Brand fidelity review · Trip Twin "Instrument"
Reviewed 16 Oct 2026 against the approved direction (B · Instrument), from `shots/` and `before/`, `tripTwin.instrument.css`, `remote.instrument.css`, the scene colour tables in `twinScene.js`, `twinPins.js` and `twinContext.js`, and the app's dark theme (`safetrekr-app-v2/src/app/globals.css` from line 96). Pixel values were sampled from the PNGs.

## Verdict
The walls, the All-in-one panels and the remote now read as Instrument: the field samples at #040c12, panels at rgba(8,22,30), the headcounts are Manrope 200, kickers are Intel One Mono, LIVE and the selection are the one light, and S2, S3 and S6 are much better than before. The direction breaks at the edges: the entry screen opens over the host World View globe (olive-grey, not teal-black), the dot grid is missing from the empty walls it was chosen for, the old navy and teal still sit in the people pins and the ground grid, the "light" exists as three different hex values, and Inter goes above 28 px on S3 and S6. A red button with no incident open breaks the rule that matters most, and "SAFETREKR" is retyped in the mono face instead of placed as the logo, so the brand link to the app comes only from the favicon.

What holds and should not be touched:
- The wall header grammar: mono 30 px kicker, Inter 22 px place line, and the LIVE badge with its breathing dot in #eef5f3.
- Manrope 200 headcounts at 56–68 px rendered.
- S6 Manrope clocks.
- Light-filled primary buttons ("Start multi-view", "Show Day 1 on S2").
- On/Off segments where On is the light.
- The white focus border on S6.
- Green kept to approved places, blue to paths, amber to the floors ladder and not-current counts.
- The favicon, which is byte-identical to the app's (white shield on #123646).

Family resemblance to the app's dark theme holds through Inter, Intel One Mono, the slate-teal ramp and the favicon. Three things differ on purpose and the brand page should state them: the deeper field (#040c12 against the app's #061a23), a 6 px radius against the app's 10 px, and a white primary against the app's green `--primary` #4ba467. In the twin, green is a meaning and cannot be a button colour.

## Rules for the brand page
1. The field is #040c12 on every surface (walls, All-in-one scene, entry backdrop, remote, snapshots), and nothing is shown before the field is set.
2. Chrome is steel only: #b8c3c7 for text and hairlines, #80959b for secondary lines and the sweep, #415e6b for the faintest grid and wireframe, and no other teal or navy hex appears in CSS or scene code.
3. Hairlines sit between 12 % and 22 % alpha, and a stronger edge means the element is on, chosen or focused.
4. The light is one token, #eef5f3, used only for what is on or chosen: selection, the LIVE badge, the live dot, primary buttons and the hotel outline.
5. Green means approved places, blue means paths, red means incidents and amber means attention; none of them is ever used for chrome, and a control turns red only while an incident exists.
6. There is one glass recipe: rgba(8,22,30,.72), 16 px blur, a 1 px top light at rgba(203,228,238,.06), and a 6 px radius (8 px for cards).
7. The 44 px dot grid at 20 % alpha appears on the empty field of every wall and never on the All-in-one or the remote.
8. Manrope is for numerals (200) and headings (300), Inter is for body text, and Intel One Mono is only for kickers, chips, ages and sources; on a wall, measured after the wall scale, Manrope is never below 26 px and Inter never above 28 px.
9. The SafeTrekr mark is always placed from the supplied asset and never retyped in any face, with "Trip Twin" set beside it in Manrope 300.
10. Every colour, including scene colours in JS, comes from the instrument token table with its role named, and each phase ends with a sampled-screenshot check of field, light, grid and type sizes on all four surfaces.
