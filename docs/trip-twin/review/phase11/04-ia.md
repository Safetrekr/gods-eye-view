# Phase 11 IA review: labels, structure and scope (after-shots)
Reviewer lens: information architecture and labelling. Read from `shots/` against `before/`, `00-AUDIT.md`, `PHASE-11-PLAN.md` and `06-layer-taxonomy.md` §5, §3.11, §17, §18. No code opened.

## Verdict
The pass fixed the floors-ladder duplication on S3. The six wall roles now read the same on the walls, the launch card and the remote previews (Site · People · Incident & attention · Weather & alerts · Day plan · Trips on watch), and every remote section seen names its scope (`· S1`, `· S2`, `· EVERY SCREEN`). The IA is not yet ready for a first-time supervisor. S3 now repeats S2's roster word for word. The "no incident" state has two names and no trip scope, while S6 shows a reported floor on another trip. An NWS advisory that ended at 19:00 still reads as current at 21:33. Many numbers have no source or age, and one line uses the banned word "all-clear". Motion should stay under 06, but as a DISPLAY group at the bottom of the station. Both surfaces should call it Display › Motion and offer the same four switches.
