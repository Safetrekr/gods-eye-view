# Phase 11 review · Motion (world-class motion designer)

Read: `05-motion-language.md` (all), `PHASE-11-PLAN.md` P11-A, `twinMotion.js`, `tripTwin.instrument.css` (motion tiers + the uncommitted sweep at 583–619), `remote.instrument.css`, `remote.css`, `remote.js`, `stations.js`, `page.js` (motion controller, feed, drift, frame), `twinScreen.js` (badge, drift gate, camera helper), `twinHud.js` (roster highlight), podium `serras-portal/index.html` 176–240 and 700–845.

## Verdict

The tier controller is clean: reduce wins, it listens to the system setting live, every curve stays inside [0, 1], and the podium fog curves, press curve and 0.52 s well timing are copied exactly. The work around it breaks the spec's hard rules in four places. The incident banner ends up off-centre on every wall. The drift keeps orbiting the old hotel after a site switch. The live dot breathes forever, even on a stale wall. The sweep is a full-screen scanline driven by `bottom`. Half of what the Event and Cinematic switches promise (echo at the old fix, selection reticle, static deltas, identify pulse) does not exist. The podium register is carried at the level of curves, not of choreography. Podium plays its wavefront when the rail opens and swaps stages in-only in 220 ms. Trip Twin replays the wavefront under the thumb on every station change and adds a 230 ms fog-out before new content, so information waits for motion.

## Signature moment

For an investor demo, the one motion I would add is the cross-wall incident cascade from spec §3.4, run in step on every wall. It does CAUSE and ATTEND: the room shows where the report came from and who it concerns, in that order, as one instrument. The remote's "Add reported floor" sends a command with the server time T0. Each wall schedules against T0, not against when the message arrived, so all walls play the same frame.

- **S1 at T0:** the floor-9 slab fades in over 200 ms, `cubic-bezier(0, 0, 0.2, 1)`. Its outline settles from 1.8× to 1.0× brightness over 600 ms.
- **S1 at T0 + 680 → 1040 ms:** the at-or-above bracket grows upward from the slab's top edge to the highest band, 360 ms, `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`.
- **S3 at T0:** "At or above reported floor 9: 3" appears complete in 200 ms.
- **S2 at T0 + 1040 ms:** the three members' rows get the 2400 ms wall highlight.
- **Room-wide at T0 + 1040 ms:** ripples start, 3 cycles × 1200 ms, scale 1.0 → 1.35, α 0.9 → 0, at most 3 across the room: the S1 bracket, S2's priority person and S3's header.
- **Remote:** the confirm bar reads "Applied · S1 S2 S3".

In reduced motion, every wall shows the end state in the frame after T0, with the static double ring. Nothing in it overshoots, nothing moves the camera except a floor-9 framing (480 ms, `bezier(0.4, 0, 0.1, 1)`) when floor 9 is off screen, and nothing repeats after the ripples end.
